# The Guide Who Becomes A Trap

**Aliases:** guide becomes trap, Virgil problem, trusted guide trap, necessary guide.

> **Fast answer:** The guide who becomes a trap names Jiang's Dante mechanism: a guide can be necessary for entering a world, but the same trusted authority can carry the false world that must later be defeated. Virgil helps Dante enter hell; he also becomes part of what Dante must outgrow.

## What Jiang Means

Jiang's guide problem is not a generic warning against teachers. It is about mediation. A guide gives language, courage, map, and access. But if the guide's authority becomes final, the student remains inside the guide's world.

The concept matters for education, AI, media, and political interpretation. Any guide can become a control surface when the person stops using it to see and starts accepting it as the replacement for seeing.

## How To Use This Term

Use this term when Jiang is analyzing necessary mediation that later becomes captivity: teacher, poet, guide, AI companion, institution, or inherited worldview.

Do not use it to dismiss guidance itself. The trap begins when the guide becomes final.
