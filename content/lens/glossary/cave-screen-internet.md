# Cave Screen Internet

**Aliases:** Plato's cave internet, screen cave, internet cave wall, ChatGPT cave wall.

> **Fast answer:** Cave screen internet is the search-facing version of Jiang's modern Plato's cave. Movies, the internet, social media, school, AI, and ChatGPT can become the wall where shadows look like reality because attention has been captured long enough to make the mediated world feel final.

## What Jiang Means

The internet matters here as ontology, not merely media. A screen is dangerous when it becomes the world a person trusts most, the surface where imagination is spent, and the place where attention is organized before the person can ask what reality is.

Jiang's cave is not anti-technology in a simple sense. The point is mediation becoming captivity. A tool becomes a wall when it trains the prisoner to mistake the wall for all reality.
