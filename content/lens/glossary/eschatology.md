# Eschatology

**Aliases:** end times, apocalypse, eschatological script, prophecy as plan, sacred script, end of history.

> **Fast answer:** Jiang uses eschatology to mean more than belief about the end times. It is a story-form that organizes the present by giving people origins, roles, direction, enemies, rewards, and a felt ending. His compact formulation is that "eschatology drives geopolitics." Start with the 2025-08-01 lecture [When Eschatologies Converge](/episodes/predictive-history-yq-xg1nibms/), especially `video:predictive-history-yq-xg1nibms@transcript:v1#seg-0003`, `#seg-0004`, and `#seg-0006`.

## What Jiang Means

In Jiang's vocabulary, eschatology is a script that converts metaphysics into action. It answers [where people come from, what they are doing here, and where history is going]{evidence="video:predictive-history-yq-xg1nibms@transcript:v1#seg-0003 video:predictive-history-yq-xg1nibms@transcript:v1#seg-0004"}. That matters politically because people do not only hold these scripts privately. They inhabit roles inside them.

The caveat is part of the term. Jiang does not say every religious believer is apocalyptic or that religion mechanically causes war. In the 2025-08-01 source, he says eschatological politics often comes from [minority or extreme formations inside a religion]{evidence="video:predictive-history-yq-xg1nibms@transcript:v1#seg-0006"}. The actors who matter are the organized ones who treat prophecy as a live plan.

The Christian Zionism lecture gives the sharpest grammar: promise becomes plan. Historic premillennialism waits for Jesus to return; dispensational premillennialism [treats prophecy as something believers must help achieve]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lkkrzq4ydqy@transcript:v1#seg-0014"}. The most organized and fanatical actors then matter disproportionately because they act as if the sacred script must be fulfilled through policy, war, settlement, alliance, or pressure.

The older Cyrus lecture supplies the template: [dark and light, truth and lie, free will, final battle, judgment, paradise, and hell]{evidence="video:predictive-history-hvvtntpzq7e@transcript:v1#seg-0033 video:predictive-history-hvvtntpzq7e@transcript:v1#seg-0034"}. That source is useful because it shows that, for Jiang, eschatology is not only a Christian or modern phenomenon. It is a durable narrative architecture that can migrate across religions, empires, and geopolitical projects.

## How To Use This Term

Use this term when Jiang is analyzing end-times scripts as engines of geopolitics, war, prediction, statecraft, or collective coordination.

Do not use it for any religious future-belief in isolation, generic collapse mood, or "apocalypse" without structured roles and action logic.
