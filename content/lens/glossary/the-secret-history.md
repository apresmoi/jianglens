# The Secret History

**Aliases:** Secret History series, hidden history, secret history of power, Jiang Secret History.

> **Fast answer:** The Secret History is Jiang's named arc for hidden mechanisms of power: alchemy, taboo, evil, capital, bureaucracy, sacred order, secret societies, and the spiritual stakes beneath ordinary political history. It is not a license to invent hidden causes; each use still needs exact source refs.

## What Jiang Means

In the corpus, Secret History names both a series and a mode of inquiry. It asks what public history leaves out: the symbolic conversions, sacred scripts, taboo boundaries, underground networks, institutional inversions, and metaphysical assumptions that make visible events intelligible.

The boundary matters. A Secret History entry should not collapse into generic conspiracy. Jiang's usable material names a mechanism and dates it to a source.
