# Education As A Soul Game

**Aliases:** soul game, school as a game, education and the soul, credential game.

> **Fast answer:** Education as a soul game is Jiang's lens for school as a battle over what the student becomes. The visible game is grades, college, status, and compliance. The deeper game is whether education awakens agency, imagination, and love or trains the student to want the institution's rewards.

## What Jiang Means

The term holds together two sides of Jiang's education work. The negative side says modern schooling turns learning into a managed game. Students learn to optimize visible rewards, compare themselves, please authorities, and fear the loss of institutional status.

The positive side says a real school should form future makers. Great Books, historical context, debate, hard questions, and model correction are not enrichment activities. They are ways to rescue education from credential management and return it to soul formation.

## How To Use This Term

Use this term when the educational issue is not curriculum alone but the student's formed desires, attention, courage, and agency.

Do not use it for every school anecdote or generic anti-school complaint.
