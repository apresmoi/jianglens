# How Poetry Creates Civilization

**Aliases:** poetry creates civilization, poets create language, Homer creates Greece, poetry as civilizational memory.

> **Fast answer:** This term names Jiang's claim that poetry does not merely decorate civilization. Poetry creates language, memory, heroes, shared perception, and interior worlds. Homer and Dante matter because their poetic universes enter people and help make civilizations think, feel, and act.

## What Jiang Means

Poetry creates civilization by making a world memorable and inhabitable. Rhythm, compression, character, image, and repetition let a poem live inside speech, education, theater, memory, and the self.

The mechanism is stronger than influence. Jiang treats poetic form as a way for another universe to enter the reader. When enough readers carry that universe, poetry becomes civilizational infrastructure.
