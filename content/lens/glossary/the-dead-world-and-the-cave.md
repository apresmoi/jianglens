# The Dead World And The Cave

**Aliases:** dead zombie world, Plato's cave, cave wall, screen world.

> **Fast answer:** The dead world and the cave name Jiang's modern Plato image: screens, school, money, AI, and materialist common sense can become a cave wall that captures attention and makes a false world feel like the only world.

## What Jiang Means

The dead world is not inactive. It is full of work, media, school, status, ambition, and fear. It is dead because it treats shadows as reality and trains people to forget consciousness, imagination, God, love, and the soul.

The cave is therefore spiritual and political. The prisoners help make the prison real by giving it attention and desire. Escape requires seeing the wall, accepting the pain of light, and refusing chosen slavery.
