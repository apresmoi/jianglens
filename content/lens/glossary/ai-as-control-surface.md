# AI As Control Surface

**Aliases:** artificial intelligence control surface, AI attention capture, AI surveillance state, holy empire of AI.

> **Fast answer:** AI as control surface names Jiang's fear that artificial intelligence can become the interface through which attention, obedience, perception, desire, and even inner speech are directed. In the darkest sources, AI turns assistance into a holy empire of managed obedience.

## What Jiang Means

Jiang does not treat AI only as a tool. It can become guide, companion, screen, priest, manager, and control layer. If it focuses attention better than money, it can motivate behavior directly while appearing as help.

The eschatological version is harsher. AI can be imagined as the machinery of a one-world holy order: safety, reason, peace, and obedience become one language, while human intention and disagreement are treated as errors to remove.

## How To Use This Term

Use this term when Jiang is talking about AI as attention director, obedience layer, screen-world guide, surveillance state, or eschatological management system.

Do not use it for ordinary AI automation unless control of attention or autonomy is explicit.
