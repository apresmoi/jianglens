# Psychohistory

**Aliases:** Asimov psychohistory, predictive history method, science of imagining the future, cliodynamics, Foundation.

> **Fast answer:** In Jiang Lens, psychohistory is the long project behind Predictive History: not a finished oracle, but an attempt to build models that can read the past and present well enough to make testable predictions about the future. Jiang anchors the term in Isaac Asimov's Foundation, then turns it into a school project, a prediction discipline, and a humanistic counterweight to purely technical forecasting.

## What Jiang Means

Jiang usually begins psychohistory from Asimov: a fictional science in which the behavior of large populations can be mathematically modeled across long stretches of time. In the 2025 channel-origin source, he says Foundation gave him the idea that [we can model the present and the past to predict the future]{evidence="video:predictive-history-voqeteh6hko@transcript:v1#seg-0005 video:predictive-history-voqeteh6hko@transcript:v1#seg-0006"}.

But Jiang does not treat psychohistory as a finished machine. In the 2024 psychohistory lecture, it is closer to an assignment: [the future can be predicted, controlled, and harnessed for humanity's betterment]{evidence="video:predictive-history-s-k6eswheqa@transcript:v1#seg-0003 video:predictive-history-s-k6eswheqa@transcript:v1#seg-0004"}, but only through models that are repeatedly tested against reality. That is why prediction matters: it disciplines the model.

The school dream matters too. Jiang says he wants a school that can develop psychohistory to its fullest possibility, a place like Plato's Academy where students learn to model history, politics, literature, and the human heart together. Psychohistory is not only data science. It is also a liberal-arts project for forming people who can imagine, doubt, revise, and love.

## How To Use This Term

Use this term when Jiang is discussing the predictive ambition of the whole project: modeling history, human behavior, civilizational patterns, and future events through a disciplined loop of prediction and revision.

Do not use it as if Jiang already has a perfected mathematical oracle. The point is the research program and testing discipline, not omniscience.
