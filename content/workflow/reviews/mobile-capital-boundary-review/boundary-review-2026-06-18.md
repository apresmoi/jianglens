## Decision

Do not create a standalone `Mobile Capital`, `Money As God`, or `Trauma Programming As Obedience Engine` public page now.

Keep the source pressure routed across existing homes:

- **Attention Capture** owns capital's target: captured attention, managed agency, anxiety, destroyed security, and the fact that mobile capital leaves once a population can no longer be profitably focused.
- **Secret Society** owns mobile capital's hidden coordination layer: trust across places, ritual displacement of responsibility, hierarchy, initiation, and dependent operators.
- **Power As Alchemy** owns the conversion layer: money becoming God-like reality, banking converting public guarantee into private power, and symbols becoming lived command.
- **Debt As Sovereignty** owns never-ending obligation and repayment machinery, including the public-debt guarantee and household anxiety only when debt itself is the active mechanism.
- **Dollar Hegemony** owns Bretton Woods, the gold break, petrodollar demand, reserve hierarchy, and later dollar-system coercion.
- **Platform Infrastructure** remains a held/proposal surface for trusted private fronts, technology delivery, and public-private control surfaces; this source does not require public platform prose by itself.
- **Prediction** owns the closing World War III warning only as dated forecast-family pressure, not as a confirmed ledger item.

Hold `trauma-programming-as-obedience-engine` as a sensitive seed. The source supplies strong internal examples, but a public child would currently be too easy to flatten into lurid topic clustering. Reopen it only after more dated sources show a reusable mechanism that can be written with precise boundaries across Secret Society, Education, Human Heart, and Dead World without turning one source's harsh examples into standalone canon.

## Boundary Reasoning

The Capital of Evil source is a hinge, not one new concept.

The opening capital definition belongs to Attention because Jiang defines capital as a mechanism for extracting energy, attention, and focus, then treats freedom, equity, money, debt, and anxiety as better ways to make people direct themselves toward work (`video:predictive-history-b5fthagng8c@transcript:v1#seg-0001`, `#seg-0002`, `#seg-0004`, `#seg-0008`). Attention already has public anchors for that mechanism, so another append would duplicate rather than clarify.

The mobile-capital turn belongs partly to Attention and partly to Secret Society. Attention owns the landlord learning to leave when a population has been exhausted (`video:predictive-history-b5fthagng8c@transcript:v1#seg-0008`, `#seg-0009`). Secret Society owns the trust problem created by that movement: mobile capital needs hidden coordination, shared worldview, and operators who can displace agency onto ritual authority (`video:predictive-history-b5fthagng8c@transcript:v1#seg-0010`, `#seg-0015`, `#seg-0016`).

The money-as-God language is real Power pressure, but it does not make a new page. Power already owns the alchemical grammar where money becomes proof, symbol, God, and lived reality; Attention owns the way the same money captures focus; Debt and Dollar own narrower machinery (`video:predictive-history-b5fthagng8c@transcript:v1#seg-0004`, `#seg-0044`, `#seg-0055`, `#seg-0064`).

The trauma-programming material should stay held. The lecture ties destroyed reality, dissociation, dependency, initiation, sex, drugs, handlers, Spartan education, gangs, child armies, Assassins, and MKUltra into one obedience model (`video:predictive-history-b5fthagng8c@transcript:v1#seg-0022`, `#seg-0026`, `#seg-0029`, `#seg-0032`). That is a plausible reusable mechanism, but current evidence is still one-source dense and sensitive. The public map is better served by routing exact future uses through Secret Society, Education, Human Heart, or Dead World by active mechanism.

## Reader / World-Model Judge

Finding: no public mutation is needed now.

A public append would make already-dense pages repeat their own anchors. A new mobile-capital child would blur Attention's extraction target with Secret Society's trust machinery and Power's money-conversion layer. A money-as-God page would duplicate Power's existing Protestant money section and Attention's capital section. A trauma-programming page would be premature and could make the lens look like a topic index of shocking examples rather than a carefully bounded mechanism.

The clearer reader map is to keep three diagnostic questions separate:

- What living force is being captured? Attention.
- What hidden trust or ritual technology lets mobile extractors coordinate? Secret Society.
- What symbol or guarantee becomes real enough to command people, states, banks, and empires? Power, Debt, or Dollar Hegemony depending on the machinery.

Residual risk: Attention is not at hard split pressure, but it has enough source fan-in that future additions should be replacement/compression or a fresh child review, not ordinary appended examples.
