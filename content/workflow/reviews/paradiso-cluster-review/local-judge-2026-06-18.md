The June 15 source already says imagination leads to truth, contradiction heightens imagination, ambiguity and economy multiply meaning, and logic can become chains. The June 16 source reinforces that method through collapsed time, multiple explanations, and the claim that great scientists were first poets. This strengthens `poetry-soul-gateway-breaks-familiar-prison`; it does not require a new Science As Poetry page.

June 15 already extended the Dante chronology with God as welcome rather than cosmic police, Piccarda's lower sphere as fear-shaped desire, and forgiveness as restored agency. June 16 tests the same mechanism under more difficult pressure: receptivity, vows, coercion, relational duty, and the limits of formulaic obedience. This is future replacement material for Free Will only if the page is being compressed, not a fresh append target.

Keep **Dead World** as the possible home for the anti-materialist and escape-practice layer. June 15 gives Great Books escape-practice pressure against school logic and test-taking. June 16 adds memory-as-access, brain-as-filter, cloud-memory, psychedelic/conscious-universe speculation, and eternity as death while imagination remains infinite. Use this only if the public edit keeps the active mechanism on dead material ontology versus living consciousness. Route Dante's language method back to Poetry and agency back to Free Will.

Keep **Human Heart** as the possible home for promise and forgiveness as social repair. June 16 gives the strongest next public pressure: human worlds require both future-binding promises and forgiveness when promises break. That belongs near `heart-forgiveness-requires-fallibility` when the active mechanism is fallible relation and social repair. Free Will owns whether a vow can be chosen under coercion; Love Recognizes owns concrete recognition of the beloved; Legitimacy Fiction owns public rule or title forms only when authorization is the active mechanism.
