## Question

The two most recent compact impact intakes both press near Bureaucracy:

- `predictive-history-cjtunkcu1ia` / **2025-04-01**, "The Structure of Scientific Revolutions", where scientific doubt becomes a productive bureaucracy and later hardens into specialization, gatekeeping, and blocked founding genius.
- `predictive-history-1dwvgtc7xzy` / **2025-02-27**, "The Useful Fiction of the Holy Roman Empire", where the Catholic Church acts as a "freelance imperial bureaucracy" that creates legitimacy, local information, and meta-reality across a politically fragmented Europe.

The question is whether this pressure justifies a public Bureaucracy append, a new `Freelance Imperial Bureaucracy` child, a merge with `Religion As Administrative Filter`, or only a routing decision.

## Current Public Surface

`Bureaucracy As Institutional Death` is not under split pressure: it is about 3,216 authored words, carries 35 evidence attributes, and uses 8 distinct local video slugs. It already includes the science case from `predictive-history-cjtunkcu1ia`: Bacon's bureaucratized doubt first creates progress, then over-specialization, funding dependence, opaque instruments, insularity, and gatekeeping make mature science more likely to block the kind of genius that founded it.

`Religion As Administrative Filter` is also not under split pressure: it is about 2,433 authored words and already carries the science case as method-based operator selection. Its active mechanism is not institutional death but the code that makes trusted operators legible: truth-duty for Persian administrators, bishop-controlled interpretation for church office, Confucian cultivation for scholar-official status, and scientific doubt for peer-reviewed truth production.

`Legitimacy Fiction` is larger at about 5,570 authored words, but the Holy Roman Empire source is already public there through `lens-point:legitimacy-fiction-governs-without-conquest`. That is the right home for the title/pretense layer: force cannot unify Europe, so legitimacy, unity, and differentiation have to be believed by pope, king, princes, priests, and subjects.

## Decision

Do not create a standalone `Freelance Imperial Bureaucracy` page now.

Keep the source pressure routed across existing homes:

- **Legitimacy Fiction** owns the Holy Roman Empire as useful fiction: the inaccurate title works because it gives divided actors a shared world that force alone cannot build.
- **Religion As Administrative Filter** owns the code and office-selection layer: doctrine, ritual, language, method, or church legitimacy makes operators trustworthy and gives them status.
- **Bureaucracy As Institutional Death** owns the institutional afterlife: offices, procedures, status monopoly, meta-reality, and information control preserve the body while draining openness, discovery, and renewal.
- **Eschatology** receives only comparison pressure from Augustine, City of God, new Jerusalem, and heaven-on-earth material unless the active mechanism is sacred time or role-script choreography.

The held phrase `freelance-imperial-bureaucracy` remains useful as a seed, but it is currently a comparison handle rather than a public child. Reopen it only if later sources show a repeated mechanism where a non-state institution performs imperial administrative work across jurisdictions by distributing legitimacy, local intelligence, culture, and meta-reality without direct command.

## Boundary Reasoning

The science case and church case are mirror images around institutional life.

In the scientific-revolution source, bureaucracy begins as a live method. Bacon's process separates hypothesis, experiment, data analysis, and replication so doubt can audit itself (`video:predictive-history-cjtunkcu1ia@transcript:v1#seg-0061`, `#seg-0062`). That belongs to Administrative Filter because method selects trustworthy operators. The same source later says specialization, funding, insularity, and rule-playing can make the system incapable of welcoming Galileo, Newton, or Einstein (`video:predictive-history-cjtunkcu1ia@transcript:v1#seg-0063`, `#seg-0064`). That belongs to Bureaucracy because the filtering code has become self-protection.

In the Holy Roman Empire source, the Catholic Church first supplies a legitimacy frame that lets the useful fiction govern where conquest cannot (`video:predictive-history-1dwvgtc7xzy@transcript:v1#seg-0008`, `#seg-0038`, `#seg-0048`). That belongs to Legitimacy Fiction. The later "freelance imperial bureaucracy" language (`video:predictive-history-1dwvgtc7xzy@transcript:v1#seg-0050`, `#seg-0051`) adds Bureaucracy and Administrative Filter pressure, but the source alone does not prove a separate public mechanism. It is one church/network case inside a wider legitimacy lecture.

A public append to Bureaucracy would mostly restate an already present science section and would risk making the page absorb every office-like religious institution.
