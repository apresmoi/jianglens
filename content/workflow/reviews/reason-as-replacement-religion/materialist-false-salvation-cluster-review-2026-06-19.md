## Boundary Decision

- **Reason As Replacement Religion** owns the atlas relation when a secular system takes over religion's truth-making, salvation, sacrifice, civil-dogma, or mythic-action functions.
- **Religion As Administrative Filter** owns selection and discipline of trusted operators: administrators, scholar-officials, scientists, priests, credentialed experts, party offices, or bureaucratic methods.
- **Attention Capture** owns consumer discipline, worker-to-consumer inversion, anxiety, captured consciousness, and attention returned to work or consumption.
- **Game Theory** owns false alternatives, payoff boards, wrong rewards, extremized opposition, and revolution/status reset only when the active source pressure is rule and incentive structure.
- **Power As Alchemy** owns money, capital, debt, science, nation, or AI becoming lived reality through trained imagination and institutional force.
- **Education** owns children, school, credential pressure, trust, and formation when education-for-money becomes the active false salvation surface.
- **Mass Society / AI Control / Eschatology** own the technocratic and AI layers by active mechanism: population administration, interface command, or holy final-order obedience.

The seed should be used only when the source shows a false-salvation chain: a material, rational, economic, technological, or educational system tells people what can save them while displacing love, spirit, relation, freedom, or the human heart.
