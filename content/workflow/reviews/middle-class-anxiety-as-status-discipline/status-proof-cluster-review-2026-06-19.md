## Decision

The cluster is real and useful, but its current public need is already met by [Attention Capture As Capital Extraction](/lens/attention-capture-as-capital-extraction/). The page has a dedicated section, **Why The Middle Class Keeps Moving**, and the stable `lens-point:middle-class-status-proof-keeps-attention-moving` anchor. The Dutch and Tocqueville episode reads already link their strongest status-proof paragraphs to that anchor. Adding another public surface now would duplicate the same mechanism before the corpus proves that readers need a separate hover or navigation home.

The seed remains valuable as a routing rule. Use it when a source carries the full status-proof ecology: unstable rank, visible proof, fear of falling, respectability, and cultural or institutional containment. Route narrower source moments to their active public homes.
