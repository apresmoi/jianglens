## Decision

Keep [How Poetry Creates Civilization](/lens/how-poetry-creates-civilization/) whole for now. Treat it as **split-watch, replacement/compression-first**, not an immediate child-extraction emergency.

The page is mature and central, but the current authored surface does not yet justify another split. Local checks on the current branch show about 5,434 Markdown words by `wc -w`, 107 evidence attributes, 9 stable lens-point anchors, and 13 distinct local video slugs. That sits below the ordinary word-count warning and remains well below the 20-source diagnostic threshold for public concept splitting. The apparent "83 sources" pressure is generated evidence/backlink density, not 83 distinct Jiang sources on the authored page.

The clearest detachable child has already been extracted: [Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion](/lens/imperial-poetry-and-trojan-horse-inversion/) now carries the Aeneid / anti-Homer / imperial-robot mechanism with five dated source slugs and the two moved durable anchors. That extraction reduced the main Poetry page's need to hold the whole Roman inversion sequence. A second immediate Poetry split would likely duplicate existing neighbors rather than clarify the atlas.

Because those neighbors already exist, a new Dante child from the current Poetry pressure would probably blur rather than sharpen the map unless clustered episode links require a narrower hover surface.

## Boundary Decision

Keep Poetry as the parent for **poetic medium as civilizational infrastructure**:

1. language makes private perception shareable;
2. rhythm, image, speech, and performance make memory portable;
3. repeated inhabitation forms admiration, shame, pity, desire, and public imagination;
4. poetry can open access beyond ordinary material reality;
5. the same access can democratize truth, privatize it into an elite priesthood, or become an imperial platform.

Do not create standalone pages now for:

- Dante As Access,
- Paradiso Method,
- Poetry As Virus,
- Poet-Prophet-Teacher,
- Oral Memory,
- Shakespeare As Language Empire,
- Modernism As Private Priesthood,
- Hollywood War Movie Reality.

Hold those as governed sections or anchors unless future source pressure proves a narrower public route is needed.
