## Boundary Decision

Keep **Human Heart** as the parent when the active diagnostic is whether a social order can preserve the human powers it claims to serve: love, guilt, pride, empathy, fallibility, forgiveness, curiosity, children, relation, conversation, courage, and meaning.

Keep **Love Recognizes The Other** as the child when the active diagnostic is whether love makes a concrete person more visible, alive, free, and capable of wisdom, or whether love's name is being used to turn that person into possession, obedience, mission obstacle, status proof, revenge license, or abstract idea.

Do not create standalone pages now for:

- Heart, Crisis, And Relational Survival;
- Tragedy, Forgiveness, And Fallible Authority;
- Promise And Forgiveness;
- Childhood Audits Civilization;
- Real Conversation Resists Performance;
- Reason Restrained By Love.

Hold those as governed parent branches unless a new source, clustered episode links, or a failed compression pass proves they need a narrower hover surface.

## Compression Rule

The next public Human Heart mutation should be replacement/compression before any ordinary append. It should:

1. preserve all 12 parent lens-point IDs;
2. keep the Love Recognizes section as a short gateway, not a re-explanation of the child;
3. tighten the literary battlefield / fallibility / tragedy sequence around one parent rule: forgiveness, argument, and fallibility let violence and perfect command stop reproducing themselves;
4. tighten the safe-place / real-conversation sequence around one parent rule: after predictive danger becomes visible, survival becomes relational and spiritual rather than private escape through wealth or geography;
5. route dense love-recognition examples to the child and route agency/noncoercion to Free Will, narrative inversion to Stories/Imperial Poetry, medium to Poetry, platform reaction to Attention, and population management to Mass Society.

If that compression cannot keep the page reader-held after another strong source, reopen child review around **Heart, Crisis, And Relational Survival** first. That branch is more distinct from Love Recognizes than another literary-forgiveness child because it tests Human Heart under catastrophe, money failure, geography failure, and online-performance pressure.
