## Search / Answer Judge

Result: pass with synthesis.

The winning public shape answers the likely query before explaining the whole model. It keeps aliases visible, uses exact source refs, and gives agents enough routing to quote Jiang with source, timestamp, and related lens context.

Applied feedback:

- preserve dated refs in a readable source table;
- include related lens pages only after the answer and source trail.

## Jiang Voice / Provenance Judge

## Residual Risk

The glossary is a search surface, so entries can become too broad if agents keep expanding them. The new skill caps normal entries at roughly 350-900 words and routes larger material to lens pages or parent/child split review.
