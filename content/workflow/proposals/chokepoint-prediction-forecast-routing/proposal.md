## Decision

Do not promote the March/April Iran-war forecast cluster directly into the forecast ledger yet.

The public Chokepoint page now has the chronology compression it needed: March 9 live-war bridge, March 11 forecast-audit pressure, March 13 no-exit dollar-war form, March 20 war-gets-own-logic, March 24 escalation ladder, and April 21 classroom war-architecture compression. The next risk is different. The dated forecast claims are now visible enough that a later worker could treat each one as a ledger item just because it recurs.

Prediction needs a comparison queue first. Each candidate below should preserve source date, conditional wording, active mechanism, and later-source comparison before any canon or ledger promotion.

## Why This Advances The Lens

The Chokepoint page teaches the material mechanism: gates, access, cheap energy, ground limits, protection credibility, exit cost, and continuing war architecture. Prediction should not duplicate that public page. Its job is to audit dated claims that put Jiang's model at risk.

This proposal turns the accumulated Chokepoint impacts into a smaller downstream rule: which forecast families are mature enough to compare, which page owns the non-forecast mechanism, and what would make a later promotion defensible. That keeps Prediction from becoming a dump of dramatic claims and keeps Chokepoint from absorbing every forecast that mentions Hormuz, draft, or Trump.

## What Not To Promote Yet

Do not promote "Jiang predicted the Iran war" as a single ledger item without tracing which earlier sources contain the forecast, what exact event each forecast named, and whether later wording converts a model claim into retrospective reputation.

Do not promote Pax Judaica, Third Temple, Gog-and-Magog, or acceleration-with-capital claims in this forecast-routing pass. They touch the same March interviews, but the active boundary is Eschatology/Prediction and needs its own comparison record with explicit speculation caveats.

Do not promote AI surveillance, digital ID, digital currency, draft, or ICE normalization only because they appear in the April 21 war-architecture lecture. Mass Society should first decide whether those are home-front-control mechanisms, forecast items, or both.

Do not add another public Chokepoint section for these forecasts. The page already carries the material chronology. The next public edit, if any, should deepen Prediction's audit discipline or route readers toward comparison records.
