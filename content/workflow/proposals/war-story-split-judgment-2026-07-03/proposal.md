## Decision

Cassandra's fresh warning is useful because the page still has high generated provenance density, but the live authored page does not force another child split. On current `main`, after the June parent compression and previous Strategy child extractions, a direct authored-page check gives about 5,149 words, 57 authored evidence attributes, 15 distinct authored local video slugs, and 12 page lens points. That keeps the page below the ordinary split diagnostics of about 6,000 authored words and about 20 distinct authored local sources.

## Why This Is The Right Boundary

The page remains a coherent parent because its active object is the **material test of war stories**. Jiang's recurring question is not only whether a story can mobilize belief, but whether the story can survive economics, organization, logistics, time, geography, morale, replacement capacity, enemy adaptation, and political consequence.

The existing Strategy children already carry the largest extracted burdens:

- **Chokepoint Empire** owns gates, passages, energy, food, ships, bases, sea-lanes, and access-control surfaces.
- **No-Exit War And Escalation Ladder** owns war momentum after entry, sunk cost, face, draft pressure, and escalation-ladder compulsion.
- **Imperial Retrenchment And Proxy Attrition** owns cost transfer, vassal expenditure, resource-fortress retreat, and replacement-muscle auditions.

The parent still has a readable job that those children cannot replace: it teaches the audit that joins world theory, war form, spectacle, hybrid pressure, shock-and-awe fantasy, dominance versus control, Rome's will-as-capacity, and Hollywood war losing reality into one diagnostic surface.

## Routing Rules

Use **When War Becomes A Story Instead Of A Material Test** when the active question is whether a war story can be carried by cost, organization, logistics, replacement, public will, political consequence, and enemy adaptation.

Route outward when the active object changes:

- **How Stories Control Reality** and **Screen-World Governance** own narrative payload, mediated story-world, repetition, and image-world control when the frame itself is the mechanism.
- **Chokepoint Empire** owns gates, straits, sea lanes, bases, energy, food, shipping, and access-control surfaces.
- **No-Exit War And Escalation Ladder** owns entry traps, sunk-cost escalation, face, draft pressure, and war momentum after entry.
- **Imperial Retrenchment And Proxy Attrition** owns resource-fortress retreat, ally burden transfer, vassal spending, and proxy attrition.
- **Game Theory** owns payoff correction, player sequencing, threat stories, and strategic interaction when material execution is not the main question.
- **Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy** owns dated forecasts, horizons, conditions, status, misses, revisions, and ledger readiness.
- **Mass Society As Political Constraint** owns population-scale administration when scarcity, war, rationing, draft, or surveillance turns bodies into a managed home front.
- **Eschatology As Script** owns sacred role sequence, end-times choreography, and suffering made meaningful when the war is being read through divine history.

## Reopen Threshold

Reopen a public Strategy split only if one of these becomes true:

- the authored parent crosses about 6,000 words or 20 distinct authored local video slugs after replacement/compression is attempted;
- clustered hovers or backlinks make `strategy-optics-sacrifices-the-board`, `strategy-spectacle-exploits-fragility`, `strategy-war-form-demands-society`, `escalation-dominance-becomes-trap`, or `control-beats-dominance` unreadable as parent anchors;
- a future Stories, Screen-World, Chokepoint, No-Exit, Game Theory, Prediction, or Mass Society review needs Strategy to take a narrower child surface for a specific remaining branch;
- a replacement-compression draft cannot keep the material-test parent readable while preserving the existing 12 anchors;
- four or more additional dated sources repeat the full spectacle/optics-sacrificing-the-board bundle in a way that cannot be routed through Stories, Screen-World, Chokepoint, or the existing Strategy parent.

Until then, do not create standalone Spectacle War, Hollywood War, Shock And Awe, War Form Makes Political Form, Dominance Versus Control, Control Beats Dominance, Military Dominance Is Not Victory, Hybrid War, or Material Test pages from the current evidence.
