## Decision

Do not create a public **Monotheism As Abstraction Engine** page now.

The seed is real, but it is not yet a clean public child concept. The current public surface already carries the necessary reader-facing claim in **Power As Alchemy**: Jiang says money, the individual, and the nation-state are concepts beyond ordinary first-principles experience, and that monotheism supplies the revolution in thought that lets such abstractions become reality (`video:predictive-history-ajfxykt9joo@transcript:v1#seg-0091`, `video:predictive-history-ajfxykt9joo@transcript:v1#seg-0094`).

The next Secret History sources show that the mechanism is bigger than Power alone, but they also split by active object:

- **Power As Alchemy** owns symbol-to-reality conversion: money, capitalism, science, the nation-state, and abstractions that command lived behavior.
- **Eschatology As Script** owns sacred origin, destination, final order, prophecy, heaven, judgment, and role assignment.
- **Religion As Administrative Filter** owns the selection and discipline of trusted operators when the code forms priests, scholars, scientists, officials, or administrators.
- **Legitimacy Fiction** owns imperial identity scripts, return stories, temple/purity claims, and founding fictions when they authorize rule.
- **Nation As God-Machine** owns the downstream national person that demands love, obedience, war, sacrifice, and memory.
- **Reason As Replacement Religion** should remain an atlas relation when reason, debate, progress, science, economics, utility, nation, or technocracy inherit religion's truth-making and salvation functions.

That means the seed should become a routing spine and backfill priority, not a public page.

The previous compact intake for `predictive-history-ajfxykt9joo` created an explicit downstream action: compare later Secret History religion lectures before deciding whether **Monotheism As Abstraction Engine** deserves public promotion. The stronger move is to hold the title while making the routing rules explicit.

## Routing Rules

Use **Monotheism As Abstraction Engine** only as a held seed when the active sentence answers: what change in reality-making lets an invisible, universal, singular, nonlocal symbol become more real than ordinary experience and thereby authorize money, individual personhood, science, capitalism, nation, law, or orthodoxy?

Use **Power As Alchemy** when the active object is symbol-to-reality conversion, money, value, scarcity, individualism, national memory, science/capitalism as operating abstractions, or power turning nothing into everything.

Use **Eschatology** when the active object is origin/destination, prophecy, Messiah, Antichrist, heaven, hell, judgment, final order, sacred role assignment, or catastrophe made meaningful by an end-times script.

Use **Legitimacy Fiction** when sacred text, temple, return, purity, empire, church, legal story, or founding myth authorizes a ruler, people, institution, or imperial identity.

Use **Religion As Administrative Filter** when the active object is office discipline: who may serve, who is trustworthy, what code selects priests, scholars, scientists, officials, or administrators.

Use **Nation As God-Machine** when religious inheritance becomes the national person, national memory, mass sacrifice, national geography, or school-installed obedience to an imagined body.

Use **Reason As Replacement Religion** when the source is about the modern transfer of sacred functions into reason, debate, progress, science, economics, utility, revolution, nation, materialism, or technocracy.

Use **Free Will**, **Human Heart**, or **Dead World** when the active object is conscience, divine spark, love, relation, consciousness, mind-before-matter, material-only ontology, or the refusal to flatten spiritual reality into administrative obedience.
