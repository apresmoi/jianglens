## Decision

Keep **Monotheism As Abstraction Engine** as a held seed. Do not create a public Monotheism page, glossary entry, durable lens point, or canon claim yet.

The seed is now real enough to govern routing. It is still not clean enough for public promotion. The impact trail shows one shared pressure: monotheism lets an invisible, universal, singular authority reorder reality so that symbols, truth, evil, individual conscience, sacred text, science, money, empire, and nation can become more binding than ordinary local experience. But the same trail also splits by active mechanism:

- **Power As Alchemy** owns symbol-to-reality conversion: Godhead formula, money, science, capitalism, individualism, and nation-state abstractions becoming lived compulsion.
- **Religion As Administrative Filter** owns office discipline: hierarchy, bishops, priests, scholars, interpreters, trusted administrators, and dogma as a selection code.
- **Scripture, Identity, And Imperial Authorization** owns sacred text as political identity machinery: return, temple, purity, law, memory, and written authorization.
- **Eschatology** owns sacred origin/destination, role scripts, temple futures, salvation, heaven, hell, prophecy, and final-order forecasts.
- **Dead World** and **Free Will** own false-God prison ontology, mind-before-matter pressure, inner light, conscience, mistakes, and agency.
- **Nation As God-Machine** owns the downstream national person when sacred abstraction becomes population, memory, sacrifice, territory, and war.
- **Reason As Replacement Religion** remains an atlas relation when modern reason, science, progress, economics, utility, nation, revolution, or technocracy inherit religion's truth-making and salvation functions.

Public promotion would blur those homes today. The right mutation is this updated boundary record: future agents can use the seed as a routing spine without turning "monotheism" into a topic umbrella.
