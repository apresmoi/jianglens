## Forecast Candidate

### AI Quality Decreases Over Two Years

On December 9, 2025, Jiang frames the passage as a "limits test" for AI over the next couple of years. He says the prediction is that AI will become less effective over the next two years (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0128`). The reason is not simply weak software. His claim is that AI needs good, clean internet data to keep producing the illusion of intelligence, but the system has reached the limit of internet data and new models are now using data created by AI itself (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0128`).

Jiang then states the test more sharply: over the next two years, does the quality of ChatGPT increase or decrease? His answer is decrease. The expected symptom is that, as a user talks more with ChatGPT, the answers become less relevant and more confusing (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0129`). A later segment restates the mechanism as degradation over time and separates that from the labor-market consequence: even if AI does not improve the economy, it can still replace white-collar workers and cause unemployment (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0131`).

Ledger status: **not ready**. The source gives a date, horizon, mechanism, and observable symptom, but a future ledger review still needs a measurement rule. "ChatGPT quality" cannot be promoted unless the project defines what counts as quality decline, whether the relevant object is ChatGPT specifically or public AI assistants generally, and how later product changes, model releases, usage changes, censorship, monetization, or embodied-data collection affect the test.

## Neighboring Mechanisms

### Power / Infrastructure

The same AI passage starts with political economy. Jiang calls AI a scam from his perspective because the underlying technology is mass computing, data centers consume community electricity and fresh water, companies do not know how to monetize, and massive scale-out demands capital (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0124`). That is not the same claim as the two-year quality forecast. It belongs to Power As Alchemy when abstraction, valuation, IPO pressure, monetization, and capital need become lived compulsion. It belongs to Platform Infrastructure when the active object is data centers, electricity, water, and deeper technical pipes.

### AI As Control Surface

AI As Control Surface owns the answer interface and degradation mechanism when the question is how the machine keeps presenting itself as a knowing surface. Jiang says AI processes scraped information and relays it in a way pleasing to the human eye, trying to fool the user, while needing clean data to keep the trick going (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0128`). If future public prose needs this source, use it as the December 2025 anti-hype origin before the March 2026 Wizard-of-Oz and AI-God bridges.

### Mass Society

Mass Society owns the escape route. When the host suggests sensors and real-world interfaces, Jiang agrees that the solution is for AI to leap into the real world, then names microchips inside the body, digital ID, and digital currency as ways to stop the data problem (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0133`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0135`). Jiang then says the play is to force everyone to be microchipped and part of the matrix, and he later explains why parents may accept bloodstream chips for child safety (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0137`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0139`).

This should not become a Prediction ledger item unless the ledger candidate is explicitly about AI-quality decline. The embodied grid is an administrative mechanism, not the forecast itself.

## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns this record because the active problem is forecast discipline: source date, two-year horizon, test condition, later comparison, miss/revision, and whether ledger promotion is possible.

AI As Control Surface owns the answering surface and data-degradation mechanism when public prose needs to explain why the interface depends on clean data and can still become a command surface even if it degrades.

Mass Society owns embodied data extraction: sensors, robotics, microchips, digital ID, digital currency, child tracking, bloodstream safety bargains, and matrix participation.

Power As Alchemy and Platform Infrastructure own the cost, valuation, monetization, electricity, water, and data-center buildout. Those pressures may explain why the system seeks new compulsion, but they are not the same as the two-year quality forecast.

Attention owns the helper/companion/dependence layer when AI becomes a focus director, motivation engine, girlfriend, Wikipedia-in-the-brain, or answer dependency.

Eschatology owns AI God, Jesus/demon masks, omniscience plus religion, Antichrist, and sacred obedience. Those later sources may compare with the December data-limit passage, but they do not make the December forecast an eschatology page.

## What Not To Do Next

Do not create standalone AI Quality Collapse, Synthetic Data Collapse, ChatGPT Decline, AI Eats Itself, AI Data Exhaustion, or Embodied AI Data Grid pages from this record.

Do not promote a ledger item that says only "Jiang predicted AI would get worse." The test must name the two-year horizon, quality/relevance/confusion symptoms, and data-quality mechanism.

Do not count body chips, digital ID, or child tracking as confirmation of the quality forecast. Those are the proposed escape route from data exhaustion, not proof that the first claim has resolved.

Do not cite layoffs as proof that AI improved or degraded. Jiang explicitly allows the paradox that AI can fail to improve the economy while still replacing white-collar labor.
