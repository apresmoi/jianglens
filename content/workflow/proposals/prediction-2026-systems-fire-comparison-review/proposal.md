## Decision

Do not promote the December 2025 and January 2026 cluster as one broad forecast that "2026 becomes World War III," "America fights everywhere," or "China is strangled."

The recent corpus-impact intakes for `interview-l9heow1syg0`, `interview-orycs0r2tpg`, and `interview-p5gg9xxbqze` create a real comparison problem. They do not yet justify another public Prediction paragraph, and they should not become one undifferentiated ledger item. The useful synthesis is to split **systems fire** into narrower forecast families:

1. Venezuela as limited war, show-force, accident risk, and hemispheric leverage;
2. Iran/Hormuz as corridor-denial and no-exit escalation pressure;
3. Odessa and Europe as ceasefire/rearmament hinge, NATO final-stand pressure, and longer-horizon European system stress;
4. China strangulation as a resource, sea-lane, dollar, and allied-proxy sequence.

This is synthesis, not intake. The audit still shows 123 missing impact files, but the last three Plato PRs were compact corpus-impact intake: `interview-orycs0r2tpg`, `interview-p5gg9xxbqze`, and the April 8 Dante reupload. No newly merged higher-pressure source outranks the portfolio rule, so the bounded useful move is to turn accumulated pressure into a comparison map.

## Why Public Prose Is Held

The public Prediction page already teaches that a forecast needs source date, object, horizon, condition, comparison trail, and correction rule before it can be scored. This cluster is not ready for a public example because each source uses the same theaters for different objects.

The related public pages already own the mechanisms:

- [Chokepoint Empire And No-Exit War](/lens/chokepoint-empire-and-no-exit-war/) owns route access, Hormuz, Malacca, the first island chain, ports, oil, food, and maritime permission.
- [When War Becomes A Story Instead Of A Material Test](/lens/when-war-becomes-a-story-instead-of-a-material-test/) owns whether Europe, Odessa, Venezuela, and Iran pass the material board rather than the desired story.
- [Imperial Retrenchment And Proxy Attrition](/lens/imperial-retrenchment-and-proxy-attrition/) owns allied bodies, resource-fortress retreat, China strangulation, and protection turning into extraction.
- [Dollar Hegemony](/lens/dollar-hegemony/) and [Debt As Sovereignty](/lens/debt-as-sovereignty/) own reserve demand, Treasury compulsion, household debt absorption, and dollar-system survival.

Prediction should not absorb those mechanisms. It should decide what can be compared later and what would count as confirmation, miss, partial signal, revision, or still-open horizon.

## Forecast Families To Split

### 1. Venezuela: Limited War, Show-Force, Or Systems Fire

The December 21 source frames Venezuela as bargaining and brinkmanship before full invasion. Jiang describes both sides playing chicken, says the assembled U.S. force is not enough for a clean amphibious conquest, and leaves escalation open through domestic distraction, advisor pressure, pilot accidents, or outside forces (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0039`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0040`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0045`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0046`).

The January 5 source turns the same theater into optics and leverage: Venezuela can look conquered inside Trump-world if the image says it is conquered, but that short-term spectacle sacrifices strategy and becomes leverage before an April China bargain (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0061`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0064`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0069`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0075`).

The December 19 source adds the systems-fire warning: a limited Venezuela operation can transmit through Hormuz, Odessa, Europe, East Asia, oil, shipping, food, manpower, and alliance credibility (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0030`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0032`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0035`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0036`).

Promotion condition: do not score "Venezuela" as one prediction. Split limited strike, full invasion, negotiated settlement, regional demonstration, accident risk, and cross-theater systems fire. A visible show of force may confirm spectacle or leverage without confirming full invasion. An accident may confirm escalation risk without confirming strategic design.

### 2. Iran And Hormuz: Corridor Denial, Dollar Survival, Or No-Exit Escalation

The December 19 source gives the cleanest corridor-denial version. Jiang says Iran is the hinge because it could let Russia, China, India, the Middle East, Africa, and Europe trade through a continental system outside American control, so America is pushed toward conflict even when the local theater is not the whole object (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0024`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0025`).

The January 5 source shifts horizon and sequence. It treats Iran as the endgame after China is settled or constrained, with 2026 accelerating the path and a possible 2027 climax (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0083`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0092`).

Routing: Chokepoint owns Hormuz, Malacca, route denial, energy flow, and maritime access. Dollar Hegemony owns the petrodollar and reserve-demand survival problem. Strategy owns ground-war risk, no-exit escalation, logistics, and whether the empire can stop once the war logic begins. Prediction owns the dated split between corridor-denial pressure, 2026 acceleration, 2027 climax, and later public-event scoring.

Promotion condition: do not merge "Iran war," "Hormuz closure," "petrodollar defense," "Eurasian corridor denial," "Israel drags America," and "2027 endgame" into one ledger item. They have different objects and different failure modes.

### 3. Odessa And Europe: Final Stand, Ceasefire Trap, Or Long Horizon Collapse

The December 21 source sharpens the ceasefire problem: Jiang says Europe has lost autonomous decision-making, is pot-committed like a gambler, and Russia cannot accept a ceasefire that leaves Odessa unresolved because a pause would let Europe prepare for the larger war (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0018`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0019`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0021`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0022`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0024`).

The January 5 source extends the same region into a five-to-ten-year NATO-collapse and European civil-war horizon centered on Odessa and European conscription limits (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0045`).

The December 19 source should stay adjacent rather than fused: its systems-fire claim says Odessa is one of the theaters that can answer a Venezuela or Iran spark, but that is not the same as a city-capture forecast (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0035`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0036`).

Routing: Strategy owns the material board: Black Sea access, European manpower, rearmament, morale, and whether a ceasefire changes the war's capacity. Bureaucracy owns European elite denial when the self-reinforcing bubble is primary. Prediction owns the date trail and must keep the horizons distinct.

Promotion condition: no ledger item should be called only "Odessa." Split Russia taking Odessa, NATO final stand at Odessa, ceasefire as rearmament window, Europe conscription stress, and five-to-ten-year NATO collapse.

### 4. China Strangulation: Resource Denial, Sea-Lane Pressure, Or Debt Absorber

The December 19 source says America wants to cut China's access to South American resources, compete in Africa, pool allied resources, and expose Japan and South Korea as cannon-fodder layers (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0039`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0040`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0042`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0044`).

The January 5 source gives the route-control layer: Taiwan, Japan, Middle Eastern oil, and Malacca become the access-control surface through which China and Japan can threaten each other's economies (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0032`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0033`). It also connects Western Hemisphere resources to dollar and China bargaining pressure before April (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0054`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0055`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0075`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0091`).

The December 21 and December 19 sources add the financial absorber problem: American debt and consumer exhaustion require Chinese household spending, financial opening, services purchases, or Treasury/dollar support, while China resists surrendering sovereignty (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0028`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0029`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0031`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0012`).

Routing: Imperial Retrenchment owns China strangulation through allied bodies and resource-fortress posture. Chokepoint owns Malacca, Taiwan, first-island-chain, oil, ports, and access. Dollar Hegemony and Debt As Sovereignty own the dollar/Treasury/household-absorber demand. Prediction owns dated scoring only after the object is narrowed.

Promotion condition: split South American resource denial, African competition, Japanese/South Korean proxy exposure, maritime access pressure, China household debt absorption, financial liberalization, and dollar/Treasury support. A trade concession cannot confirm all of them.

## What Not To Do Next

Do not append this cluster to public Prediction as "Jiang predicted 2026 chaos." That would make the page larger while making the forecast less falsifiable.

Do not create standalone Venezuela, Hormuz, Odessa, China Strangulation, Japan/South Korea Cannon Fodder, Eurasian Corridor, 10-20 Year War, April China Visit, or Trump World pages from this cluster. Existing mechanism pages already have better homes.

Do not let one public event confirm multiple claims at once. A Venezuela strike is not automatically a systems-fire confirmation. A China trade deal is not automatically a resource-denial confirmation. An Odessa ceasefire is not automatically a Russia-miss or Europe-collapse confirmation. An Iran headline is not automatically Hormuz, petrodollar, corridor, and 2027-endgame confirmation.

Do not erase chronology. December 19 gives the broad multi-front and China-strangulation frame. December 21 gives frozen-assets, Odessa ceasefire, and Venezuela show-force refinement. January 5 gives the April China-bargain, optics, Malacca, Odessa/NATO horizon, and Iran-endgame sequence. Later April and May sources may intensify the model, but they should not become the first formulation just because they were processed later.
