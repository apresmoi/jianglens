## Parent Boundary After Scripture Split

The Scripture child split clarified the parent. Legitimacy Fiction should now stay focused on the inheritance mechanism:

1. a political crisis cannot be settled by force or administration alone;
2. a story, name, office, title, ritual, debt, mercy practice, public scene, text, or rule system makes the solution repeatable;
