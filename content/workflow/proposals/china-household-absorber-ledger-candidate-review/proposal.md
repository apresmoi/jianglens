## Decision

Do not promote "U.S.-China grand bargain" or "China household absorber" as one confirmed ledger item.

The December 2025 and January 2026 source cluster is strong enough for a ledger-candidate review, but not for promotion. It splits into at least five different objects:

1. Chinese household spending and credit as the absorber for exhausted American demand;
2. Chinese financial liberalization as a sovereignty trap;
3. education purchases and student debt as the narrow compromise surface;
4. Western Hemisphere oil, food, and resources as leverage before a China bargain;
5. continued dollar, Treasury, or U.S.-dollar oil demand as the reserve-order payoff.

This is synthesis, not corpus-impact intake. The audit still shows 123 missing impact files, but recent Plato work has been impact-heavy and the previous `prediction-2026-systems-fire-comparison-review` explicitly named this narrow China household absorber / U.S.-China bargain review as a next useful mutation. No fresher high-pressure missing source appeared in room history.

## Candidate Under Review

Working family label:

> In late 2025 and early 2026, Jiang argues that American debt and consumer exhaustion push Washington to make Chinese households, Chinese finance, Western Hemisphere resources, and dollar-denominated oil purchases absorb the burden that American consumers and Treasury demand can no longer carry alone.

That sentence is not a ledger candidate. It is the family boundary.

Promotion must choose one exact candidate:

- **Household absorber**: America demands that Chinese consumers spend, borrow, and max out credit cards because American consumers are exhausted (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0012`).
- **Financial liberalization trap**: America demands market opening, while China refuses because Western bankers would steal resources, bankrupt the country, and addict it to easy money (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0031`).
- **Education-purchase compromise**: Chinese banks lend to families so Chinese students buy U.S. or European education, possibly giving both sides a face-saving channel (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0033`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0036`).
- **Resource and embargo leverage**: cutting China's food or oil supply, Venezuela pressure, Caribbean force, and Western Hemisphere resources force China toward negotiation (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0028`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0029`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0075`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0091`).
- **Dollar/Treasury support**: the United States needs China to keep buying dollars or Treasuries, while China needs energy access and trade, producing mutual destruction risk if either side pushes too far (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0008`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0012`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0014`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0015`).

## Source Trail

### December 19: Household Spending As The First Absorber

`interview-p5gg9xxbqze` gives the compact household version. Jiang says American consumers have maxed out their credit cards, so the demand shifts toward Chinese consumers spending, borrowing, and opening financial markets. The sovereignty refusal is already inside the same segment: China says it will not surrender sovereignty (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0012`).

This source is already public on two stable anchors:

- `lens-point:debt-sovereignty-household-absorber`
- `lens-point:power-alchemy-consumer-debt-absorber`

Ledger implication: this source supports a household-absorber candidate, but it does not by itself score an April bargain, Treasury demand, Venezuela leverage, or education-purchase compromise.

### December 21: Bargain Mechanics And Sovereignty Refusal

`interview-l9heow1syg0` turns the household claim into a negotiating sequence. Jiang says Trump wants to cut off China's oil and food supply, resolve the U.S.-China trade war in 2026, and find the only absorber large enough for $38 trillion in U.S. debt: the Chinese consumer (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0028`). The coercive offer is financial liberalization and consumer spending, or embargo, with Venezuela underneath the bargaining pressure (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0029`).

The source then narrows the refusal. China reads Western bankers as parasites who bring easy credit, resource theft, bankruptcy, and addiction, so financial-market liberalization is not a neutral trade concession; it is a sovereignty trap (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0031`). That forces the likely compromise surface into education: Chinese banks lend to families, Chinese parents send children to the United States or Europe, and Trump does not allow Taiwan independence (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0033`). The follow-up says U.S. higher education needs Chinese students quickly and could even export U.S. college storefronts into China charging dollar tuition (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0036`).

Ledger implication: this source is stronger than a generic "deal" claim because it names possible concessions. But those concessions are not equivalent. Education purchases are not financial liberalization; a Taiwan accommodation is not consumer debt; an embargo threat is not a completed household-spending transfer.

### January 5: Dollar Order And Resource Leverage

`interview-orycs0r2tpg` shifts the same family into dollar-system chronology. Jiang says the big 2026 event is Trump's April China visit and a grand bargain, anchored in the post-1971 dollar: petrodollar demand plus China opening stabilize the dollar after gold (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0006`). He then says China became worried about dollar stability and tried to regain sovereignty through yuan internationalization and gold-corridor alternatives (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0007`).

The January version makes Treasury dependence explicit. Trump wants China to keep buying dollars because a Treasury dump would create a U.S. sovereign-debt crisis; Venezuela and Middle East pressure can force China to buy oil using U.S. dollars and rely more on the Western Hemisphere (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0008`). The relation is mutual captivity: China needs energy and markets, the United States needs China not to dump Treasuries, and an oil cutoff plus Treasury dump could destroy both (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0012`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0014`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0015`).

Later in the source, Western Hemisphere leverage becomes the practical board. Trump controls or pressures Venezuela, Colombia, Mexico, Africa, and China-Japan tension before April, then can offer China access to Venezuelan oil infrastructure under U.S. dollar terms (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0075`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0091`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0092`).

Ledger implication: January is not merely a restatement of household debt. It adds the reserve-order object: keep China inside dollar and Treasury demand by controlling energy and resource access. That belongs beside the household candidate, not inside it.

## Routing

Prediction owns this review only as dated ledger hygiene: source date, object, horizon, comparison trail, partial signal, possible miss, and revision. It should not absorb the mechanisms.

Debt As Sovereignty owns Chinese household borrowing, education loans, and financial liberalization as sovereignty loss. Its existing `debt-sovereignty-household-absorber` anchor is the right public home for the obligation body.

Power As Alchemy owns the conversion layer where one empire's debt seeks a new social body through another population's consumption, credit, education purchases, or market opening. Its existing `power-alchemy-consumer-debt-absorber` anchor is sufficient for public linkage.

Dollar Hegemony owns reserve demand, Treasury buying, dollar oil purchases, petrodollar history, and the pressure to keep China inside the dollar game.

Imperial Retrenchment owns the Western Hemisphere resource-fortress posture: using Venezuela, South America, Africa, oil, food, and resources to make China buy through the empire's controlled zone.

Chokepoint owns oil access, food supply, Malacca, Taiwan, Japan, Middle East routes, and Western Hemisphere gates when the material access surface is primary.

Education receives only secondary evidence unless the future candidate is specifically Chinese student purchases, U.S. higher-education dependence, and exported U.S. college storefronts.

## Ledger Readiness

Recommended status: **split-before-promotion; active comparison family**.

Reasons:

- The source trail has strong date continuity: December 19 gives household absorber; December 21 gives the coercive bargain and education workaround; January 5 gives dollar/Treasury/resource leverage.
- The objects differ enough that one public event cannot confirm them all. A China trade deal may signal rapprochement without proving household debt absorption. A rise in Chinese students may confirm education-purchase pressure without proving Treasury support. A Venezuela oil deal may confirm resource leverage without proving financial-market opening.
- The exact horizon is mixed: December 21 says 2026 and April meetings; January 5 says April and several 2026 meetings; the household mechanism itself has no clean public scoring date.
- Several neighboring mechanisms are already public and specific enough. Promotion should not create a standalone China Household Absorber, Grand Bargain, April China Visit, or Financial Liberalization Trap page.
