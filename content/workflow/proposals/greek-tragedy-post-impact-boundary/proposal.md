## Decision

Do not create a standalone Greek Tragedy or Greek Theater page now.

Do not append ordinary public prose to Human Heart, Poetry, Fictional Heroes, or the atlas root now.

Do not promote Public Ritual Memory Machines from this cluster.

The public map already routes the pressure more cleanly than a new page would. A standalone tragedy page would mostly gather topic aliases and then have to split itself immediately across heart, poetry, theater/media, fictional character, education, and civic ritual. The active mechanisms are real, but they are not one missing public concept.

## Boundary Reasoning

**Human Heart As Civilizational Measure** owns tragedy when the active mechanism is public heart-knowledge: revenge, grief, hubris, pity, power, judgment, self-accusation, and fallible authority become visible before they govern the city blindly. This is the correct home for the 2024 tragedy lecture's democratic mirror-work.

**How Poetry Creates Civilization** owns tragedy when the active mechanism is poetic moral infrastructure: language, performance, remembered character, hubris, epiphany, catharsis, sorrow, and civilizational memory train the inner person. This is the correct home for the Great Books #3 tragedy layer.

**Jiang Lens / World-Making Media** owns Greek theater when the active mechanism is a public symbolic form training consciousness: citizens perform, watch, judge, switch perspective, imagine enemies, debate power and justice, and form a shared democratic mind. This should remain an atlas relation unless repeated public-form pressure creates a navigation problem.

**How Fictional Heroes Become Part Of The Self** owns tragedy only when the mechanism is character inhabitation: the spectator recognizes his own hubris, pity, terror, sorrow, and desire through another person until the character becomes usable inside the self.

**Public Ritual Memory Machines** remains a reviewed seed, not a promoted public surface. It becomes relevant only when sources compare staged public forms as collective-memory machines. Greek theater can support that seed, but the current cluster's strongest public work is already done by Human Heart and World-Making Media.

**Education As A Soul Game** should not absorb this cluster merely because theater educates citizens. Education owns school, authority, tests, trust, compliance, creativity, and institutional formation. The Greek legacy / liberal arts sequence can support Education later only if the question is training consciousness through a curriculum or schooling regime.

## Split And Compression Judgment

The cluster does not justify a new public child page now.

Human Heart is near normal watch pressure but has recent split and post-love-child governance. The tragedy branch is not the leading extraction candidate; Love Recognizes The Other remains the stronger child if Human Heart grows again.

Poetry is also mature and evidence-dense. It should receive replacement/compression before another ordinary tragedy append. The tragedy/catharsis paragraph already carries the Great Books #3 mechanism, and the 2024 tragedy lecture should remain background there unless Poetry is being compressed around Greek civilizational memory.

The atlas root is large by nature and should not be treated like a concept page. Its Greek theater anchor works as a compact cross-page relation. A new atlas mutation is unnecessary unless multiple public pages begin duplicating the theater-as-consciousness explanation.

## Reader Consequence

For readers, a standalone Greek Tragedy page would be less useful than the current distribution. It would answer "what is tragedy?" by pulling material out of the pages where it does diagnostic work:

That distribution keeps the public lens mechanism-first rather than topic-first.
