The previous collapse forecast routing proposal prevented a single broad "Jiang predicted collapse" ledger item. This record does the next narrower job: it separates one forecast family from the rest of the collapse cluster and names the public mutation conditions.

That matters because this family crosses several tempting but different public surfaces. If placed only in Power, the forecast and timing problem disappears into money ontology. If placed only in Prediction, the mechanism that makes the forecast intelligible disappears. If placed only in Game Theory, the monetary system becomes a generic payoff board. The useful object is therefore a comparison record: what claim is being compared, what evidence carries it, what each existing page owns, and what would justify promotion.

## Core Mechanism

The family has five separable steps.

1. **Natural-cycle rejection.** Jiang rejects the economics-class boom-bust explanation and says financial collapse is not accidental or natural but has to be engineered (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0001`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0005`).

2. **Liquidity command.** Money appears as bank-created liquidity rather than simple saved wealth: the bank loan example makes money "an idea," "a concept," and "a collective hallucination"; interest rates coordinate bank release or restriction of liquidity rather than merely guiding consumers (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0006`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0008`).

3. **Profit/loss asymmetry.** The finance-war genealogy says profits are privatized while losses are socialized by nation-states and people; activity, including war, becomes the route by which money must go do something and generate more money (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0016`).

4. **Payoff reversal.** The hidden game is not whether the official borrower, lender, or economy survives. It is whether the player behind the loan, bubble, or capital flow benefits more from continuation or default. The 2008 and mortgage examples support the already-public real-game anchor without requiring a new page (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0028`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0030`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0032`).

5. **Exit and acquisition.** The late lecture turns the mechanism into a forecast family: private credit and AI bubbles can be collapsed; capital can shift away from America; an engineered financial crisis lets positioned actors leave with money, buy distressed assets such as water and oil, and profit from chaos or "blood on the streets" (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0047`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0048`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0056`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0057`).

The comparison family is not the same as general civilizational decline. The older collapse surface says decline is slow and collapse is sudden, with collapse moving from consent and deception into coercion and survival (`video:predictive-history-k-l9jbgo74@transcript:v1#seg-0044`, `video:predictive-history-k-l9jbgo74@transcript:v1#seg-0047`). It also says financial capitalism and speculation move away from real wealth (`video:predictive-history-k-l9jbgo74@transcript:v1#seg-0011`, `video:predictive-history-k-l9jbgo74@transcript:v1#seg-0012`). The March 31 source adds agency and timing: collapse becomes rational when the break itself pays.

It is also not simply the Bronze Age capital-collapse model. The Bronze Age source says trade routes, bronze capital, and total interconnection turn crisis into systemic collapse (`video:predictive-history-rkt0hqooghe@transcript:v1#seg-0002`, `video:predictive-history-rkt0hqooghe@transcript:v1#seg-0003`, `video:predictive-history-rkt0hqooghe@transcript:v1#seg-0023`). The March 31 source is narrower and more dangerous: it asks who can time a financial break, exit the host, and buy the rubble.

## Routing Rules

Route to **Power As Alchemy** when the active mechanism is money as invented but binding reality: bank liquidity, interest-rate coordination, collective hallucination, money-as-God, privatized profit/socialized loss, capital migration to safer enforcement, and distressed-asset buying.

Route to **Game Theory** when the active mechanism is the real player/payoff reversal: the visible role says preserve the loan, bank, economy, or empire, while the hidden payoff makes default, bubble rupture, war, or migration rational.

Route to **Prediction** when the active object is a dated forecast comparison: sudden U.S. or global financial collapse, private-credit rupture, AI-bubble rupture, war-tied collapse, or capital-center movement. Prediction should not count every later recession, market decline, AI-market correction, or Israel-related claim as confirmation unless the source date, horizon, mechanism, object, and later outcome match.

Route to **Imperial Retrenchment** only when the active mechanism is successor-center pressure: China as attempted post-2008 center, China refusing or failing to carry full reserve-currency and overseas-base burden, or Israel appearing as a next capital/activity destination.

Route to **Secret Society** only when hidden trust, initiation, taboo, covert coordination, or named elite-network machinery is the active object. The presence of "elite families," "occult," or behind-the-scenes actors in the March 31 source is not enough by itself to move the family out of Power/Game Theory/Prediction.

Route to **Eschatology** only when Pax Judaica, Greater Israel, Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, messianic role, or sacred sequence drives the claim. The capital-center projection toward Israel is not automatically eschatology.

Use **Collapse As Transition Surface** only as an atlas relation unless a future review proves the public map needs a standalone page for crossing points where slow decline becomes sudden break, hidden beneficiaries time the rupture, assets or authority transfer, and post-collapse forms become possible.

## Promotion Test

A public mutation becomes justified only if one of these conditions is met:

- **Power compression/replacement:** Power As Alchemy needs revision and this family can replace weaker money/capital prose with a tighter liquidity-command and exit-liquidity section without pushing the page into bloat.
- **Prediction method mutation:** Prediction needs a boxed example showing how a financial-collapse forecast family is split into source date, object, horizon, mechanism, later comparison, and possible miss/revision before ledger promotion.
- **Ledger promotion:** later 2026 or 2027 records let reviewers compare the March 31 claims against actual or revised surfaces: U.S. financial crisis, global collapse, private-credit rupture, AI-bubble rupture, war trigger, capital migration, or distressed-asset acquisition.
- **Collapse page review:** multiple dated sources show that existing Power/Game Theory/Prediction/Retrenchment pages blur the engineered-break mechanism, and the atlas would become clearer with a public Collapse As Transition Surface child or page.

Until one of those tests is met, keep this as workflow synthesis. It should guide future exact episode links and prevent broad public claims.

## Boundary Notes

This record does not verify the external truth of Jiang's hidden-coordination claims. It records Jiang's source-backed model and requires future public prose to keep attribution, dates, and speculation caveats visible.

Do not let "engineered" become an all-purpose explanation for every downturn. The family requires a named positioned actor, mechanism of liquidity or bubble control, payoff from rupture, and exit/acquisition path.

Do not let "collapse" become a mood. The comparison surface should ask: what breaks, when, by what mechanism, who benefits, what later evidence would count, and whether Jiang revises the claim.

Do not let the Israel capital-center passage silently merge with Eschatology. It may touch Pax Judaica or sacred sequence in neighboring sources, but this source's capital route is primarily a Power/Imperial Retrenchment/Prediction problem unless sacred role-script evidence is cited directly.
