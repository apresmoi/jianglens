## Decision

Keep **Love Recognizes The Other** whole.

Cassandra's new split-set warning is useful as a generated navigation signal, but the current public page is not oversized and does not yet contain a mature second child boundary. Direct checks on `main` show about 1,892 authored Markdown words, 18 authored evidence attributes, 28 unique authored exact source refs, 9 distinct authored local video slugs, and 2 stable page lens points.

That keeps the child page far below the ordinary split diagnostics of about 6,000 authored words or about 20 authored local video slugs. Love Recognizes should remain on compression-watch only because recent Dante atlas routing and Human Heart backlinks are making it more visible as a receiving page.

Do not create another public love child page now. Future work should route ordinary examples outward or replace/compress existing sections before adding prose.

## Why This Is The Right Boundary

Love Recognizes is already a child of [Human Heart As Civilizational Measure](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/). Its job is narrow: test whether love makes a concrete person more visible, alive, free, and capable of wisdom, or whether love's name turns the beloved, enemy, child, dead, or God-bearing person into possession, proof, mission obstacle, obedience test, revenge license, or abstract idea.

The page's sections are not separate mature concepts yet. They are facets of one relation test:

- **Secret Language** supplies the private, falsifiable recognition pattern through Odysseus and Penelope.
- **Anti-Possession** gives the rule that love wants what is best for the person rather than using the beloved to fill the self.
- **Treachery Blinds Love** shows betrayal as perceptual damage: the soul loses the ability to see a concrete act of love.
- **Relation As Divine Light** keeps Dante/Beatrice and divine light relational instead of abstract.
- **Enemy Recognition** gives the battlefield version through Priam and Achilles.
- **Imperial Anti-Love** shows the Aeneid's mission logic sacrificing Dido, Turnus, and concrete persons to destiny, piety, and revenge.

Splitting any one of these now would create a thin page that depends on the parent relation-test frame to make sense. The existing page is still short enough for a reader to hold the whole diagnostic at once.

## Routing Rules

Use **Love Recognizes The Other** when the active diagnostic is whether love makes a concrete person visible or erases the person under love's name.

Route outward when the active object changes:

- **Human Heart As Civilizational Measure** owns the broader civilizational audit of love, guilt, pride, empathy, fallibility, forgiveness, children, relation, conversation, courage, and meaning.
- **Free Will As Cosmic Burden** owns agency, nonintervention, chosen cleansing, self-forgiveness, and the fact that love cannot force redemption.
- **Dante As Poetic Access And Reception** owns Dante as access, reception, dream, downloaded vision, poetic summons, and reader transformation.
- **How Poetry Creates Civilization** owns the poetic, epic, theatrical, and scriptural medium that carries heart-knowledge.
- **Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion** owns the Aeneid when inherited Homeric scenes are reversed into imperial piety.
- **The Guide Who Becomes A Trap** owns Virgil and guide logic when mediation, translation, contract, debt, duty, or guide-limit is the mechanism.
- **How Stories Control Reality** owns narrative infection and reality control when the story-world rather than the relation is primary.
- **Eschatology As Script** owns afterlife structure, fear of death, heaven/hell/purgatory imagination, and sacred sequence.

## Reopen Threshold

Reopen a public Love Recognizes split only if one of these becomes true:

- the authored child crosses about 4,000 words or 15 distinct authored local video slugs after replacement/compression is attempted;
- clustered hovers or backlinks make `heart-love-recognizes-person` or `heart-treachery-blinds-love` unreadable as child anchors;
- a future Human Heart, Dante, Free Will, Imperial Poetry, Guide Trap, Poetry, Stories, or Eschatology review needs Love Recognizes to export a narrower surface for treachery-blindness, imperial anti-love, or enemy-recognition;
- a replacement/compression draft cannot keep the child readable while preserving the existing 2 anchor IDs;
- four or more additional dated sources repeat one full held-child bundle in a way that cannot be routed through the existing page and neighboring concepts.

Until then, keep Love Recognizes as one child page. Its next public mutation should be replacement/compression or careful routing, not another child extraction.
