## Parent Boundary After Extraction

Keep **Human Heart** as the parent when the active diagnostic is whether an order, institution, story, school, crisis plan, or social model can see and preserve the human powers it claims to serve: love, empathy, guilt, pride, vulnerability, forgiveness, fallibility, curiosity, relation, children, conversation, and courage.

The parent should stay a civilizational measure. It should not become a warehouse for every literary scene where love appears or every interview moment where Jiang recommends family, neighbors, spirituality, or conversation.
