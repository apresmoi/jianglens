## Candidate Objects To Split Before Scoring

### 1. Protection-Credibility Failure

Object: allies, GCC states, Japan, South Korea, Europe, or other clients visibly doubt the reliability of American military protection after an Iran/Gulf failure.

Possible signals: public basing reconsideration, security realignment, explicit protection doubts, Gulf accommodation with Iran, or Jiang's later revision that protection credibility has failed.

Do not score from: a single bad headline, a temporary market decline, ordinary diplomatic hedging, or an oil-price spike without a protection-credibility surface.

Primary owner: [Chokepoint Empire](/lens/chokepoint-empire-and-no-exit-war/) for the tested gate; [No-Exit War](/lens/no-exit-war-and-escalation-ladder/) for the exit trap; Prediction for source date, object, and status.

### 2. Treasury-Buyer Doubt

Object: states that buy or hold Treasuries because of the U.S. security and trade order begin treating Treasury demand as optional, risky, or politically unjustified.

Possible signals: named buyer reduction tied to protection failure, official reserve diversification tied to Gulf credibility, explicit Treasury-buyer doubt in later Jiang comparison, or a policy move that breaks the protection-for-debt bargain.

Do not score from: generic debt anxiety, ordinary yield movement, or BRICS/gold discussion not tied to this protection-credibility chain.

Primary owner: [Dollar Hegemony](/lens/dollar-hegemony/) and [Debt As Sovereignty](/lens/debt-as-sovereignty/). Power As Alchemy owns only the conversion where the debt instrument becomes lived geopolitical reality.

### 3. GCC Oil-Dollar Recycling Loss

Object: Gulf oil sales, sovereign wealth, or surplus recycling stop reinforcing U.S. markets, the dollar order, or AI/data-center finance in the way Jiang names.

Possible signals: oil settlement away from dollars, Gulf sovereign-wealth withdrawal from U.S. markets, public redirection of Gulf surplus toward another security patron, or explicit later Jiang scoring of GCC recycling loss.

Do not score from: Gulf infrastructure vulnerability alone, a strike on an oil facility alone, or a broad claim that "the petrodollar is dying" without the recycling mechanism.

Primary owner: Dollar Hegemony for oil-dollar demand and recycling; Power As Alchemy for the `power-alchemy-dollar-protection-loop` conversion; Chokepoint for the Gulf access surface.

### 4. AI-Finance Inflow Rupture

Object: GCC or Gulf sovereign-wealth inflows into U.S. AI, data centers, or market valuations become a named casualty of protection failure.

Possible signals: Gulf AI-finance withdrawal, data-center investment reversal tied to Gulf security, market shock explicitly tied to Gulf capital recycling, or later Jiang comparison that the AI-finance support broke.

Do not score from: ordinary AI valuation correction, data-center cost pressure, or technology-policy change unconnected to Gulf capital.

Primary owner: Dollar Hegemony when the inflow is reserve-demand machinery; Platform Infrastructure only if pipes, data centers, or compute ownership become the active mechanism; Prediction for scoring surface.

### 5. Domestic-Order Crisis From Monetary Loss

Object: the loss of GCC petrodollar recycling or Treasury demand becomes a domestic American economic/political crisis in Jiang's comparison trail.

Possible signals: Jiang explicitly links Gulf monetary loss to domestic fracture, civil conflict, default, market crisis, or authoritarian hardening; public event evidence matches the named monetary trigger.

Do not score from: domestic unrest with no monetary trigger, general recession, or boomer/gerontocracy authoritarian pressure unless the dollar/GCC loss is the named cause.

Primary owner: Prediction for claim status; Dollar Hegemony for demand loss; Imperial Retrenchment for the resource-fortress replacement posture; Gerontocracy/Mass Society only when their mechanisms become primary.
