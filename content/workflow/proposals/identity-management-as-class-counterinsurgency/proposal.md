## Proposed Boundary

Identity management as class counterinsurgency names Jiang's recurring mechanism in which elite institutions redirect class grievance into identity categories so a broad economic coalition cannot remain one political body. The active move is not simply "DEI is bad" or "identity politics exists." The mechanism is a counterrevolutionary substitution: class anger that could name a banking, financial, or oligarchic target is broken into race, gender, historical-grievance, or managed victim categories.

This should remain a held proposal seed for now, not a public concept page. The source cluster is real, but the public atlas already has strong neighboring homes:

- [Proximity Games And Elite Overproduction](/lens/proximity-games-and-elite-overproduction/) owns the player/payoff question: which elite faction benefits when public conflict becomes the visible surface of a nearer elite game?
- [Mass Society As Political Constraint](/lens/mass-society-as-political-constraint/) owns the population-management surface when fragmentation keeps large populations administratively governable.
- [Attention Capture As Capital Extraction](/lens/attention-capture-as-capital-extraction/) owns anxiety, consumer inversion, debt, and focus discipline when alienation is redirected away from solidarity.
- [Power As Alchemy](/lens/power-as-alchemy/) owns financial abstraction, bailout, and money-world conversion when the banking or asset layer is primary.
- [Education As A Soul Game](/lens/education-as-a-soul-game/) and [Meritocracy Converts Wounds Into Credentials](/lens/meritocracy-converts-wounds-into-credentials/) own school, credential, and university formation when identity categories become the soul-game itself.
- [Bureaucracy As Institutional Death](/lens/bureaucracy-as-institutional-death/) owns administrative category hardening when offices, HR systems, and compliance procedures are the active machinery.

The seed is useful because it prevents those neighboring pages from absorbing a narrow class-fragmentation claim too loosely. It should be promoted only if future public prose needs one reusable surface for the exact Occupy-to-DEI / class-to-identity substitution.

## Boundary Notes

- **Not Proximity Games by default**: Proximity Games owns elite player/payoff analysis. This seed belongs there when the question is which elite faction benefits from redirected grievance. It exceeds the page only if repeated public prose needs the specific identity-category substitution.
- **Not Mass Society by default**: Mass Society owns scale administration, digital permission, AI surveillance, and population governability. This seed belongs there when fragmentation is one control instrument among other administrative tools.
- **Not Attention Capture by default**: Attention owns the broader inversion of alienation into consumer comparison, debt, work, posting, and focus discipline. This seed owns identity categories only when they break class solidarity.
- **Not Education or Meritocracy by default**: universities matter in the April 6 formulation, but the active mechanism is not school formation unless identity doctrine becomes the reward/credential game.
- **Not Bureaucracy by default**: HR, compliance, and administrative categories may harden the mechanism, but the current source cluster is about elite political use before office procedure.
- **Not generic anti-DEI canon**: Jiang's claim is morally and politically sharp, but the lens should not flatten it into a culture-war stance. The usable mechanism is the substitution of managed historical grievance for economic class alignment.

## Future Public Shape

If promoted later, the public surface should likely be a compact section or atlas relation before a full concept page. A possible public sequence:

1. Financial crisis exposes class power and makes Occupy a possible 99-percent coalition.
2. Elite institutions answer by foregrounding race, gender, and historical grievance over wealth redistribution.
3. Universities and culture channels make the new categories feel morally compulsory.
4. The trick succeeds as anti-solidarity, but can later backfire when the same grievance machinery turns against Gaza-war or genocide politics.
5. In the AI-order recurrence, fragmentation becomes even more valuable because a lonely, divided public is easier to model, soothe, monetize, surveil, and govern.
