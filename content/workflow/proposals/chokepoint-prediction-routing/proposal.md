## Boundary

**Chokepoint Empire And No-Exit War** owns the material mechanism:

- Hormuz, Malacca, Gibraltar, Panama, the Arctic, Gulf desalination, food routes, bases, and sea-lane permission.
- Pressure-point warfare against air defenses, airports, banks, data centers, oil infrastructure, food routes, and water systems.
- Ground-war, draft, logistics, manufacturing, occupation, mountain-fortress, and limited-operation failure.
- Protection credibility: allies host bases, recycle surpluses, buy debt, and trust the dollar because the empire appears able to protect the route system.
- War momentum: stopping discredits the order the war was meant to preserve.
- Ceasefire theater: peace signals are tested against continuing war architecture.

**Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy** owns the audit layer:

- the stated dates and horizons;
- what would count as confirmation, miss, revision, or unresolved speculation;
- Jiang's moral burden around correct war prediction;
- explicit speculative modeling caveats;
- comparison across March 9, March 11, March 13, March 20, March 24, April 21, and later April 24 sources before ledger promotion.

**Power As Alchemy** owns the monetary conversion only when the active mechanism is dollar belief, petrodollar recycling, Treasury demand, Gulf surplus reinvestment, or money as control. Chokepoint can cite the dollar trap, but it should not become a general money page.

**Eschatology As Script** owns sacred role assignment, Pax Judaica, Third Temple, Gog and Magog, acceleration-with-capital, and any claim where catastrophe is made meaningful by end-times sequence.

**Imperial Retrenchment And Proxy Attrition** owns the shrinking empire's business model: fortress hemisphere, resource/weapons/financing sales, allied cost transfer, long-run retreat, and successor-muscle auditions.

**Mass Society** owns the home-front machinery: draft, ICE normalization, AI surveillance, digital ID/currency, rationing, work restriction, and population control.
