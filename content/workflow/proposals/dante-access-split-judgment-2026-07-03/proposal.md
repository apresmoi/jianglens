## Decision

Keep **Dante As Poetic Access And Reception** whole.

Cassandra's new warning is useful as a navigation-density signal, especially because the Dante family now has a sidebar family and the page serves as the first receiving page. But direct checks on current `main` show that this is not an oversized public concept and not yet a mature parent that needs a second-generation child.

The page is about 2,833 Markdown words by `wc -w` and about 2,804 content words after frontmatter removal. It has 27 authored evidence attributes, 29 unique exact source refs, 10 distinct authored local video slugs, 25 generated doc evidence marks, 29 generated source refs touching the route, and 2 stable lens-point anchors. That is below the ordinary split diagnostics and below the source-fan-in threshold that would justify creating a narrower public Dante child.

Do not create a new Dante, Inferno, Purgatorio, Paradiso, Virgil, fraud, prophecy, or virtues page from this warning alone. The current page should stay on compression-watch because its `Dante Cluster Map` is a routing surface, but replacement/compression and outward routing remain the right next moves.

## Why This Is The Right Boundary

The page is already a child of [How Poetry Creates Civilization](/lens/how-poetry-creates-civilization/). Its job is narrow: Dante's poem gives access across mediation, enters memory, works through delayed reception, trains imagination, and transforms the reader over time. That remains one coherent mechanism.

The page's apparent breadth comes mostly from its cluster-map duty after PRs #1196 and #1197. That map names adjacent Dante lecture pressure, but it routes the active mechanisms outward:

- Virgil and guide-dependence route to [The Guide Who Becomes A Trap](/lens/the-guide-who-becomes-a-trap/).
- Agency, ascent, self-forgiveness, non-possession, and chosen redemption route to [Free Will As Cosmic Burden](/lens/free-will-as-cosmic-burden/).
- Concrete love and person-recognition route to [Love Recognizes The Other](/lens/love-recognizes-the-other/) and [Human Heart As Civilizational Measure](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/).
- Fraud, post-truth, and poisoned interpretation route to [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) or [Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy](/lens/prediction-as-falsifiable-prophecy/) until a reviewed seed matures.
- Meritocratic rivalry and competition route to [Meritocracy Converts Wounds Into Credentials](/lens/meritocracy-converts-wounds-into-credentials/).
- Roman/Aeneid inversion routes to [Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion](/lens/imperial-poetry-and-trojan-horse-inversion/).

The right boundary is therefore **Dante as poetic access and reception**, not Dante as a complete topic. The current public page is doing that job while keeping the larger Dante family legible.

## Routing Rules

Use **Dante As Poetic Access And Reception** when the active mechanism is poetic form giving the reader access across institutional mediation, entering memory before understanding, training imagination, or becoming legible through delayed reception.

Route outward when the active object changes:

- **How Poetry Creates Civilization** owns poetry as the broader civilizational medium.
- **The Guide Who Becomes A Trap** owns Virgil, guide-dependence, false rescue, interior authority, and guide-defeat.
- **Free Will As Cosmic Burden** owns agency, ascent, chosen cleansing, self-authored station, and non-possessive divine love.
- **Love Recognizes The Other** owns concrete recognition, anti-possession, enemy recognition, and love that does not erase the person.
- **Human Heart As Civilizational Measure** owns forgiveness, promise, relation, guilt, empathy, and heart-knowledge as civilizational test.
- **How Stories Control Reality** owns narrative governance, fraud, and interpretive poisoning when story-control is primary.
- **Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy** owns dated forecast discipline and ledger-readiness, distinct from poetic prophecy as delayed recognizability.
- **Meritocracy Converts Wounds Into Credentials** owns elite competition and status humiliation.
- **Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion** owns Aeneid/Roman inversion and imperial piety.

## Reopen Threshold

Reopen a public split review for this page only if one of these becomes true:

- the authored child crosses about 4,500 Markdown words after replacement/compression is attempted;
- the page reaches about 15 distinct authored local video slugs and the new sources are not merely route-map support;
- the `Dante Cluster Map` grows beyond routing and starts carrying mechanism prose that cannot be compressed back into the existing page;
- clustered hovers, backlinks, or episode links make either `poetry-invades-and-remakes-reader` or `poetry-democratizes-access-to-reality` unreadable as compact anchors;
- four or more additional dated sources repeat one held-child bundle around Dante fraud/post-truth, Purgatory imaginative practice, or Paradiso imagination beyond logic in a way that cannot be routed through existing receiving pages;
- a future Poetry, Guide Trap, Free Will, Love Recognizes, Human Heart, Stories, Prediction, Meritocracy, or Imperial Poetry review needs Dante Access to export a narrower surface for reader navigation.

Until then, keep Dante Access whole, replacement/compression-first, and governed by active mechanism rather than Dante topic names.
