## Source Trail

### May 29, 2024: Trap Before Active Watch

The 2024 Iran Trap lecture is not a confirmed war record. It is a speculative model exercise that names a possible two-to-four-year path and then asks why an invasion that looks like American initiative can become Iran's preferred terrain (`video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0010`, `video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0011`, `video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0019`).

The reusable point is the inversion. Jiang says the United States wants regime change, while Iran wants the invasion on terrain it can control; other regional and great-power actors may each imagine a different payoff inside the same catastrophe (`video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0025`, `video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0043`, `video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0046`). The trap also includes a nuclear-threat exit problem and China naval-capacity branch, but those must remain separate from later no-nuclear-use and China-restraint branches (`video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0047`, `video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0049`).

### June 18, 2025: Live Field Before March 2026

The June 2025 update changes the epistemic status. Jiang now treats U.S. bombing as imminent and argues that regime-change tools cannot work cleanly against Iran because the target is not only leadership but national capacity: leadership decapitation, infrastructure pressure, sanctions, opposition financing, and sectarian fragmentation all try to break the state-population bond (`video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0001`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0002`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0003`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0004`).

The same update gives Iran As Pressure Point its live bridge: Persian identity, mountainous geography, observed Iraq lessons, Hormuz, Gulf clients, American bases, oil prices, consumer pressure, and domestic support all move together (`video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0005`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0006`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0007`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0008`). It also becomes an earlier field application of the later control-over-dominance rule: America has military dominance, but Iran may have strategic flexibility because it can widen pain surfaces and sustain costs differently (`video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0006`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0009`, `video:predictive-history-8xdl-7taqnu@transcript:v1#seg-0010`).

For Prediction, the branch set is the key.
