## Forecast Family

The family can be named:

> Jiang's eschatological convergence forecast family tests whether rival sacred scripts can move multiple actors toward the same Middle East crisis while U.S. retreat, China absence, Odessa pressure, Turkey/Byzantium reversal, Israel/Third Temple escalation, Putin succession, and baby-boomer time horizon unfold on different clocks.

That sentence is not ledger-ready. It is a family label.

Ledger promotion would need to choose one exact candidate with source date, horizon, conditions, neighboring mechanism, later comparison trail, and miss/revision status. The August 1 source contains at least eight separable candidates.

1. **Middle East world-war convergence**: Jiang says a major Middle East conflict will draw in the world and become World War III (`video:predictive-history-yq-xg1nibms@transcript:v1#seg-0025`). This belongs to Prediction only as a dated forecast candidate; Eschatology owns why different traditions converge on the same charged geography.

## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns this proposal because the live problem is forecast hygiene: source date, horizon, conditional sequence, later comparison trail, and ledger readiness. It does not own the whole eschatological mechanism.

Eschatology owns the role-script: convergence points, Antichrist functions, sacred geography, Orthodox/Dugin strategy, Dome/Temple sequence, world-unifier expectations, and tribulation. The public Eschatology page already carries this source as a definition spine and does not need a child page from this record alone.

Chokepoint owns Iran/Hormuz when the active board is water, infrastructure, Strait closure, ground invasion, terrain, supply, and no-exit war. The August 1 near-term Iran scenario should compare with the March-April Iran-war cluster before any ledger item.

Imperial Retrenchment owns American retreat into the Western continent, allied exhaustion, and successor-center burden. It should not become a generic "America absent" page.

Gerontocracy owns the baby-boomer time horizon: rulers who do not counterstrategy because the bill arrives after their expected deaths. Prediction can preserve the date and 10/20/30-year language, but the mechanism is intergenerational extraction.

Secret Society is secondary here. The source does compare fragile conspiracy with unconscious coordination, but this forecast-family record is not a hidden-coordination proposal. Use Secret Society only when the active mechanism is secrecy, deniability, trust, membership, or coordination technology.
