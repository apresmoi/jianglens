That sequence is useful because it clarifies routing. It does not yet justify a new public child. The active mechanisms are already distributed across durable homes:

- **Poetry** owns living memory, poet-prophet access, language as shared world, Homer as civilizational medium, and bardic memory as a force that makes the past present.
- **Dead World And The Cave** owns Great Books as escape practice from materialist school ontology and false cave surfaces.
- **Fictional Heroes** owns Achilles and Odysseus when the active mechanism is a literary person becoming real inside the reader.
- **Human Heart** owns Priam, Achilles, Odysseus, guilt, love, forgiveness, family, enemy-recognition, and wrongdoer-side repair.
- **Stories** owns speech projecting inhabitable worlds and the Trojan-horse / rival-world machinery when narrative makes reality.
- **Free Will** owns divided desire and self-responsibility when Odysseus' fake madness or Achilles' hidden motives make injury partly self-authored.
- **Love Recognizes The Other** owns the narrower relational test when love makes the enemy, beloved, victim, or child concretely visible.

## Seed Status

Hold `great-book-as-perspective-reincarnation` as a seed. It names a real pattern: a great book can force the reader into many lives, including the defeated enemy's perspective. But public promotion would currently duplicate Dead World's Great Books escape practice, Poetry's living-memory mechanism, Fictional Heroes' character-inhabitation mechanism, and Human Heart's forgiveness/recognition mechanism.

Hold `conscious-universe-as-memory-field` as a seed. The vocabulary recurs across Great Books #1, #2, #3, #4, Paradiso, Talese, and Dead World material, but it remains too broad for one diagnostic page. Sometimes the active mechanism is poetic access; sometimes dead-world ontology; sometimes a character becoming real; sometimes memory as moral repair; sometimes attention or imagination as capturable energy.

Hold `home-after-evil` as a seed. The Odyssey source gives a strong moral-repair mechanism, but current public homes can carry it better: Human Heart for guilt and family recognition, Poetry for bardic memory, Free Will for divided desire, and Love Recognizes for concrete relation.

Hold `soul-as-worldview-rupture` as a seed. It is a useful translation layer, but it needs more dated sources before becoming a public lens. Until then, route it by active mechanism to Human Heart, Dead World, Education, Attention, or Free Will.

## Boundary Notes

Do not make a standalone Homer page. Jiang uses Homer as poet-prophet, teacher, character-maker, civilizational founder, living-memory portal, and anti-Virgil counterweight. A topic page would blur mechanisms that the current atlas keeps usefully distinct.

Do not make a standalone Iliad page. The Iliad pressure is split: Achilles as fictional hero and heart mirror; Homeric speech as Stories/Poetry; Priam and Achilles as Human Heart / Love; Great Books escape as Dead World; Trojan perspective as a held perspective-reincarnation seed.

Do not make a standalone Odyssey page. Odyssey pressure is split: wrongdoer-side repair belongs to Human Heart and Free Will; bardic memory to Poetry; concrete Penelope/Odysseus recognition to Love; the Trojan horse before Roman inversion to Stories or Imperial Poetry only when comparison is explicit.

Do not promote `conscious-universe-as-memory-field` into canon or glossary before a separate review distinguishes Jiang's metaphysical vocabulary from public diagnostic mechanisms. The phrase could otherwise collect every use of consciousness, Geist, God, universal memory, archetype, imagination, and soul without telling the reader what to do with it.

## Public Readiness Test

A future public child becomes justified only if a later pass can prove all of these:

1. At least four dated sources with impact records carry one narrower mechanism, not only shared Homeric or consciousness vocabulary.
2. The mechanism cannot be read cleanly through Poetry, Dead World, Human Heart, Fictional Heroes, Stories, Free Will, or Love without losing reader usefulness.
3. The page can state a diagnostic more precise than "great books let readers inhabit other lives" or "memory exists in a conscious universe."
4. It preserves chronology across January 7, January 14, January 21, January 28, and March 4 rather than flattening the sequence into one metaphysical thesis.
5. It avoids turning Jiang's strong literary-metaphysical claims into unsupported empirical canon.

The likely public candidate, if this cluster later matures, is not a Homer page. It would be narrower:

> A great book becomes a soul technology when living memory makes another consciousness present enough that the reader can inhabit rival perspectives, recognize hidden guilt, and return to the world with a different moral imagination.

That sentence is still too cross-cutting for public promotion. It needs another comparison against the existing Dead World Great Books anchor, Poetry living-memory anchor, Fictional Heroes character-inhabitation anchors, Human Heart battlefield-forgiveness anchor, and Love Recognizes child.
