# How Stories Control Reality Split Review

Status: proposal for public lens size governance

## Decision

Treat [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) as a split-review candidate before adding another ordinary source section. The page is still just under the word-count threshold at about 5,965 words, but it already carries 101 evidence refs, 13 distinct Jiang sources, and 13 durable lens points. The page remains a strong parent entry point, but the anchor structure now shows several child-like mechanisms living under one broad name: viral story infection, living scripture, Trojan-horse inversion, imperial-poetic training, subconscious coordination, operative myth, screen-world governance, war-script feedback, and guide-trap desire training.

The next public mutation should keep the parent as Jiang's general mechanism of story control while testing child boundaries for the clusters that already have their own stable anchors and neighboring pages. Do not append another ordinary literature, media, war, or legitimacy example to the parent unless the edit compresses an existing section or moves a cluster toward a child page.

## Why This Advances The Lens

This is synthesis work after several Prediction comparison and compact intake passes, not another historical backfill. The live corpus-impact audit still shows many missing historical files, but no fresh high-pressure source appeared in the room or audit. Cassandra's latest room signal points to size-governance pressure on public pages, and this page is the clearest unhandled case: it has not yet had a split-review proposal, while Game Theory already has one and has already produced a public child page.

The issue is not that the Stories page is badly written. The issue is that it is becoming a parent atlas node while still behaving like a single concept chapter. Future sources can correctly route to it from literature, war, media spectacle, religion, education, legitimacy, secret society, and guide-trap material. Without a split decision, the page will keep absorbing true examples until readers lose the mechanism.

## Recommended Parent Boundary

Keep the parent page as **Jiang's general mechanism for how narrative sets the world people act inside**:

1. attention is captured;
2. imagination projects a world;
3. the world becomes memorable through speech, song, scripture, film, ritual, or poetry;
4. desire and action start to feel natural inside the projected scene;
5. material reality eventually tests the story.

The parent should hold the mechanism, a compressed chronology, and short gateway examples. It should not become the full home for every repeated child mechanism.

## Child Candidate 1: Story Virus And Remade Consciousness

First child candidate:

**Story Virus And Remade Consciousness**

Mechanism: a story lodges in memory, repeats inside consciousness, removes guilt or inhibition, and spreads through people and institutions as a new way to see and act. This is narrower than "stories control reality" because the active movement is infection, internal repetition, moral disinhibition, and institutional transmission.

Source base already visible in the parent:

- **2025-12-28**, `interview-f7n-6pid0tk`: real magic as storytelling; the song that becomes a virus; Frankist doctrine spreading through elites and institutions.
- **2026-01-07**, `predictive-history-tsd-8fga84a`: attention as energy and captured imagination as recruited world-building.
- **2026-04-01**, `interview-oerknj-uypa`: operative myth shaping action while making truth correction unstable.
- **2026-04-08**, `predictive-history-6m1z-v3wgok`: poetry entering memory and producing dissonance over time.

Boundary: Stories owns the world-setting parent mechanism. The child would own infection, repetition, guilt removal, and consciousness remaking. Secret Society owns the membership/trust network; Taboo owns forbidden breach and moral permission; Attention owns focus capture when the inner resource rather than the narrative vehicle is primary.

This is probably the most public-ready child because it already has contemporary political and war examples, a clear reader diagnostic, and a neighboring Strategy page that would benefit from cleaner routing.

## What Not To Split Yet

Keep it as a parent-page anchor unless later Genesis, Bible, civilization, or education sources make living-memory debate a multi-source public mechanism.
