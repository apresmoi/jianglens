Use **Chokepoint**, **Iran As Pressure Point**, **No-Exit War**, or **Strategy** when the active mechanism is Hormuz, Gulf infrastructure, water, energy, trade-route access, war capacity, occupation, logistics, escalation, pressure hardening, or material coercion.

Use **Power**, **Dollar Hegemony**, **Debt**, or **Imperial Retrenchment** when the active mechanism is reserve legitimacy, capital safe house, asset migration, forced buying, successor-center burden, stablecoins, debt, or money becoming operating reality.

Use **Secret Society** only when the source supports hidden trust, secrecy, ritualized binding, blackmail, transgression, or coordination machinery. Do not route ordinary apocalyptic role-script material there merely because actors are hidden, elite, or speculative.

Use **AI As Control Surface** and **Mass Society** when the active mechanism is interface or administration: answer engine, companion, joined database, digital ID, digital currency, data center, police prediction, work allocation, draft, rationing, body sensor, or permission grid. Eschatology receives AI only where the tool is cast as Jesus-mask, Antichrist function, holy empire, sacred final order, or salvation-through-obedience machinery.

Use **Poetry**, **Human Heart**, **Love Recognizes The Other**, and **Free Will** when the active mechanism is the medium of imaginative access, concrete relation, non-possessive love, agency, refusal, or the rule that salvation cannot eliminate choice without becoming hell.

## Split Or Merge Judgment

Do **not** create **Sacred Sequence Becomes Geopolitical Choreography** now.

The sacred-sequence branch is the densest section and remains the first future child candidate, but it is still functioning as the geopolitical middle of the Eschatology parent. It has five anchors across role assignment, promise becoming plan, ritual sequence, geopolitical plan, and collapse/capital acceleration. Those anchors route different active objects well enough: role-script, operational promise, ordered ritual choreography, sacred geostrategy, and capital timing.

Do **not** create **Holy Empire / Obedient AI** now.

The June 21 checkpoint still holds. The current public surface is one compact section and one durable anchor. The AI system belongs in Eschatology only when it is made into sacred final order; database, interface, population, attention, value, and free-will layers already have sharper homes.

Do **not** create **Heaven As Remembered Bliss** now.

The Paradiso addition is real pressure, but it is a counterweight inside the parent rather than a page-ready child. Poetry owns the medium that makes Heaven felt; Human Heart and Love Recognizes own the concrete relation and non-possessive love; Free Will owns agency and noncoercive salvation. Eschatology owns the afterlife-script change: Heaven becomes inhabitable through memory, music, love, and imagination instead of institutional terror.

Do **not** merge Eschatology into Prediction, Stories, Religion, Free Will, or Poetry.

Those pages own adjacent mechanisms. Eschatology owns the ending-story as role-script: the grammar by which sacred destination, ordered acts, and afterlife meanings coordinate present action.
