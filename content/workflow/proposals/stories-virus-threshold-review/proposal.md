> How does a story, song, myth, poem, or doctrine enter consciousness, repeat from inside the person, change what feels possible or guiltless, and then transmit through people or institutions as a self-reproducing way to see and act?

Do not use Story Virus merely because a source says "story," "myth," "propaganda," "poetry," "religion," "virus," or "attention." Route by active mechanism:
