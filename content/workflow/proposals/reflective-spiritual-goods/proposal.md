## Proposed Boundary

Record **reflective spiritual goods** as a compact atlas relation for Jiang's anti-zero-sum moral cosmology. The mechanism is that some goods do not diminish when shared. Love, generosity, forgiveness, kindness, light, and courage intensify through reflection: one person's action changes the whole field because each soul mirrors the others.

This seed should stay narrower than "love" or "spirituality" as topics. It applies when a source shows the same chain:

1. a person mistakes reality for material scarcity or zero-sum possession;
2. the source distinguishes material goods from spiritual goods;
3. a spiritual act is shared, reflected, or multiplied through other people;
4. the multiplication changes the moral stakes of one person's choice.

The seed should not become a public concept page yet. The existing pages already own most of its active surfaces. [Human Heart](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/) owns relational love, recognition, empathy, and forgiveness when another person becomes visible. [Free Will](/lens/free-will-as-cosmic-burden/) owns the choice to shine, forgive the self, cleanse the soul, or refuse chosen darkness. [Game Theory](/lens/game-theory/) owns zero-sum status and payoff analysis when the active question is incentives. [Attention Capture](/lens/attention-capture-as-capital-extraction/) owns consumer envy when comparison redirects focus into debt, display, work, or platform reaction.

2026-05-25 update: the seed has been promoted only to a compact atlas relation and durable atlas lens point, `lens-point:reflective-spiritual-goods-multiply`, in `website/src/content/docs/lens.md`. It remains below the threshold for a standalone concept page.

This proposal records the missing comparative boundary: when Jiang's sources move from zero-sum material scarcity to a reflective spiritual field, future workers should ask whether the field itself has become the mechanism.

## Source Pressure

The strongest fresh pressure is **2026-05-22**, [Dante's Revolution](/episodes/predictive-history-otyupkhptym/). Jiang reads Dante's terrace of envy as a lesson in the difference between material and spiritual goods. Dante's cited passage says that where sharing material things gives less to each, heavenly love works differently: the more souls say "ours," the greater the good possessed by each (`video:predictive-history-otyupkhptym@transcript:v1#seg-0009`). Jiang glosses this as the difference between the material world, where sharing can be punished because it is a zero-sum game, and the spiritual world of love, generosity, and forgiveness (`video:predictive-history-otyupkhptym@transcript:v1#seg-0010`, `video:predictive-history-otyupkhptym@transcript:v1#seg-0011`).

The lecture then gives the image that makes the mechanism reusable. Jiang imports Indra's net: each soul is a pearl in a universe of pearls, each pearl reflects the universe, and each smile or anger is reflected by the others (`video:predictive-history-otyupkhptym@transcript:v1#seg-0016`). He closes the beat by saying moral action matters because each person carries the fate of the universe in the heart (`video:predictive-history-otyupkhptym@transcript:v1#seg-0017`). The episode read already preserves this as "envy as a zero-sum illusion" and "each soul is a pearl in a universe of pearls."

The later Purgatory passage folds the same image into self-cleansing. A soul cannot enter heaven carrying doubt, hatred, or unjudged sin because it must become "the pearl that reflects the universe" (`video:predictive-history-otyupkhptym@transcript:v1#seg-0052`). That material already lives correctly on [Free Will](/lens/free-will-as-cosmic-burden/) as self-forgiveness and chosen cleansing. The seed does not move it. It notes that the pearl image is doing more than self-esteem or moral purity: the soul's condition matters because it reflects through the whole field.

An earlier **2025-10-15** Secret History source gives the same reflection rule in darkness-test form. Jiang says God and Satan cannot interfere directly because of free will; only in complete darkness can humans fully shine (`video:predictive-history-9murgjjg4aa@transcript:v1#seg-0054`). When one person shines, that light is reflected in everyone else so everyone's light shines as well (`video:predictive-history-9murgjjg4aa@transcript:v1#seg-0055`). The public [Free Will](/lens/free-will-as-cosmic-burden/) page already uses this source for divine nonintervention. The proposed seed marks the cross-source recurrence: one person's light does not stay private.

The **2025-01-07** Paradiso source supplies a neighboring form rather than a new boundary by itself. Beatrice's mirror lesson and Jiang's candle language make divine light humanly relational: love burns brighter when humans love one another. That belongs on Human Heart when the active question is relation and shared inquiry, but it also shows why the reflective-spiritual-goods seed should remain available if later sources keep stressing the field-level multiplication.

## Why This Advances The Lens Map

The public lens already has many pieces of the mechanism, but without a routing note future work can drift in two opposite directions. One error would be to turn every spiritual multiplication passage into Human Heart because the vocabulary is love. The other would be to put every choice-to-shine passage into Free Will and lose the field effect.

The seed prevents both flattenings. It asks what is active:

- Is another concrete person becoming visible, trusted, forgiven, or loved? Use Human Heart.
- Is the soul choosing, cleansing, refusing intervention, or bearing responsibility? Use Free Will.
- Is an analyst naming players, incentives, status, and payoff? Use Game Theory.
- Is envy being captured into comparison, consumption, debt, display, or work? Use Attention Capture.
- Is the source stressing that a spiritual good multiplies by being shared, and that one soul's condition reflects through others? Keep this seed in view.

This advances the lens map by preserving a precise bridge between moral theology and social diagnostics. Jiang's point is not only that kindness is good. It is that the analyst must know which goods behave like apples and which behave like light.

## Boundary Notes

**Not generic optimism**: The seed should preserve the severe side of the claim. Anger, spite, hatred, and sin also reflect. The field can transmit darkness as well as light.

**Not a new love page**: Love-as-recognition, love-as-giving, enemy-recognition, and shared inquiry already have public homes on Human Heart. Use this seed only when reflection or multiplication is the active mechanism.

**Not free will by another name**: Free Will owns the burden of choosing. This seed owns the consequence that the choice is not private because the field reflects it.

**Not game theory unless incentives are named**: Zero-sum language alone is not enough. Game Theory owns zero-sum status and payoff analysis; this seed owns the spiritual contrast where more sharing creates more good.

**Not consumer envy**: When envy is redirected into consumption, display, debt, or platform reaction, Attention Capture is primary. This seed applies when envy is corrected by a metaphysical distinction between material scarcity and spiritual abundance.

## Candidate Lens Points

Do not add these to public pages until promotion is justified. Possible future anchors:

- `spiritual-goods-multiply-by-sharing`  
  Spiritual goods differ from material goods when love, generosity, forgiveness, or kindness grows rather than shrinks as more people participate in it.

- `soul-reflects-through-the-field`  
  The soul becomes part of a reflective field when one person's smile, anger, light, or darkness is mirrored by others and changes the moral condition of the whole.

- `envy-mistakes-light-for-apples`  
  Envy begins when the person treats spiritual good as if it were a divisible material object, making another person's gain feel like loss.

- `perfect-pearl-can-reflect-the-universe`  
  Self-cleansing matters because the soul that reflects the universe must not carry unjudged hatred, doubt, or sin into the field it helps illuminate.

## Promotion Test

Keep this below standalone-page status unless later source pressure makes the reflective field itself the missing reader surface. Promotion beyond the compact atlas relation would become justified if at least two of these are true:

1. Later sources repeat the material-versus-spiritual-goods contrast beyond the Dante envy passage.
2. Existing Human Heart and Free Will pages begin duplicating the same explanation of reflection, multiplication, and non-zero-sum spiritual goods.
3. A future atlas mutation needs a stable relation joining Human Heart, Free Will, Game Theory, Attention Capture, and spiritual cosmology around non-zero-sum moral propagation.
4. Episode reads start carrying strong marks where neither relation, agency, incentives, nor attention capture is primary because the active mechanism is the reflective field.

Until then, treat reflective spiritual goods as routing discipline. The diagnostic question is not "is love mentioned?" but "does the source say this good behaves like light rather than an apple?"
