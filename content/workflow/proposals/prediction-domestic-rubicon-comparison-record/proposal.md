## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns this comparison because the live question is whether a set of dramatic domestic-political forecasts can be kept dated, conditional, and checkable after recurrence. Prediction should not absorb the whole civil-war or police-state mechanism.

Chokepoint owns the war trigger: Iran ground invasion, limited Marine deployment, logistics, manufacturing, mission creep, and the material impossibility of air-war victory stories. Draft pressure belongs to Prediction only when the active object is a dated forecast; it belongs to Chokepoint when it is evidence that the war plan fails the material test.

Mass Society owns the domestic control machinery: ICE normalization, AI surveillance, digital ID, digital currency, data centers, rationing, draft administration, and population-scale pacification. Prediction may cite these as forecast content, but a ledger item should not substitute for a Mass Society mechanism review.

Proximity Games owns the elite/faction payoff when street violence, ICE, National Guard deployment, Wall Street versus Silicon Valley/Palantir, or civil-war provocation is read as the nearest domestic game. The Prediction record should not turn faction analysis into a settled forecast without the dated claim and horizon.

Legitimacy Fiction owns the Caesar/Rubicon and third-term title problem: how crisis can make personal rule feel like order. Prediction owns whether Jiang's dated third-term, emergency-power, or election-route claims later confirmed, missed, or revised.
