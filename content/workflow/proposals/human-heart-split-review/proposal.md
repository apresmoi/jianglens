# Human Heart Split Review

Date: 2026-06-01
Status: concept-scoped split/compression judgment; public prose held
Work type: synthesis after Socrates/Cassandra size alert
Public surface affected later: `website/src/content/docs/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure.md`

## Decision

Keep [Human Heart As Civilizational Measure](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/) as the parent page for now. Do not split a public child page immediately.

The page is about 5,938 words and cites 20 distinct local Jiang source slugs. That puts it at the source-fan-in warning line and just below the ordinary 6,000-word size warning. The page still works as a parent because its current sections answer one diagnostic question: what inner law of love, guilt, pride, trust, curiosity, compassion, recognition, and relation must an outer order respect if it wants human beings to flourish instead of revolt, collapse, or make hell?

The next ordinary Human Heart append should not add another love, forgiveness, crisis-resilience, tragedy, or conversation example without replacing weaker prose, compressing the parent, or reopening this split review. The strongest child candidate is **Love Recognizes The Other**, not a generic Love page, Dante page, Homer page, or Forgiveness page.

## Why This Advances The Lens

This is synthesis work, not historical backfill. Recent Plato work has already been impact- and proposal-heavy, and the latest room signal named overgrown public lens pages rather than a fresh source handoff. The useful move is to govern a page that is near both size and source-fan-in thresholds before it receives another attractive literary or crisis example.

Human Heart is a root lens and will naturally attract many sources because Jiang uses heart, love, empathy, forgiveness, relation, family, courage, and soul as answers to several different pressures. That makes the parent valuable, but it also creates a catchall risk. Without a split/compression rule, the page can absorb material that would be sharper on Free Will, Stories, Poetry, Guide Trap, Attention, Education, Civilization, or a future narrower love-recognition child.

## Recommended Parent Boundary

Keep **Human Heart** as the parent when the active question is:

1. whether a society gives people structure, meaning, purpose, love, creation, learning, growth, curiosity, agency, empathy, and relation;
2. what part of the heart has to be repressed so an order can keep control;
3. how guilt, pride, vulnerability, forgiveness, fallibility, love, and relation make or break civilization;
4. whether a model, institution, school, church, state, market, empire, story, or survival plan can see the human being it claims to serve;
5. whether crisis preparation turns toward private escape or toward spirituality, courage, family, friends, neighbors, children, community, and real conversation.

That parent boundary remains coherent because it names the diagnostic measure rather than one topical branch. The parent should preserve gateway examples from social order, empathy, love, forgiveness, reason, children, and crisis relation, but it should not continue expanding each branch at full section length.

## Child Candidate 1: Love Recognizes The Other

First child candidate:

**Love Recognizes The Other**

Mechanism: love is not possession, obedience, mission, status proof, or private feeling. It recognizes a concrete person and makes that person more visible, alive, free, and capable of wisdom. It can appear as secret language, shared inquiry, divine light in another human being, anti-possession, enemy recognition, forgiveness, or the refusal to turn beloved memory into hatred.

Current source base is already strong:

- **2025-01-07**, `predictive-history-gfqji0qevok`: Beatrice makes love into shared inquiry and experiment; Jiang says love exists between humans rather than in abstract objects or ideas.
- **2025-01-14**, `predictive-history-asbkjezwijm`: true love wants what is best for the person and respects who the beloved is.
- **2025-03-25**, `predictive-history-v6cdqjnrvlk`: intellect must mature in the flame of love; God is the light in humans that lets them love others.
- **2025-05-20**, `predictive-history-0ralqcpwuh0`: Anna Karenina gives the anti-possession rule; love is giving and requires trust.
- **2025-11-06**, `predictive-history-4ehovunrsrw`: Homer makes forgiveness an inward battlefield in which Priam makes Achilles see a father and a son.
- **2026-03-11**, `predictive-history-as-nfpspmu8`: Odysseus and Penelope's secret signs and bed test make recognition concrete and falsifiable.
- **2026-03-18**, `predictive-history-ebwtrvjz1dw`: anti-Homer inversion makes enemy-recognition and mercy look weak.
- **2026-03-25**, `predictive-history-yxtrlvfirt8`: the Aeneid tests love against mission, Dido, Pallas, Turnus, vengeance, and imperial destiny.

This is the strongest future child because the public parent already has one large anchor, `heart-love-recognizes-person`, carrying many literary and theological sources. If the next Human Heart mutation would add another Dante, Homer, Virgil, Anna, Odysseus, Penelope, Beatrice, enemy-recognition, or love/forgiveness source, a child extraction or parent compression should be considered before appending.

If extracted, preserve the stable ID `heart-love-recognizes-person` only if the child keeps the same meaning. The parent should retain a compact gateway paragraph that routes love-as-recognition to the child while keeping Human Heart as the wider civilizational measure.

## What Would Make A Split Public-Ready

Extract **Love Recognizes The Other** only if at least one condition is met:

- the next public Human Heart edit would otherwise add another Dante, Homer, Virgil, Anna, Odysseus/Penelope, Beatrice, enemy-recognition, anti-possession, or love/forgiveness section;
- the parent rises above about 6,000 words or 20 authored evidence sources and the love-recognition branch is still the easiest coherent extraction;
- episode-to-lens provenance needs a narrower hover target than `heart-love-recognizes-person`;
- a new source repeats the same person-recognition mechanism strongly enough that appending it would blur the parent boundary.

Extract **Heart, Crisis, And Relational Survival** only if future crisis-preparation sources make the safe-place relation branch too large for the parent or if `heart-safe-place-is-relation` becomes the dominant episode-link target for non-literary Human Heart material.
