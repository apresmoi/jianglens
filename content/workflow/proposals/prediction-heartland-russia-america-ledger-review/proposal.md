The December 9, 2025 Canadian Prepper interview `interview-lhssc-w0bk4` is the earlier route-control formulation. Jiang says America and Britain are naval powers that benefit from Eurasian conflict because sea routes remain the safest way to move energy (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0022`). This is not yet the twenty-year Russia-America forecast. It supplies the mechanism: Anglo-American naval power depends on preventing a land-linked Eurasian economic bloc.

### April 13 Forecast-Family Layer

The April 13, 2026 source supplies the dated Prediction pressure. Jiang says the Iran war must continue because peace would let Russia, Iran, and China form a trade alliance that counters American control of the seas and extends into Europe and Africa (`video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0041`). ## Ledger Status
