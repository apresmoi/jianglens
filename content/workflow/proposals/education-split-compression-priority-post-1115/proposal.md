## Boundary Decision

Keep **Education As A Soul Game** whole for now. Its parent mechanism remains:
school forms a person by training trust, authority, attention, creativity,
compliance, testing, institutional legibility, national memory, selfhood, and
rival formation.

Keep **Tests, Trust, And Institutional Legibility** held as the strongest future
Education child candidate, not a public page now. The parent still carries the
two-sided mechanism compactly:

- Game Theory #3 shows that delay, resilience, self-reflection, and
  self-control become rational only when a child can trust adults, authority,
  and the surrounding world.
- The 2014 Sinica education interview shows the institutional reverse:
  families trust a visible test when they do not trust teachers, committees,
  qualitative measures, civil society, administrators, or captured institutions.

Keep neighboring routes stable:

- **Meritocracy Converts Wounds Into Credentials** owns elite-school
  wound-to-credential conversion, admissions discretion, conditional parenting,
  credential hunger, hollow achievement, and best-student fragility.
- **Living School For Psychohistory** owns Jiang's positive institution-building
  dream: Great Books, historical context, psychohistory, model correction,
  public classroom archive, future school-building, and site/community
  formation.
- **The Dead World And The Cave** owns school as one surface of materialist
  false reality, dead ontology, and escape practice.
- **Attention Capture As Capital Extraction** owns education only when the
  active mechanism is captured focus, managed consciousness, distraction,
  status discipline, or attention converted into value.
- **The Borderland Engine** owns energy, openness, adaptation, worldly
  resilience, and margin-to-center force when school examples are functioning as
  borderland mechanisms.

## Reopen Conditions

Reopen an Education public compression or child split only if one of these
conditions becomes true:

- the authored Education page crosses about 6,000 raw Markdown words or 20
  distinct local video slugs after a proposed public edit;
- clustered episode links make `education-trust-makes-delay-rational` and
  `test-score-replaces-institutional-trust` confusing as parent hovers;
- new corpus-impact records add multiple dated sources specifically thickening
  exams, institutional distrust, selection rituals, or test-legibility mechanics
  after Meritocracy and Living School branches are routed away;
- a replacement-compression draft cannot keep the trust/test mechanism readable
  inside the parent;
- a future public PR would otherwise append another PISA, Gaokao, marshmallow,
  credential, classroom, creativity, compliance, or Viking-school example
  without improving the reader's diagnostic map.

Until then, do not create standalone Tests, Trust, Institutional Legibility,
Gaokao, PISA, Marshmallow Test, Test Culture, Creativity School,
Anti-Creative Classroom, Compliance School, Viking School, Great Books School,
or Education Reform pages from the 33-page generated-pressure cue alone.
