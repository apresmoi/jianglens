## Recommended Parent Boundary

Keep **Education As A Soul Game** as the parent for formation through rewarded educational games:

1. an institution names learning, creativity, citizenship, wellbeing, or human development as the mission;
2. the actual reward game trains trust, obedience, status ambition, compliance, attention habits, measurable legibility, or rival self-formation;
3. the student adapts until the rewarded game helps decide what the student can trust, fear, desire, notice, and become;
4. rival education tries to recover attention, world contact, historical imagination, bodily competence, craft, love of learning, and the belief that action matters.

The parent should become a shorter gateway chapter. It should preserve all stable lens-point IDs unless a public split proves one ID's meaning changes. It should route readers to children only when a child carries a reusable mechanism more clearly than a parent section.
