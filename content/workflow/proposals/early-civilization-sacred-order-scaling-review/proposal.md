## Decision

Do not create a new public early-civilization page, Sacred Order child, Common Sacrifice child, Bronze Age Collapse child, or Elite Overproduction As Scaling Trap child now.

The recent intake cluster is real synthesis pressure, not mere accounting. It says Jiang's early-civilization arc keeps returning to one broad problem: a people can become coherent only when an invisible order, material form, social access pattern, and scaling discipline reinforce each other. But that problem is already publicly distributed across two strong homes:

- [Civilization As Inner Order](/lens/civilization-as-inner-order/) owns the whole human arrangement: religion, food, sex, property, violence, memory, city form, trade, status, debt, collapse, and human type.
- [Sacred Machines](/lens/sacred-machines/) owns the material or staged form that turns invisible order into public infrastructure: cave, ritual center, temple, pyramid, funeral, city-facing sacred project, or modern replacement religion.

Current page-size pressure does not force extraction. Direct checks on this branch measured about 3,461 words for `civilization-as-inner-order.md`, about 2,897 words for `sacred-machines.md`, and 12 distinct local video slugs across the two pages. Both pages are below ordinary split thresholds and already route the recent sources with stable public anchors.

This run is synthesis rather than another compact intake because the last three Plato PRs were compact corpus-impact passes. Audit still shows 61 historical gaps, but no fresh merged source is waiting for Plato: Virgil's `predictive-history-7ag3emdvkt0` is still an episode PR under Aristotle QA. The useful move is therefore to consolidate the accumulated early-civilization pressure and name promotion tests before the next backfill source adds more seed names.

## Boundary Rules

**Civilization owns the whole order.** Use Civilization when the source joins religion, economy, settlement, sex, property, violence, memory, city form, trade, debt, and human type into one social world.

**Sacred Machines owns the interface form.** Use Sacred Machines when a cave, temple, ritual center, pyramid, funeral, public project, or modern replacement machine makes invisible order publicly inhabitable.

**Power owns conversion into command.** Use Power when money, capital, numbers, priestly value, or sacred abstraction becomes operating reality, not when the source is only about shared imagination.

**Debt owns obligation.** Use Debt when compound interest, debt slavery, hostage assets, or obligation structure governs the mechanism. Do not send every Bronze Age rent passage there if the active problem is civilizational resilience.

**Chokepoint owns route density.** Use Chokepoint when Troy, tin, Gulf routes, Oxus links, Hormuz-like access, tolls, or trade corridors become the mechanism. Keep Civilization primary when routes matter because they form a whole urban or elite order.

**Proximity Games owns modern factional proximity.** Use Proximity Games when elite overproduction becomes domestic factional conflict, street surfaces, civil-war payoff, or nearest-board incentives. Keep Bronze Age elite overproduction on Civilization unless a later synthesis proves a civilizational-scale child.

**Religion As Administrative Filter owns access codes.** Use it for Brahmin gatekeeping, priestly operator selection, temple-office control, or sacrament-like access. Do not route early religion-before-economics there unless office selection or access monopoly is active.
