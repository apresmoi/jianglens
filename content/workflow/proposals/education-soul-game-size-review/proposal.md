## Recommended Parent Boundary

Keep **Education As A Soul Game** as the parent for the formation mechanism:

1. education names an official mission such as learning, creativity, citizenship, wellbeing, or human development;
2. the actual institution rewards a different game: grades, tests, parental face, teacher compliance, institutional calm, elite selection, national memory, status, brand, or numb obedience;
3. students adapt to the rewarded game until the game forms what they trust, fear, desire, notice, and believe they can become;
4. rival education tries to recover attention, trust, imagination, worldly competence, exploration, and the belief that the student's will can matter.

The parent should remain an entry point that shows why school is not just a policy topic. It is one of Jiang's central machines for soul formation.
