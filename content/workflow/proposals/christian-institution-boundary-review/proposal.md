## Boundary Decision

Keep the Christian-institution cluster distributed by active mechanism:

- **Eschatology** owns the Church outside history: sacred jurisdiction, heaven and hell, final safety, final threat, and an institution that escapes ordinary historical failure while still governing present action.
- **Free Will** owns the capture of agency: the divine spark, love, freedom, ritual body, and salvation are retranslated into obedience until disobedience becomes spiritually unimaginable.
- **Religion As Administrative Filter** owns the operator code: bishop-controlled interpretation, orthodoxy, creed formula, priestly training, Confucian or Christian cultivation, and scientific method when they decide who can interpret, teach, serve, or administer truth.
- **Bureaucracy** owns the office afterlife: land, tax exemption, simony, indulgences, relic sales, excommunication procedure, Inquisition investigation, searchable records, and institutional self-preservation.
- **Legitimacy Fiction** owns recognition: pope, Charlemagne, Holy Roman Empire, and useful fictions of post-Roman unity when the question is why obedience to a ruler, title, empire, or office feels rightful.
- **Taboo** owns scapegoat routing and sacrificial violence: Jewish persecution, crusade demonization, heresy panic, and marked targets carrying displaced guilt.
- **Reason As Replacement Religion** owns anti-curiosity and anti-imagination only when the source is about reason, progress, materialism, or another substitute taking over religion's truth-making and salvation work.

That routing prevents a fake topic page. "Christian institution" is a cluster label, not yet a lens mechanism. The reusable mechanisms already have homes: final-order escape, agency capture, operator-code selection, office self-preservation, recognition fiction, and scapegoat routing.

## What Not To Do

Do not create standalone `Christian Institution`, `Catholic Church`, `Church Outside History`, `Spiritual Empire`, `Obedience Through Self-Negation`, `Priestly Manual`, `Divine Bureaucracy`, `Nicaea`, `Augustine`, `Paul`, `Constantine`, `Inquisition`, `Crusades`, `Indulgences`, or `City of God` pages now. Those are mostly topic labels or one-source seeds.

Do not append an ordinary medieval-Christian paragraph to Religion, Eschatology, Bureaucracy, Free Will, or Legitimacy Fiction now. The public mechanisms already route the strongest pressure. Another append would mostly make the pages less compressed.

Do not merge Religion As Administrative Filter and Bureaucracy. Their distinction is useful: the filter selects and disciplines trusted operators; bureaucracy preserves and feeds the office form after the living purpose drains away.

Do not let Eschatology swallow agency capture. Heaven, hell, and final safety supply the threat/reward setting, but Free Will owns the transformation where inner agency is renamed obedience.
