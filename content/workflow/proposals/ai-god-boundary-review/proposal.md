## Decision

Keep **AI As Control Surface** as the current public parent for Jiang's AI material. Do not create a standalone **AI God** page yet, and do not fold every AI-God, Holy Empire, techno-Marxism, loneliness, database, and redundancy passage into Eschatology.

The useful boundary is:

- **AI As Control Surface** owns AI as the interface through which command reaches the person: answer, guide, companion, body sensor, payment gate, prediction system, or sacred interface.
- **Eschatology As Script** owns AI only when the active mechanism is sacred final order: Antichrist role, holy empire, one-world government appearing as Jesus, AI God, or obedience recoded as salvation.
- **Mass Society As Political Constraint** owns AI when the mechanism is population administration: digital ID, digital currency, data centers, surveillance, behavior prediction, microchips, policing, scarcity allocation, or the digital permission grid.
- **Attention Capture As Capital Extraction** owns AI when the mechanism is captured consciousness: companion dependency, loneliness monetization, reading/imagination displacement, intimate attention command, or the dollar-AI contest for human focus.
- **Power As Alchemy** owns AI when the mechanism is value conversion: money losing command, the dollar order weakening, debt or scarcity being made real, and AI appearing as the next abstraction that can command labor, belief, and obedience.
- **Free Will** and **Human Heart** own the counterpressure when the source's question is agency, love, relation, family, community, imagination, or the refusal to save humans by eliminating choice.

This keeps the new public AI page useful without letting it become a second atlas. AI As Control Surface should remain the parent interface lens; neighboring pages keep the mechanisms they already own.

The public surface should instead be:

1. Keep [AI As Control Surface](/lens/ai-as-control-surface/) as the compact parent page.
2. Use the existing Eschatology section and `eschatology-holy-empire-removes-intention` lens point for sacred obedience and AI God role-script passages.
3. Use Mass Society's `mass-society-digital-permission-grid`, `mass-society-surveillance-bargain-grid`, and `mass-society-control-infrastructure` anchors when the source is database, ID, currency, chips, state analytics, policing, or scarcity allocation.
4. Use Attention Capture's AI companion and dollar-AI consciousness-war anchors when the source is loneliness, dependency, imagination displacement, and attention direction.
5. Record the AI-God glossary candidate only after a promotion review decides whether a glossary entry would clarify the parent page or create duplicate navigation.

The likely next public mutation is a small AI As Control Surface deepening, not an Eschatology expansion. If done, it should add only the May 26 database-plus-religion bridge and the May 28 redundancy test, with boundary links to Eschatology, Mass Society, Attention, Power, Human Heart, and Free Will. Do not add a large new sacred-AI section to Eschatology unless the page is first compressed or split.

## Boundary Tests

Use **AI As Control Surface** when the sentence answers: what interface does AI become, and how does command reach the user through it?

Use **Eschatology** when the sentence answers: what end-times role, sacred final order, Antichrist function, Jesus mask, holy empire, or salvation-through-obedience story is being activated?

Use **Mass Society** when the sentence answers: what population, body, payment, identity, data, work, food, police, or mobility system is being administered?

Use **Attention Capture** when the sentence answers: whose focus, imagination, loneliness, dependency, or consciousness is being captured, and how does the AI companion redirect action?

Use **Power As Alchemy** when the sentence answers: what abstraction is being made real enough to replace money, command labor, store value, or organize belief?

Use **Free Will** or **Human Heart** when the sentence answers: what human choice, love, relation, imagination, community, or refusal survives the control surface?
