The likely pressure is broader link/backlink/index surface around Game Theory, including episode links, generated references, or source-adjacent references. That still matters: Game Theory attracts many neighboring claims because it is a method lens. But the correct response is stricter routing, not another child page without a mature boundary.

This is synthesis work, not historical backfill. That parent role is still coherent. The page is large but not currently above the ordinary 6,000-word warning line, and the first child extraction already reduced the densest domestic-faction branch.

## Boundary Decision

Game Theory should remain the parent method when the active question is:

- who the real player is;
- whether the visible actor is carrying another actor's incentives;
- whether the declared prize is not the actual reward;
- whether the nearest family, faction, party, school, bureaucracy, audience, or spiritual game governs the visible action;
- whether a material, financial, religious, status, or narrative model has made the players too simple.

Move or route material away from Game Theory when the active mechanism is stronger elsewhere:

- [Proximity Games And Elite Overproduction](/lens/proximity-games-and-elite-overproduction/) owns domestic-faction proximity, elite overproduction, fifth-generation cohesion attacks, and street-level surfaces of elite conflict.
