This run answers Socrates' direct request for a bounded judgment rather than historical source backfill. The default corpus-impact audit reports no fresh missing corpus-impact item in the budget window, and historical backfill remains paused.

## Boundary Rules Before More Public Edits

Keep **How Poetry Creates Civilization** as the parent when the active mechanism is:

1. poetic language turns private perception into a shared world;
2. rhythm, memory, performance, image, metaphor, or character makes that world portable;
3. poetic form creates civilization-scale access to meaning, morality, and metaphysics;
4. the same power can democratize, infect, privatize, imperialize, educate, or trap the soul;
5. a source is needed to explain why poetry itself is infrastructure.

Route narrower pressure outward:

- [Dante As Poetic Access And Reception](/lens/dante-as-poetic-access-and-reception/) owns Dante-specific access, invasion, delayed reception, prophecy-as-present-critique, and reader transformation.
- [Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion](/lens/imperial-poetry-and-trojan-horse-inversion/) owns the Roman/Aeneid inversion branch.
- [The Guide Who Becomes A Trap](/lens/the-guide-who-becomes-a-trap/) owns Virgil when guidance, false rescue, guide dependence, or Roman loyalty is the active mechanism.
- [Free Will As Cosmic Burden](/lens/free-will-as-cosmic-burden/) owns ascent, practice, returned emotional time, self-authored station, and chosen redemption when agency is primary.
- [Human Heart As Civilizational Measure](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/) and [Love Recognizes The Other](/lens/love-recognizes-the-other/) own forgiveness, concrete recognition, love, desire, and relational repair.
- [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) owns narrative governance when story-control, not poetic medium, is the active object.
- [Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy](/lens/prediction-as-falsifiable-prophecy/) owns forecast object, horizon, revision, status, and ledger-readiness discipline.

## Reopen Conditions

Reopen Poetry split review only if at least one of these becomes true:

- the parent rises above about 6,000 authored words again after a compression attempt;
- the parent reaches about 20 distinct authored local video slugs;
- generated backlinks or episode hovers cluster around a remaining parent anchor so broadly that readers need a narrower public hover surface;
- four or more dated sources create a new mechanism not already held by the Dante child, Imperial Poetry child, Stories, Guide Trap, Free Will, Human Heart, Love Recognizes, or Prediction;
- compression cannot preserve reader usefulness in `Poetry As A Gateway Into The Soul`, `The Poem As Portal`, or `Shakespeare And Language As Empire`.

Until one of those conditions holds, Poetry should stay whole, replacement/compression-first, and governed by mechanism rather than author/topic splits.
