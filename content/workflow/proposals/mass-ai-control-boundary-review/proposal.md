## Decision

Do not append another ordinary AI-surveillance, federal-voter-list, database, or imagination-control section to **Mass Society As Political Constraint**, **AI As Control Surface**, or **Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy** yet.

The public surface already carries the main April-May pressure:

- Mass Society is about 5,750 words and now has `mass-society-domestic-war-control-grid`, `mass-society-digital-permission-grid`, and `mass-society-surveillance-bargain-grid`.
- AI As Control Surface has the compact late-May `ai-omniscience-religion-obedience` point for database omniscience plus religious imagination.
- Prediction has workflow records for the March 24 emergency-powers chain, late Iran / World War III family, and Hasan closing forecast split.

The useful next move is routing discipline. Treat the April-May database/control cluster as a shared mechanism family with distinct homes:

- **Mass Society** owns population administration: draft, voter lists, federal force, ICE, digital ID, digital currency, data centers, databases, rationing, work, mobility, and policing when the question is how a large population is made legible and governable.
- **AI As Control Surface** owns the interface layer: AI as answer, companion, prediction surface, body interface, sacred voice, imagination governor, or obedience channel through which command reaches the user.
- **Prediction** owns dated claims, clocks, public-event checks, misses, revisions, and ledger readiness. It should not absorb the control apparatus except as a neighboring mechanism.
- **Eschatology** owns AI God, Antichrist, Jesus-mask, holy empire, Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, and obedience-as-salvation role language.
- **Attention Capture** owns loneliness, companion dependency, reading/imagination displacement, and dollar-versus-AI command over consciousness.
- **Platform Infrastructure** owns the public-face/deeper-pipe reversal when the visible AI product, app, or company distracts from data centers, cables, identity systems, payment rails, or state-subsidized technical infrastructure.

This proposal is synthesis, not impact intake. The source impacts already exist for the main pressure points. The purpose is to prevent Prediction ledger work from smuggling in a vague "AI police state" mechanism and to prevent Mass Society or AI Control from becoming catchalls.
