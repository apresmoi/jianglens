## Boundary Decision

Keep **Poetry** as the home for Dante's access method: imagination, paradox, ambiguity, compressed language, unseen connection, faith as imaginative access, and public Dante teaching. The Poetry page already carries the June 15 Paradiso method and existing Dante democratic-access anchors. June 17 strengthens those anchors but does not require a new Poetry child or another additive section.

Keep **Free Will** as the home for the obedience reversal: the soul is not saved by delegated compliance, and love cannot be made into a command without damaging the freedom that makes love real. June 17 belongs near `free-will-obedience-renamed-freedom` only if a future public edit compresses the Paul/Augustine institutional inversion against Dante's positive anti-obedience layer.

Keep **Human Heart** as the home for forgiveness, promise, fallibility, and imaginative entry into another person's position. June 16 is already public through `heart-forgiveness-requires-fallibility`; June 17 supplies the sequel pressure that forgiveness needs imagination, not only rule revision. This is the strongest next public candidate, but it should be replacement/compression rather than append.

Keep **Dead World** as the home for dead ontology and escape practice. June 15-16 already strengthened the page with school-logic escape, memory-access, and eternity-versus-infinity pressure. June 17 does not add enough new Dead World pressure to outrank the Human Heart / Free Will boundary.

Keep **Living School** as bounded classroom-method evidence only. The June 17 seminar shows student objection, exact text, and teaching Dante as love, but it is not primarily an institution-building source. Use it later only if a public classroom-method compression compares it with the June 13 school exit-plan and older Predictive History classroom origin sources.

Keep **Bureaucracy** and **Religion As Administrative Filter** as held routing homes for Francis/Dominic institutional decay. One Dante canto can show the mechanism, but a public institution-decay child would need comparison across religious orders, schools, revolutionary movements, or agent organizations.
