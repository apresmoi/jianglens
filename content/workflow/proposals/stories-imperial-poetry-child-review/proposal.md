# Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion Child Review

Status: proposal for public lens boundary decision

## Decision

Do not extract **Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion** as a standalone public child page yet. Keep `stories-trojan-horse-inverts-inherited-world` and `stories-aeneid-trains-imperial-robot` on [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) for now, while treating the cluster as a governed split candidate if the parent needs another compression pass.

The mechanism is strong and reusable: when power cannot erase a rival inherited world, it enters that world through beloved forms, memorized poetry, education, and beauty, then reverses the moral charge until love, mercy, clever speech, Greek freedom, and human hesitation feel like danger while obedience to empire feels like piety. But the cluster is not cleanly a Stories-only child. It is already shared across [How Poetry Creates Civilization](/lens/how-poetry-creates-civilization/) and [The Guide Who Becomes A Trap](/lens/the-guide-who-becomes-a-trap/), with [Legitimacy Fiction](/lens/legitimacy-fiction/) owning the Augustan authorization layer.

The next public move should not be a new child page. It should be either a compact parent compression that preserves these two anchors, or a later public extraction only if the extraction can reduce duplication with Poetry and Guide Trap rather than create a third page rehearsing the same Virgil/Aeneid material.

## Why This Advances The Lens

This is concept-governance synthesis after the Stories split review, the Screen-World extraction, the Screen-World source-fan-in strengthening, and the Story Virus boundary proposal. The live corpus-impact audit still shows many historical gaps, including three of the four main Aeneid sources here, but the room has no fresh ready source from Virgil and no newly merged high-pressure source lacking first impact. Cassandra's latest room signal names oversized public pages as a current lens-governance problem. This proposal answers that signal without turning historical missing-impact count into the work queue.

The pressure is real because [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) remains a parent-scale page with many source refs and multiple child-like anchors. The question is not whether this cluster matters. It clearly does. The question is whether a separate public child now would clarify the atlas. At the moment it would mostly duplicate public Poetry and Guide Trap prose unless the split is paired with a careful redistribution of anchors and page responsibilities.

## Current Source Pressure

The public parent page already carries two durable anchors:

- `stories-trojan-horse-inverts-inherited-world`: a story becomes Trojan-horse control when power enters an inherited world through familiar beauty, trust, or education and reverses that world's moral charge.
- `stories-aeneid-trains-imperial-robot`: a story makes an imperial robot when repeated inhabitation teaches the reader to discard love, pity, decency, and human hesitation as weakness until obedience to mission feels like inner piety.

The source base has four dated layers:

- **2024-12-05**, `predictive-history-rkpgrrugjnk`: the Aeneid appears as a comparative kingship device. Jiang says Greek culture is treated as a Trojan horse that will corrupt and destroy Rome, while David's Bible is being used to solve legitimacy, identity, and differentiation.
- **2026-03-18**, `predictive-history-ebwtrvjz1dw`: the Great Books anti-Homer lecture gives the core inversion. Rome has conquered Greece physically but fears Greek spiritual conquest, so the Aeneid corrupts Homer from inside; Aeneas's fall-of-Troy story recodes Greek theater, philosophy, rhetoric, and love as deceptive or evil.
- **2026-03-25**, `predictive-history-yxtrlvfirt8`: the Poetry of Empire lecture turns inversion into inner training. Virgil makes piety, obedience to gods and father, and imperial mission compete with love; at Turnus's plea, Aeneas supplies the command himself and becomes the perfect soldier by abandoning pity, emotion, and soul.
- **2026-04-08**, `predictive-history-6m1z-v3wgok`: Dante's La Commedia lecture shows the afterlife of the Virgil-world. Poetry behaves like a virus that enters memory and remakes perception; Virgil's Aeneid creates emotions of piety, obedience, hatred, empire, and love as disease that allow hell itself to exist; Dante must trust and then defeat Virgil's world.

This is enough for durable anchors and enough to justify a future split review. It is not yet enough to prove that a new public page would be cleaner than strengthening or compressing the existing Stories, Poetry, and Guide Trap distribution.

## Recommended Boundary

Keep **How Stories Control Reality** as the parent mechanism: narrative sets the world people act inside by capturing attention, projecting a scene, internalizing memory, training desire, coordinating action, inverting rival worlds, and meeting material tests.

Treat a future **Imperial Poetry And Trojan-Horse Inversion** child as narrower:

1. a rival world is too embedded in memory or education to be destroyed directly;
2. power enters through the rival world's own forms, scenes, beauty, or trusted prestige;
3. the story reverses the moral charge of the inherited world, so old virtues feel dangerous;
4. repeated memorization or inhabitation trains the person to desire obedience, mission, or empire as inner piety;
5. a later counter-poem or counter-memory has to release the reader from the installed world.

That child should not become a general Aeneid page, a literary plot summary, or a broad Poetry page. Its object would be the conversion path from inherited poetic world to imperial obedience.

## Boundary With Neighboring Pages

- [How Poetry Creates Civilization](/lens/how-poetry-creates-civilization/) owns poetry as civilizational medium: language, rhythm, memory, performance, metaphysical access, shared world, moral formation, and literature as a public or private access regime. It should keep `poetry-invades-and-remakes-reader` because the active mechanism there is poetry entering memory and becoming a universe inside the reader.
- [The Guide Who Becomes A Trap](/lens/the-guide-who-becomes-a-trap/) owns Virgil as necessary mediation and false rescue: a guide who can orient the soul while carrying the world the soul must escape. It should keep Virgil's speech, limbo, Minos, Dido, Statius, Beatrice, and release diagnostics.
- [Legitimacy Fiction](/lens/legitimacy-fiction/) owns imperial authorization: descent, prophecy, divine plan, founding violence, names, offices, and inherited political right. The Aeneid belongs there when it makes Augustus or Rome legitimate.
- [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) owns the inversion mechanism itself: narrative enters an already-beloved world and changes what the reader feels love, mercy, danger, piety, and obedience mean.
- [Human Heart As Civilizational Measure](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/) and any future love proposal own the positive relational test. The Aeneid material can supply a negative case, but the child should not become a general love page.

## What Would Make It Public-Ready

Extract the child only when at least one of these conditions is met:

- the Stories parent needs another compression pass and moving both Aeneid anchors materially improves reader navigation;
- corpus-impact intake for `predictive-history-rkpgrrugjnk`, `predictive-history-ebwtrvjz1dw`, and `predictive-history-yxtrlvfirt8` confirms this as a distinct downstream pressure rather than just Poetry/Guide Trap support;
- a public redistribution plan says which Aeneid sections stay on Stories, which stay on Poetry, and which stay on Guide Trap, so the child removes duplication rather than creating it;
- episode-to-lens provenance needs a narrower hover than the current Stories anchors can provide.

If extracted, preserve `stories-trojan-horse-inverts-inherited-world` and `stories-aeneid-trains-imperial-robot` unless the child rewrites their meaning. They are already specific, source-grounded, and linked from episode read JSON. A move would be a public lens mutation requiring Dante review and generated-link validation.

## Proposed Next Mutation

Do not publish an Imperial Poetry child as the next public mutation. The next useful Stories-family work should be one of:

1. a public parent compression if another source would otherwise append to How Stories Control Reality;
2. a targeted corpus-impact intake for one of the three missing Aeneid sources, starting with `predictive-history-yxtrlvfirt8` if the immediate aim is to test the imperial-robot anchor;
3. a concept-scoped redistribution proposal for Stories, Poetry, and Guide Trap if repeated Aeneid/Dante material continues to appear in all three pages.

This proposal intentionally does not choose another historical corpus-impact intake. No fresh source is waiting for first-pass impact, the latest room signal is split-review governance, and the current need is to prevent a real cluster from becoming a duplicative public page.
