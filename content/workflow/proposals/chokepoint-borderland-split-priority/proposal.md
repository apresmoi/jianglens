## Decision

Split-review **Chokepoint Empire And No-Exit War** before **The Borderland Engine**.

Do not immediately split both pages, and do not split Borderland first. Chokepoint is the more urgent public-lens constraint because the authored page is already at the source-count warning line: about 5,646 words, 205 local evidence refs, 20 distinct local evidence source slugs, and 11 durable lens-point anchors. It is still just below the ordinary word-count warning line, but it is now exactly the kind of page where another ordinary Iran, Hormuz, Malacca, Gulf, draft, or World War III append would turn a useful child concept into a topic archive.

Borderland is a real size-watch page, but it is not the first split target. The authored page is about 3,972 words, with 172 local evidence refs, 14 distinct local evidence source slugs, and 10 durable lens-point anchors. Its current breadth is mostly doing source-trail work for one coherent diagnostic: marginal pressure becomes historical motion only when it creates energy, openness, and cohesion. The page should be compressed or reorganized later if new material adds ordinary cases, but it does not yet need the first split.

## Why Chokepoint First

The earlier 2026-06-03 Chokepoint split review was right to hold the page together at about 5.3k words and 18 distinct sources. The page has now crossed the source-count warning line while remaining close to the word-count line. That changes the next move: another compression or child-boundary decision is now more urgent than more Chokepoint intake or another ordinary source section.

Chokepoint is also the higher-risk blur surface. It is adjacent to Strategy, Prediction, Power As Alchemy, Eschatology, Imperial Retrenchment, Mass Society, and Stories. One loose append can pull forecast audit, sacred role scripts, petrodollar belief, resource-fortress retreat, and population-control infrastructure back into the access-control page. A split-review now protects the atlas.

Borderland has wide fan-in, but the public page is still conceptually compact. Its sections are different cases and caveats of the same diagnostic:

- stable centers become stagnant;
- margins learn through contact;
- poverty is not magic;
- immoral easy money drains motion;
- cases test the same energy/openness/cohesion mechanism;
- victory can turn the margin into the next center.

That structure is broad but not yet overgrown. The next Borderland edit should be a compression pass if new public evidence is needed, especially around Rome or modern energy/openness/cohesion scorecards. It should not be the first split unless a later source proves one child mechanism is recurring independently of the parent.

For the next public Chokepoint PR, test one of two shapes:

1. **Compression without split:** Keep `chokepoint-empire-and-no-exit-war.md` as one page, but replace repetitive March-April chronology and weaker Iran-war prose with tighter gateway language. Preserve all stable anchor IDs and do not add new ordinary examples.
2. **No-Exit child extraction:** Create a child page only for no-exit war and escalation ladder, preserving anchor IDs if moved. Keep the parent as the access-control and systems-pivot page.

Do not create standalone pages for Iran War, Hormuz, Kharg Island, World War III, Energy Platform, Ground Invasion, Draft, or Chokepoint Map from this pressure alone. Those would be topic pages rather than reusable mechanisms.

## Boundary Rules

Use **Chokepoint** when the active mechanism is a material gate or access-control surface: strait, canal, route, base network, blockade, port, island chain, food or oil passage, naval attrition, client-state exposure, or the exit cost created by protection credibility.

Use **No-Exit War And Escalation Ladder** only if the draft can teach a narrower mechanism: a war becomes hard to stop because stopping audits the order that launched it, and each actor's next move becomes locally necessary.

Use **Prediction** for dated claims, horizons, public-event checks, miss/revision status, draft or emergency forecast families, and ledger readiness.

Use **Power As Alchemy** when the petrodollar, Treasury demand, stablecoins, reserve assets, or AI bubble finance are the active mechanism by which money becomes lived compulsion.

Use **Eschatology** when Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, Gog and Magog, Pax Judaica, Antichrist, AI God, martyrdom, or sacred acceleration define the role script.

Use **Imperial Retrenchment** when the mechanism is fortress America, resource/weapon/financing business model, ally burden transfer, or regional conflict as a late-imperial business model.

Use **Mass Society** when draft, ICE, voter lists, digital ID, digital currency, rationing, AI surveillance, or data centers are population-administration machinery rather than the access-control board itself.

Use **Borderland** when the active mechanism is pressure becoming energy, openness, cohesion, adaptation, learning, sacrifice, or margin-to-center reversal.
