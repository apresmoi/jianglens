## Decision

Do not promote "Venezuela" as one Prediction ledger item.

The December 2025 and January 2026 source trail names at least six different forecast objects under the same theater: calibrated bargaining, limited strike or embargo pressure, full invasion, regional demonstration, accident or outside-force escalation, and cross-theater systems fire. A later public event can confirm one of those surfaces while leaving the others unconfirmed or revised.

This is synthesis, not intake. The audit still shows a large historical impact backlog, but recent Plato work has already built the needed cluster: `interview-pnt-6v5m1wa`, `interview-p5gg9xxbqze`, `interview-l9heow1syg0`, and `interview-orycs0r2tpg` have impact records or proposal trails that repeatedly point to a narrow Venezuela review. This proposal turns that accumulated pressure into a ledger gate rather than adding another compact source silo.

## Source Trail

### 2025-12-13: Calibrated Bargaining Before Occupation

In `interview-pnt-6v5m1wa`, Jiang starts with game theory: ask what Trump wants, how Maduro responds, and which strategies each can sustain. Trump wants oil, anti-drug legitimacy, a harder Monroe Doctrine line, and a privatized Venezuela, but Jiang says the optimal strategy is not clean occupation. He names limited strikes, land-strike threats, and embargo as pressure for a treaty or settlement, because Venezuela is hard to occupy and outside actors could treat invasion as their own survival problem (`video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0004`, `video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0006`, `video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0007`).

That source also records a live revision. After the oil-tanker seizure, Jiang says he had thought Trump was bluffing but now treats Venezuela as a test case for South American permission politics, food and oil access, and China/Russia/Iran activity in the hemisphere (`video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0024`, `video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0025`).

Ledger gate: this source supports a candidate for **calibrated coercion toward settlement**, not a simple full-invasion forecast.

### 2025-12-19: Local War As Systems Fire

In `interview-p5gg9xxbqze`, Jiang widens the theater. Venezuela is no longer only Trump/Maduro bargaining; it becomes one ignition point in a multi-front chain where Venezuela, Hormuz, Odessa, Europe, East Asia, oil, shipping, food, manpower, and alliance credibility can answer each other (`video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0030`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0032`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0035`, `video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0036`).

This is not a replacement for the December 13 bargaining claim. It is a different object: the risk that a supposedly limited Venezuela move teaches neighboring states that they are next or activates other theaters through material interdependence.

Ledger gate: this source supports a candidate for **limited operation becoming systems fire**, not proof that full invasion is likely.

### 2025-12-21: Show-Force, Face, And Accident Risk

In `interview-l9heow1syg0`, Jiang says both sides are playing chicken and that neither side benefits from all-out war. The likely mechanism is a display strike calibrated to show capacity while leaving negotiation open. Venezuela is for show, with South America as the real audience and China as the larger bargaining target (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0039`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0040`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0042`).

The same answer refuses a false reassurance. Jiang leaves open donor pressure, riots, pilots, Wagner, Cuba, Israel, Iran, or accident as ways the board can be kicked away from managed equilibrium (`video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0045`, `video:interview-l9heow1syg0@transcript:v1#seg-0046`).

Ledger gate: this source supports **managed show-force with accident risk**. It should not be scored as a full-invasion prediction unless the future event is a ground occupation, and it should not be scored as a systems-fire prediction unless other theaters materially respond.

### 2026-01-05: Spectacle Becomes China Leverage

In `interview-orycs0r2tpg`, Jiang shifts from bargaining mechanics to imperial optics. The Venezuela spectacle sacrifices strategy for visuals: Trump treats capture, domination, and television victory as if they were the same as control. The Maduro raid can be productive for Trump's image while damaging American standing and making America look like a criminal enterprise (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0061`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0064`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0069`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0089`).

The January source then ties the spectacle to April China leverage. Jiang says pressure across South America, Colombia, Mexico, possibly Brazil, Africa, Japan, and China is meant to give Trump leverage before the April China meeting (`video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0091`, `video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0092`).

Ledger gate: this source supports **spectacle-as-leverage before a China bargain**. A televised humiliation can confirm the Screen-World/Strategy optics mechanism without confirming a successful strategic conquest.

## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns the date trail, object split, horizon, and correction rule. It should not absorb the mechanisms that neighboring pages already carry.

Game Theory owns the bargaining model: Trump, Maduro, Machado, settlement range, face, bluff, and payoff constraints. Strategy owns whether the war story survives occupation, logistics, and material capacity. Chokepoint owns South American oil, food, ports, maritime permission, and China/Russia/Iran access. Screen-World owns the image-belief loop when Trump treats spectacle as reality. Imperial Retrenchment owns the wider resource-fortress and ally/proxy extraction posture.

Do not create standalone Venezuela, Maduro Raid, Monroe Doctrine War, South America Hegemony, Trump Gorilla, or Systems Fire pages from this cluster. Existing concept pages are the right homes until a public page-size problem or four-source mechanism proves a narrower child boundary.

## Ledger Promotion Threshold

Before any `content/lens/ledger/predictions/**` promotion, require:

1. one narrow claim sentence with source date and horizon;
2. the relevant source trail from this proposal;
3. a public-event comparison packet that distinguishes event from interpretation;
4. a status label that can be `open`, `confirmed`, `partial`, `miss`, `revised`, or `not-scorable-yet`;
5. a boundary note explaining why the ledger item is not just Chokepoint, Strategy, Game Theory, or Screen-World prose.

The first plausible ledger candidate is **Venezuela managed show-force and bargaining, December 13 to December 21 formulation**. The systems-fire and April China-leverage candidates should remain adjacent until public events prove they need separate scoring.
