## Decision

Do not create a public **Grand Strategy As World Theory** page yet.

The April 9, 2026 lecture `interview-rsld81qme04` makes the seed real: Jiang says a country without grand strategy, without a theory of how the world works, becomes a pawn for actors that do have one (`video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0028`, `video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0029`). But the source also shows why the seed should be held. "Grand strategy" is not one more geopolitics topic; it is a joining function across existing mechanisms: character, speech, geography, structural force, motivating ideas, and dated forecast accountability.

The public map already has the right near-term homes:

- **Strategy** owns material and structural diagnosis: force, constraints, war-board feasibility, sunk cost, and the difference between a dramatic story and the test of reality.
- **Stories** owns speech as world-making and coordination when the active mechanism is identity, motive, common future, and the words or actions that make a population move.
- **Nation** owns the state or people as a coordinated actor when the national machine, shared identity, or civilizational personality is primary.
- **Chokepoint Empire** owns the Heartland/Rimland and sea-lane surface only when access control, route denial, oil clocks, or maritime permission is the active mechanism.
- **Prediction** owns source-date scoring and later status, not the full explanatory board.

The next public move should be replacement, compression, or a narrow durable anchor inside an existing page. It should not create standalone **Grand Strategy**, **World Theory**, **Thucydidean Strategy**, **Sunzi Versus Thucydides**, **State Personality**, **Speech As National Coordination**, **Heartland War**, or **China As Pawn** pages from this lecture alone.

## Source Pressure

### April 9 Grand Strategy Lecture

`interview-rsld81qme04` contrasts a Chinese habit of utility, stratagem, and short-term crisis response with a Thucydidean habit of reading a state's character and structural position. Jiang says nation-states and societies have a soul, psychology, emotional state, personality, and outlook; pure utility does not explain why societies act (`video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0007`).

He then turns from stratagems to speeches. The point is not rhetorical decoration. Speeches reveal a nation's personality and outlook, create common identity, tell people who they are and where they are going, and can turn sacrifice into political energy when action itself becomes speech (`video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0008`, `video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0015`, `video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0017`).

Finally, he sets genius against structural force. A great swimmer cannot beat an ocean wave; the Trump question is too small if the war follows Heartland pressure, Anglo-American naval strategy, and the fear that Russia, Iran, and China could unite the Eurasian pivot area (`video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0019`, `video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0020`, `video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0021`).

The seed phrase is therefore precise: a player needs a theory of the world before it can answer short-term crises; otherwise it is used by those whose theory already organizes the board (`video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0028`, `video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0029`).

### April 13 Petrodollar And Russia-America Pressure

The April 13 petrodollar impact `interview-p-dhmudovdo` intensifies the same board. Jiang says the goal is to keep the Iran war going because peace would let Russia, Iran, and China form a trade alliance that counters American sea control and extends through Europe and Africa (`video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0041`, `video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0042`).

He compares America to Athens as an aggressor that may cause others to align against it (`video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0058`).

Together these records justify a held Grand Strategy seed, not a public page. The recurring mechanism is large enough to name, but each public application still belongs to a more specific active surface.

## Boundary Rules

Use **Strategy** when the question is whether a story survives material reality: force, terrain, logistics, structural pressure, timing, sunk cost, hubris, and actual war-board feasibility.

Use **Stories** when the question is how speech, myth, or narrative gives a people identity, motive, common direction, and permission to sacrifice.

Use **Nation** when the question is the state or people as an actor with personality, outlook, coordination, and internal identity.

Use **Chokepoint Empire** when the question is the geographical gate: Heartland/Rimland, Hormuz, Malacca, rail corridors, sea lanes, oil clocks, and access denial.

Use **Prediction** only for dated claims, horizons, later status, ledger readiness, and miss/shift/revision. Do not turn Grand Strategy into another way to append broad geopolitical claims to Prediction.

Use **Imperial Retrenchment** when the question is America's shift from finance empire to resource-fortress retreat, paid sea control, allied burden transfer, or weapons/resources sales. The April 9 lecture already supports that public surface through the resource-empire anchor (`video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0069`).
