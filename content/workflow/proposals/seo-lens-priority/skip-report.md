## Skipped

### `/lens/psycho-history-as-method/`

Skipped as an alias/parent-boundary problem, not for lack of evidence.

The mature material is already split into better public homes:

- `living-school-for-psychohistory` owns the school, Great Books, human-heart variables, open/transparent AI, democratic scenario work, and future institution.
- `game-theory` owns player, rule, incentive, payoff, proximity, coordination, and psychological state modeling.
- `prediction-as-falsifiable-prophecy` owns dated forecasts, misses, hostile audit, and correction.

Creating `psycho-history-as-method` now would mostly duplicate those pages or become an index page. A future page could be justified only if it has a distinct reader job, such as a public "method overview" that intentionally routes to the three existing pages rather than claiming a new concept boundary.

### `/lens/overton-window/`

Skipped for insufficient Jiang-specific concept maturity.

Search showed public-opinion, taboo, discourse, and mainstream-shift material, but not a stable Jiang mechanism named or shaped around the Overton window. The strongest nearby material already belongs to:

- `taboo-as-control-surface` for what can or cannot be crossed, and how a public breach changes the board.
- `how-stories-control-reality` for narrative surfaces and imagination capture.
- `game-theory` and `proximity-games-and-elite-overproduction` for public signaling, factional incentives, and audience games.

The current evidence would produce a generic media/politics page rather than a Jiang Lens page. Keep this skipped until a source cluster explicitly treats the acceptable-speech window as a reusable mechanism.

### `/lens/red-lines-of-war/`

Skipped as a child/section of existing strategy pages.

There is enough source material around escalation, nuclear thresholds, ground-invasion triggers, ceasefire theater, and "act of war" language. But the mature mechanism is already carried by:

- `chokepoint-empire-and-no-exit-war`, especially the escalation-ladder, war-gets-own-logic, and ceasefire-theater lens points.
- `when-war-becomes-a-story-instead-of-a-material-test`, especially dominance versus control, asymmetric warfare, hybrid war, and the material audit of war stories.
- `prediction-as-falsifiable-prophecy` for dated claims about invasion, draft, and escalation conditions.

A standalone `red-lines-of-war` page would currently duplicate these strategy children. Revisit only if future sources make "red lines" a distinct mechanism: who names thresholds, who benefits from crossing them, and how red-line language itself changes player options.
