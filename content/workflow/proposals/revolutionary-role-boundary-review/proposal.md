Keep `revolutionary-role-sequence`, `spy-mind-warps-history`, `security-office-incubates-extremism`, `faith-executes-itself`, and `false-dialectic-as-controlled-opposition` as held routing seeds until the French Revolution middle sources have their own corpus-impact decisions. ## Boundary Decision

Do not create standalone pages for `Revolutionary Role Sequence`, `Poet Prophet Prince`, `Spy Mind`, `Security Office Incubates Extremism`, `Faith Executes Itself`, `False Dialectic`, `Robespierre`, `Napoleon`, `Stalin`, `Lenin`, `Marx`, `Communism`, `French Revolution`, `Russian Revolution`, `Great Purge`, or `Mother Russia` from this cluster now.

Route by active mechanism:

- Reason As Replacement Religion / atlas relation owns secular truth, salvation, progress, civil dogma, and reason inheriting religion's work.
- Taboo owns Robespierre when the active mechanism is regicide, breach, no-return solidarity, scapegoat purification, and transferred guilt.
- Legitimacy Fiction owns Napoleon when the active mechanism is Caesar/messiah role, narratable destiny, and myth requiring action.
- Power As Alchemy owns Stalin when the active mechanism is will, institution, fear, memory, and national psychology becoming lived reality.
- Bureaucracy owns security-office threat manufacture and office self-justification.
- Secret Society receives only caveated hidden-network pressure; do not convert suspicious police/criminal/revolutionary overlap into verified membership claims.
- Game Theory owns false alternatives, real-player reading, and board/payoff diagnosis.
- Nation owns Mother Russia and sacrificial collective body when international doctrine gives way to place, people, and mutual dying.
- Eschatology / Free Will receive faith-executes-itself only when sacred role, martyrdom, confession, chosen surrender, or agency loss is primary.
