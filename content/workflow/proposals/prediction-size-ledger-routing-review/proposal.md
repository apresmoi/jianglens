## Why Prediction Should Not Become A Forecast Archive

Prediction owns Jiang's source-date accountability: forecast family, clock, condition, miss, shift, active-watch, later revision, and ledger readiness. It does not own every mechanism that appears inside a dramatic claim.

The page already teaches the core rules:

- **forecast families before ledger items**, from September 2025 through the late-2025 / early-2026 cluster;
- **financial collapse must be split before scoring**, so market decline, AI-bubble rupture, war-tied crash, capital-center shift, and distressed-asset acquisition cannot all count as one hit;
- **active watch is not confirmation**, especially for long-horizon Iran / civil-war / eschatological branches;
- **quality forecasts need measurement surfaces**, as in the two-year ChatGPT / public-assistant quality claim;
- **theater must become testable**, rather than protecting public spectacle claims from contradiction;
- **hostile audit and falsification keep prediction from becoming charisma**.

Another additive paragraph would probably make the page more complete but less useful. The next public mutation should sharpen a rule already on the page, not add a new list of forecast surfaces.
