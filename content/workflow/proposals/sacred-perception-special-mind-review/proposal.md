## Decision

Do not create a public `Sacred Perception And The Special Mind` page yet.

The seed is real, but it is not one mature public mechanism. It currently joins two adjacent pressures:

1. **Shamanic special mind** from the September 3, 2024 cave-painting lecture: Jiang speculates that some disabled or visibly different people may have been treated as specially able to communicate with the spirit world, meditate, see reality differently, and create cave symbols (`video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0023`, `video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0024`, `video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0025`, `video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0030`, `video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0031`).
2. **Poet-prophet-teacher** from the January 14 and January 21, 2026 Homer lectures: Jiang says Homer accesses archetypes, universal memory, or a larger field of consciousness and turns that access into language where listeners recognize themselves and build civilization (`video:predictive-history-ft2cuowguyc@transcript:v1#seg-0022`, `video:predictive-history-ft2cuowguyc@transcript:v1#seg-0023`, `video:predictive-history-ft2cuowguyc@transcript:v1#seg-0029`, `video:predictive-history-xrp407wsa0w@transcript:v1#seg-0015`, `video:predictive-history-xrp407wsa0w@transcript:v1#seg-0016`).

Those pressures rhyme, but they should not be fused yet. The cave-painting source is explicitly speculative and tied to disability, shamanism, ritual space, altered perception, symbols, and early religion. The Homer sources are stronger public evidence for Poetry because Jiang routes the unusual access through beautiful language, shared memory, and civilization-making. A public child page would currently blur several homes that already carry the active mechanisms.

This record is synthesis, not intake. The relevant corpus-impact files already exist for `predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4` and `predictive-history-ft2cuowguyc`, and Poetry already carries the public `lens-point:poetry-poet-prophet-teacher` anchor.

## Routing Rule

Use the active mechanism, not the topic label:

- **Sacred Machines** owns cave, ritual sound/darkness, shaman costumes, animal/human figures, symbols, and staged access to the spirit world when the question is how material or ritual form bridges invisible order into public life.
- **Civilization As Inner Order** owns the wider claim that religion, common memory, shared mythology, and collective consciousness make society possible before economics can explain human history.
- **Poetry** owns poet-prophet-teacher, archetypes, universal memory, Homer, beauty, truth, language, and civilization-making when unusual access becomes shared language.
- **Human Heart** owns the moral-psychological diagnosis when a literary or sacred figure lets a person see pride, insecurity, compassion, disability, love, or self-recognition.
- **Attention** owns consciousness as capturable or directable energy, especially when altered perception, screens, AI, money, school, or platforms focus imagination.
- **Dead World And The Cave** owns escape practice, meditation, psychedelics, pain, Great Books as rival universes, and reality-perception discipline when the active question is leaving the material cave.
- **Stories** owns symbolic narrative world-making only when the source pressure is story as shared reality rather than cave/ritual infrastructure or poetry as civilizational language.

## Boundary Notes

The seed should not become a disability page. The cave-painting lecture uses disability as one possible route into sacred social status, but the evidence should remain caveated. The active claim is not "disability is sacred" in general; it is that a community can interpret visible difference as access to a hidden world when its religious imagination makes special perception valuable.

The seed should not become an archetype page yet. `archetypes-as-universal-memory` is already held as comparison pressure from the Homer impact intake. If that branch matures, Poetry and Fictional Heroes are the first homes to test because the current public evidence is about Homer creating living characters and shared language, not about archetypes as a free-standing theory.

The seed should not become a psychedelic page. Psychedelics appear in the cave lecture as one possible explanation for geometric signs and altered perception (`video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0030`, `video:predictive-history-x1e5rrmcit4@transcript:v1#seg-0034`) and in the cave/Great Books material as escape-practice pressure. They do not yet define a public mechanism on their own.

The seed should not become a generic prophet page. The Homer lecture explicitly says prophet here is not first a fortune-teller; it means speaking truth from a timeless field (`video:predictive-history-ft2cuowguyc@transcript:v1#seg-0027`, `video:predictive-history-ft2cuowguyc@transcript:v1#seg-0028`). Prediction should only enter when a concrete dated forecast object and comparison trail exist.

## Public Readiness Test

A public page would become justified only if later synthesis can prove all four conditions:

1. At least four dated Jiang sources carry the same mechanism across different domains, not just the cave-painting and Homer lectures.
2. The mechanism cannot be cleanly carried by Sacred Machines, Poetry, Human Heart, Attention, or Dead World/Cave without losing its force.
3. The page can state a reusable diagnostic more precise than "special people see reality differently."
4. The evidence preserves Jiang's caveats around speculation, metaphor, external sources, and poetic truth instead of treating every altered-perception claim as settled ontology.

The likely public mechanism, if it matures, would be narrow:

> A civilization can treat unusual perception as sacred access when difference, disciplined attention, altered consciousness, or poetic receptivity gives a community symbols, language, or scenes through which invisible reality becomes shareable.

That sentence is not yet public-ready. It still needs source fan-in, boundary testing, and a decision about whether the center is sacred infrastructure, poetic language, heart-recognition, attention discipline, or cave escape.
