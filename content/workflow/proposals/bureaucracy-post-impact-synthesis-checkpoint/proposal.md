## Boundary Reasoning

**Bureaucracy As Institutional Death** owns the institutional afterlife: offices, procedures, exams, ranks, policies, departments, security threats, and official narratives preserve the body while draining openness, feedback, creativity, and lived judgment.

**Religion As Administrative Filter** owns the operator-selection code: doctrine, Confucian cultivation, priestly authorization, or scientific method makes a class of interpreters trustworthy and legible. It should receive the Middle Kingdom and Byzantine material only where the active question is how a symbolic code selects and disciplines operators, not where the dead office form is the mechanism.

**Education As A Soul Game** owns schooling when the active mechanism is formation, trust, testing, authority, creativity, and the child's soul. Bureaucracy owns school only when management layers, credential offices, grievance procedures, or administrative status games become the beneficiary class.

**The Borderland Engine** owns openness, competitive pressure, and margin learning. It receives only contrast pressure from the Middle Kingdom and Byzantine closure layers.

**When War Becomes A Story Instead Of A Material Test** owns the material audit of NATO, Odessa, Russia, and war-board claims. Bureaucracy owns the institutional condition that makes a strategic class repeat a failed story because admitting error would threaten the official reality.

## Split And Compression Judgment

The page is below the ordinary split-watch line. Its authored size, source count, and anchor count do not justify extraction by themselves.

A future child page would need a narrower mechanism with at least four dated sources and reader-navigation pressure. Plausible child tests remain conditional:

- **Administrative Meta-Reality**: if Byzantine, Middle Kingdom, Catholic Church, modern security, media, and university sources accumulate around status/literacy/information/narrative monopoly as a distinct page-length mechanism.
- **Threat Manufacture And Security Bureaucracy**: if state-security examples beyond Huawei, Stalin/security-office pressure, and later domestic-control sources show repeated office self-justification through manufactured danger.
- **Exams, Credential Language, And Compliance**: only if Education, Meritocracy, and Bureaucracy cannot keep the Keju / school / credential / specialist-language boundary readable inside their parent pages.

None of those tests is met now.
