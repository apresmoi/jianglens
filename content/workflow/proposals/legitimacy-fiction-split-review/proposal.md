# Legitimacy Fiction Split Review

Status: proposal for public lens size governance

## Decision

Treat [Legitimacy Fiction](/lens/legitimacy-fiction/) as a compression and split-review candidate before adding another ordinary public section. The page is now about 6,136 words with 15 distinct Jiang sources, so it has crossed the ordinary warning line but has not reached the hard 8,000-word or 25-source threshold.

Do not split the page immediately. Keep **Legitimacy Fiction** as the parent mechanism for political inventions that become inheritable worlds: names, offices, texts, titles, rituals, debts, rules, mercy practices, wounds, and public performances that survive the founding crisis and reorganize later action.

The next public mutation should compress the parent around that inheritance mechanism and decide whether one child page is ready. The strongest child candidate is **Scripture, Identity, And Imperial Authorization**, not a generic David page, Bible page, or religion page.

## Why This Advances The Lens

This is synthesis after the `predictive-history-rkpgrrugjnk` corpus-impact intake, not another historical backfill. That intake recorded the December 5, 2024 David apology lecture as the earlier origin layer for the Aeneid Trojan-horse comparison and as strong Legitimacy Fiction pressure. It also warned that the public page is already above the 6,000-word size-governance line.

The source pressure is real, but the public page already has a David/scripture section and a stable lens point:

- `legitimacy-fiction-scripture-outlives-spin`: political spin becomes durable legitimacy when writing, priestly explanation, ritual use, and collective memory turn a crisis-management story into sacred or literary inheritance.

Appending more David prose would make the page longer without solving the actual governance problem. The useful work now is to define the parent boundary, name the child candidates, and prevent David, Ezra, Cyrus, Virgil, Napoleon, American monarchy, and credit/debt examples from becoming one undifferentiated pile.

## Recommended Parent Boundary

Keep **Legitimacy Fiction** as the parent for the inheritance problem:

1. a political crisis cannot be solved by force alone;
2. a story, title, text, ritual, rule, office, debt, mercy practice, or public scene makes the solution believable;
3. multiple actors need the fiction enough to repeat or inhabit it;
4. the fiction survives the founder, scandal, battle, election, or fiscal emergency;
5. later people inherit the made world as a name, scripture, constitution, title, debt, office, role, wound, or game board.

The parent should teach this mechanism with compressed gateway examples. It should not become the full home for every recurring biblical, Roman, American, financial, or imperial submechanism.

## Child Candidate 1: Scripture, Identity, And Imperial Authorization

First child candidate:

**Scripture, Identity, And Imperial Authorization**

Mechanism: writing turns a political crisis into memory, and later imperial authorization, return, temple, law, purity, and public reading can edit identity into an inheritable text-world. The text may begin as apology or administration, but it can outlive the sponsor and become sacred or national memory.

Current source base:

- **2024-12-05**, `predictive-history-rkpgrrugjnk`: David's apology shows writing as expensive royal sponsorship used to create legitimacy; the story of Uriah and Bathsheba converts political violence, weakness, repentance, and reader imagination into durable scripture.
- **2025-11-18**, `predictive-history-3t0mevbd24i`: the Dawn of the Jews lecture treats return, temple, law, purity, Ezra, Cyrus/Artaxerxes authorization, and biblical memory as an imperial identity script that can later become a script for leaving empire.
- **2024-12-12**, `predictive-history-hvvtntpzq7e`: Cyrus shows a foreign ruler entering saved people's sacred memory as Messiah through mercy, return, and temple sponsorship.
- **2024-12-05**, `predictive-history-rkpgrrugjnk`: the same lecture also supplies the Aeneid/Trojan-horse comparison, which belongs on Stories when the active mechanism is rival-world inversion but belongs here when the question is legitimacy, identity, differentiation, and political inheritance.

This child is stronger than a David-only split because the reusable mechanism is not one king's apology. It is the path from crisis writing to inheritable identity, and from imperial authorization to sacred memory that can outlive its sponsor.

## What Would Make A Split Public-Ready

Extract **Scripture, Identity, And Imperial Authorization** only if at least one condition is met:

- the next public Legitimacy Fiction edit would otherwise add more David, Ezra, Cyrus, Bible, temple, return, or sacred-memory prose;
- compression of the parent cannot preserve reader clarity while keeping both the David and Dawn of the Jews sections;
- episode-to-lens provenance needs a narrower anchor than `legitimacy-fiction-scripture-outlives-spin` or `legitimacy-fiction-imperial-script-edits-identity`;
- source fan-in grows beyond the current David/Cyrus/Ezra cluster and shows the same crisis-writing-to-inherited-identity mechanism in another dated source.

If split, preserve existing lens-point IDs unless the move changes their meaning. The likely moved anchors are `legitimacy-fiction-scripture-outlives-spin` and `legitimacy-fiction-imperial-script-edits-identity`. A public move would require Dante review, generated-link validation, and any episode/read JSON link preservation needed by the compiler.
