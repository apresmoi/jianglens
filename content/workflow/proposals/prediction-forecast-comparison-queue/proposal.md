## Why This Advances The Lens

Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy should teach method: framework, dated claim, test condition, miss, revision, and hostile audit. It should not become a repository for dramatic geopolitical claims that merely recur across interviews.

The comparison queue keeps each forecast family tied to:

- source date and horizon,
- conditional versus unconditional wording,
- active mechanism and owning public concept,

This is the right boundary because Chokepoint, Power As Alchemy, Game Theory, Eschatology, Mass Society, and Imperial Retrenchment own the mechanisms. Prediction owns the audit relation among forecast, model, date, miss, and revision.
