## Boundary Decision

Use **Chokepoint Empire And No-Exit War** when the active mechanism is:

> public peace language or tactical pause hides a continuing access-control war architecture: straits, blockades, carrier movement, draft/munitions preparation, sea-lane policing, energy access, trade permission, low-visibility coercion, and the material cost of exit.

Use **Imperial Retrenchment And Proxy Attrition** when the active mechanism is:

> a weakening finance empire retreats toward continental resources, nearby labor, manufacturing, oil, weapons, paid sea control, and regional proxies so the world pays for the enforcement surface that dollar legitimacy no longer carries.

Use **Iran As Pressure Point** when the active mechanism is:

> a move against Iran transmits through Hormuz, Gulf clients, energy, food, dollar demand, base credibility, alliance belief, morale, and escalation incentives.

Use **Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy** when the active work is:

> preserving source date, forecast object, horizon, condition, later comparison, and scoring for May rapprochement, famine, NATO/Ukraine, long-war, Europe civil-war, or ceasefire-reset claims.

Use **Mass Society As Political Constraint** only when scarcity becomes population administration: rationing, digital currency, digital ID, enforcement, migration control, anxiety management, or permission infrastructure.

## Why This Advances The Lens

The corpus pressure is not a new topic called "April Iran War." It is a repeated Jiang move: peace language is audited against the material machinery that remains in motion. The April 16 source adds the oceanic and imperial-business form. The April 22 source adds the recalibration form after the quick exit fails. The April 21 classroom source is already public on Chokepoint as the compressed war-architecture anchor.

If this material is appended mechanically, Chokepoint will become a chronological archive of every Iran-war statement. If it is split too early, "war recalibration" and "Technate resource fortress" become thin pages that duplicate Chokepoint and Imperial Retrenchment. The useful move is a replacement/compression pass that teaches the April sequence as a sharper diagnostic while keeping the parent-child map intact.
