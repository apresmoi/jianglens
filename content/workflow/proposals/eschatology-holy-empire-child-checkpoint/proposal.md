## Boundary Decision

Use **Eschatology** when the active claim is sacred final order: AI as Jesus-mask, Antichrist function, holy empire, one-world government as salvation, reason as obedience, or human intention recoded as the defect to remove.

Use **AI As Control Surface** when the active claim is the interface: answer engine, companion, tutor, guide, body sensor, hallucinated god-image, or command surface through which AI reaches the person.

Use **Mass Society** when the active claim is administration: joined databases, digital ID, digital currency, data centers, Palantir-style prediction, microchips, rationing, work allocation, policing, or population permission.

Use **Attention Capture** when the active claim is captured consciousness: loneliness, dependency, reading displacement, imagination capture, intimate focus, or the dollar-AI contest for human attention.

Use **Power As Alchemy** when the active claim is abstraction becoming operating reality: money losing command, AI becoming a successor god, stablecoin or Treasury machinery, or belief becoming command.

Use **Free Will** and **Human Heart** when the active claim is agency, love, refusal, relation, community, or the rule that salvation cannot remove choice without becoming hell.
