The newly completed impact records for `predictive-history-jjqf9t59uy0`, `predictive-history-rat-zudjhrm`, and `predictive-history-j4htfjwl5d8` sharpen the early-civilization arc, but they do not break the current public map. ## Boundary Rules

**Civilization owns whole-order replacement.** Use Civilization when religion, economy, sex, property, violence, inheritance, memory, status, and human type change as a package.

**Pastoral Conquest is glossary and seed for now.** Use it to name the Yamnaya/steppe mechanism in source accounting and glossary support. Do not make it a public child until at least four dated sources require a narrower hover surface than Civilization and Borderland provide.

**Borderland owns marginal pressure and borrowed innovation.** Use Borderland when openness, border mixing, cohesion, adaptation, or a marginal borrower beating a settled center is the active mechanism. Do not route all Yamnaya material there merely because Jiang names open competition and ruthless borrowing.

**Power owns wealth becoming command.** Use Power when money, property, capital, or abstraction becomes operating reality. In the Yamnaya source, cattle wealth is one element inside a total pastoral order, so Power remains secondary.

**Taboo owns boundary enforcement.** Use Taboo when shame, prohibition, shared transgression, scapegoating, or no-exit solidarity is central. Old Europe's gossip and shame matter here only as part of a whole nonviolent order.

**Sacred Machines owns public interface forms.** Use Sacred Machines when cave, temple, ritual center, pyramid, funeral, city project, or modern replacement machine stages invisible order. Old Europe symbols and Mother Goddess imagery are comparison pressure, not enough for a new interface section.
