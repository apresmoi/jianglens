## Boundary Notes

Power owns host-value extraction: the alchemical conversion of war, frozen assets, science, academia, reconstruction, or humanitarian language into private yield. Keep this as a conversion mechanism, not a scandal catalog.

Secret Society owns operator trust: secrecy, blackmail, transgression, layered knowledge, and cross-silo recognition. It should not become the default home for every named elite, every ethnic label, every occult aside, or every reconstruction deal.

Proximity Games owns replacement struggle: the nearest game may be elite versus counter-elite competition over the position of extractor. Exposure of files can be a move in that game without proving the files were released by a single master planner.

Taboo owns scapegoating and visible blame. The existing Taboo page already warns that visible middle managers can absorb rage for hidden structures. Use Taboo when the evidence is about guilt routing, marked bodies, public hatred, or purification, not simply when the source names a controversial group.

Prediction owns scoreable status. A future ledger candidate must split the source into exact dated objects: Iran/no-ceasefire/global-economy pressure, Libya/Gaza/Ukraine reconstruction extraction, elite/counter-elite civil-war continuation, or later public-event comparison. Do not score "Host-Parasite Operator Network" as one forecast.

Secret Society and Taboo must also preserve the hostile guardrail against ethnic-conspiracy drift. The reusable Jiang lens is a mechanism for separating visible intermediaries, hidden trust, value extraction, and faction payoff. It is not a license to make Jews, Epstein, Chabad, Rothschilds, Kushner, or any named public group into a standalone public lens topic.
