- **Stable Means Stagnant** teaches the center's equilibrium failure.

The useful next move is therefore compression or replacement if new evidence arrives, not extraction.

## Boundary Rules

Use **The Borderland Engine** when the active mechanism is pressure becoming energy, openness, cohesion, adaptation, learning, sacrifice, contact, margin-to-center reversal, or the victory trap where the edge becomes a new center.

Use **Borderland Knowledge Becomes Empire** only if a public draft can show a narrower repeated mechanism: a marginal conqueror absorbs the center's intellectual or military machinery, standardizes it, co-opts elites, and rules through the captured culture.

Use **Bureaucracy As Institutional Death** when the active mechanism is office, procedure, authorized knowledge, credentialed preservation, or institutional capture after the knowledge machinery hardens.

Use **Education As A Soul Game** when the active mechanism is formation of persons through school, teacher, curriculum, tests, trust, creativity, or authority.

Use **How Poetry Creates Civilization** when language, myth, literature, archetype, or speech is forming imagination and common world rather than supplying the administrative grammar of conquest.

Use **Strategy** when battlefield adaptation, logistics, war form, and material audit are primary. Borderland owns the underlying energy/openness/cohesion scorecard only when that diagnostic is doing the work.

Use **Prediction** for dated forecasts, comparison trails, horizon discipline, and ledger readiness.
