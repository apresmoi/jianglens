## Boundary Notes

Power owns this review because the active mechanism is alchemical: where does capital believe its abstraction can still live, grow, and receive enforcement after the old host becomes a debt-fire?

Imperial Retrenchment owns host selection and burden transfer. If the question is whether Israel, America, or another state can carry coercive cost after American retreat, use the replacement-empire review, not this one.

Prediction owns dated scoring. A future ledger candidate must define observable thresholds: capital flows, talent movement, elite relocation, infrastructure investment, enforcement guarantees, and later Jiang revision. Do not score the phrase "Pax Judaica" itself.

AI Control and Mass Society own the surveillance stack. Data centers, digital ID, digital currency, individualized media bubbles, chips, Gaza-style control, and population administration should not be swallowed by Power except as reasons a platform may look enforceable.

Chokepoint and Strategy own route control, reconstruction corridors, tollbooths, Hormuz, and material feasibility. A rebuilt region can attract capital, but capital attraction is not the same as military control or successful reconstruction.

Eschatology owns Jerusalem, Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, final-war, messianic, Antichrist, and sacred-acceleration language. Capital may align with sacred actors, but this review does not score sacred sequence.

Secret Society remains a boundary, not the default home. Use it when the active claim is hidden coordination, intelligence, blackmail, public faces, or membership trust. The investor test belongs to Power.
