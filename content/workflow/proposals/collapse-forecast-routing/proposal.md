## Decision

Do not promote a single ledger item called **Jiang predicted collapse**.

The recent impact cluster has enough dated pressure to justify a comparison record, but not enough boundary clarity for public canon. The sources name several different things: engineered financial collapse, private-credit and AI-bubble rupture, global depression through Hormuz production failure, Venezuela limited-war bargaining, ally rebellion, U.S. civil war, Al-Aqsa/Third-Temple shock, and AI quality decline. Those are not one forecast family.

Prediction should compare them before promotion. Power, Game Theory, Chokepoint, Eschatology, Imperial Retrenchment, Gerontocracy, Mass Society, and Legitimacy Fiction already own the mechanisms that make each forecast intelligible. The immediate synthesis step is to prevent ledger sprawl and public-page bloat by naming which claims can be compared, what page owns the mechanism, and what would make later promotion defensible.

## Why This Advances The Lens

The last several Plato runs produced compact impact decisions and tight provenance repairs around AI control, Mass Society, engineered collapse, and Venezuela/civil-war pressure. Another historical backfill would add accounting, but it would not answer the synthesis question those intakes created: how should Prediction handle collapse claims that overlap with money, war, eschatology, ally extraction, domestic legitimacy, and control grids?

This proposal makes the accumulated impacts usable. It keeps Prediction focused on source date, horizon, condition, later comparison, and scoring. It keeps Power from absorbing every financial doom claim, Game Theory from absorbing every bargaining forecast, and Eschatology from turning every Israel or Al-Aqsa passage into public canon before speculation and chronology are separated.

## Forecast Families To Compare

### 1. Engineered Financial Collapse And Exit Liquidity

Compare claims where collapse is not treated as natural disaster but as a profitable timing choice for actors positioned inside credit, banking, bubbles, or capital flows.

- **2026-03-31**, `predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo`: Jiang opens with the claim that financial collapse has to be engineered, then builds the mechanism through money as bank-created hallucination, interest-rate liquidity commands, insider bubbles, distressed-asset buying, and capital leaving the American host (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0001`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0006`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0008`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0032`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0048`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0057`).
- The same source names **private credit** and **AI** as current bubbles that can be rolled forward while insiders benefit, or broken if collapse becomes the better payoff (`video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0034`, `video:predictive-history-vbowbtjwkbo@transcript:v1#seg-0035`).

Routing: Power As Alchemy owns money as symbolic reality, liquidity command, and private capture of distress. Game Theory owns the player/payoff reversal where a lender or insider can profit from default more than continuation. Prediction owns dated comparison only if the claim is split into checkable surfaces: U.S. financial collapse, global collapse, private-credit rupture, AI-bubble rupture, or capital-center movement.

Promotion condition: no ledger item until later 2026 sources are compared for whether Jiang revises the trigger, horizon, and destination. Do not collapse the whole March 31 lecture into one Great Reset ledger item.

- **2025-12-13**, `interview-pnt-6v5m1wa`: Jiang says the likely Venezuela path is limited war within six months to a year, used to create leverage for a settlement that preserves Maduro's face while opening Venezuela to American private interests (`video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0007`, `video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0010`, `video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0016`).

Promotion condition: do not promote a broad "Venezuela war" item until later sources clarify whether the test is limited strikes, embargo, seizure, covert pressure, negotiated settlement, or full invasion. The December 13 source itself argues against treating full invasion as the default.

AI As Control Surface owns degraded answers and the answering interface. Mass Society owns embodied data grids and population legibility.

Promotion condition: do not use layoffs, data-center buildout, chips, safety policy, censorship, or digital ID rollout as clean confirmation of the AI-quality forecast. Those are related control-grid mechanisms, not the same measurement surface.

## What Not To Promote Yet

Do not promote "Collapse 2026" as one ledger item. It would mix finance, energy, war, allies, civil war, eschatology, and AI into a dramatic topic instead of a falsifiable forecast family.

Do not create standalone pages for Great Reset, Engineered Collapse, Global Depression, Venezuela Trap, U.S. Civil War, Pax Judaica, Al-Aqsa Shock, AI Eats Itself, or Ally Cannibalization from this cluster alone. Existing public pages can carry the mechanisms while this comparison work matures.

Do not treat a source being high-pressure as proof of public-page readiness. Several parent pages in this cluster are already size-sensitive. More ordinary appends would make them harder to read.

Do not merge speculative hidden-coordination claims into factual public voice. Where Jiang discusses secret societies, CIA drug financing, Pax Judaica, capital-center migration, or engineered crash timing, keep attribution and source date visible.
