## Boundary Decision

Use **Chokepoint Empire** when the active mechanism is maritime permission: first island chain, Malacca, Hormuz, Indian Ocean, ports, bases, food, oil, markets, blockade, and energy access.

Use **Prediction** when the active work is dated scoring: China-Japan confrontation in 2026, Taiwan non-invasion / absorption, U.S.-China bargain sequence, Russia-China naval alignment, or later miss/shift/revision status.

Use **Game Theory** when the active mechanism is player-payoff correction: China preferring economic absorption over invasion, Japan facing a now-or-never timing window, or public confrontation functioning as bargain sequence.

Use **Imperial Retrenchment** when the active mechanism is the weakening center's business model: paid sea control, Western Hemisphere resources, resource-fortress retreat, and protection turning into extraction.

Do not create standalone China Maritime Constraint, Malacca Dilemma, First Island Chain, Reactive Alliance, China-Japan War, Taiwan Absorption, Russia-China Alliance, or Japan Closing Window pages from current pressure.
