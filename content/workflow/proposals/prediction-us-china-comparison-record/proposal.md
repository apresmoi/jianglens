## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns the family comparison because the live question is not whether debt, routes, Taiwan, or player incentives matter. The live question is whether a dated forecast remains checkable after several related claims appear.

Game Theory owns the bargain-as-sequence mechanism when the reader needs the player map: prior pressure, leverage, factional constraints, desired payoff, and next-game positioning. The existing public anchor `game-theory-bargain-as-sequence` should remain the parent public point until a cross-source Game Theory subsection is justified.

Power As Alchemy owns stablecoins, Treasury demand, dollar stabilization, debt absorption, and legal/retail compulsion when the monetary sign is the active object. This record should not turn every stablecoin or Treasury source into a Prediction item; Prediction only owns the forecast audit.

Chokepoint owns Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, cheap energy, market access, Western Hemisphere resources, and route pressure when material access is primary. The U.S.-China bargain family may cite those boards, but it should not become a generic China/Taiwan page.
