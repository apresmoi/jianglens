## Decision

Do not create **AI Eats Itself** as a standalone public lens page yet.

The December 9, 2025 Canadian Prepper interview gives Jiang's cleanest AI-degradation sequence: AI needs clean internet data, starts training on synthetic output, becomes less relevant, then seeks a real-world data escape through sensors, body chips, digital ID, and digital currency (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0128`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0129`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0133`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0135`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0137`).

The mechanism is real, but the public surface can already carry it:

- **AI As Control Surface** owns the degradation-to-interface sequence when the question is how AI becomes an answering, body, predictive, or sacred command surface.
- **Mass Society As Political Constraint** owns the embodied data grid: sensors, chips, digital ID, digital currency, child tracking, payment permission, and population-scale legibility.
- **Attention Capture As Capital Extraction** owns the inner dependence and focus layer when AI is sold as helper, companion, intelligence, safety, or ability.
- **Power As Alchemy** owns the data-center cost, monetization failure, money-to-AI replacement, and abstraction becoming lived compulsion.
- **Eschatology** owns AI only when degradation, omniscience, religion, Jesus-mask, Antichrist, or holy-empire language becomes the active role script.

Keep **AI Eats Itself** as a named seed and boundary test, not a new page. The next public move should be compression or replacement inside AI As Control Surface or Mass Society if the pages grow, not another append.

## Source Pressure

The **2025-12-09** interview supplies the seed. Jiang first rejects AI as a technological moonshot, saying the underlying system is mass computing, data centers, extreme electricity and water demand, and an unproven monetization model (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0124`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0126`). He then gives a checkable two-year limits test: ChatGPT should become less effective as the quality of data falls (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0128`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0129`, `video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0131`).

The distinctive move is not only "AI gets worse." Jiang says the system reaches the limit of internet data and begins using AI-created data, so AI is eating itself and data quality decreases (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0128`). That makes a pressure point for **Prediction** only if later ledger work treats the two-year quality claim as checkable. For public lens routing, it is first an AI Control and Mass Society mechanism.

The same source immediately supplies the escape route. AI can solve the data problem if it leaps into the real world (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0133`). Sensors and robotics are the bridge (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0134`). Jiang names microchips inside the body, digital ID, and digital currency as the system that may stop the data problem (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0135`). He then says the play is to force everyone into microchipping and the matrix (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0137`).

That later half already has a stable public home. The public AI page carries `ai-body-interface-sells-control-as-safety`, while the Mass Society page carries `mass-society-embodied-data-grid`. The source also explains why people might accept the grid: Jiang says, as a parent, he can see the appeal of a bloodstream chip if it promises that a lost or kidnapped child can be found (`video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0139`). The sales surface is safety; the administrative result is bodily readability.

The **2026-03-09** Sneako interview supplies the attention bridge, not the data-degradation origin. Jiang says consciousness is true wealth, money focuses attention, and AI girlfriend or ChatGPT can focus attention, motivate action, and make a person do things (`video:interview-o1delachnro@transcript:v1#seg-0123`, `video:interview-o1delachnro@transcript:v1#seg-0124`, `video:interview-o1delachnro@transcript:v1#seg-0125`). This belongs beside AI Eats Itself only when the real-world data grid is being sold back to the user as intimacy, help, motivation, or meaning.

The **2026-03-11** Alex Ray interview supplies the Wizard-of-Oz and matrix bridge. Jiang says money can go away, but the system still needs to direct attention; AI becomes the new god, a hallucinated screen, and a personal matrix (`video:interview-crw5ccq8uf4@transcript:v1#seg-0033`, `video:interview-crw5ccq8uf4@transcript:v1#seg-0034`, `video:interview-crw5ccq8uf4@transcript:v1#seg-0035`). That strengthens the boundary: Attention owns consciousness command; AI Control owns the answering surface; Mass Society owns the permission grid.

The **2026-05-26** classroom synthesis gives the later AI-God version. Jiang says solving the AI problem requires omniscience through a joined database of hospital, school, work, purchase, police, health, and browsing records, then religion so imagination animates the machine (`video:predictive-history-dja6dkcfnge@transcript:v1#seg-0058`, `video:predictive-history-dja6dkcfnge@transcript:v1#seg-0059`). This is downstream of AI Eats Itself, not identical to it: the December source explains why fresh embodied data is needed; the May source explains how total data becomes sacred command.

The **2026-05-28** final examination adds political-economy and human-value limits. Jiang says billionaires fund AI for more money, power, wealth, and control, while governments fund it for more control over people (`video:predictive-history-re2uribefio@transcript:v1#seg-0039`). He later says AI makes human beings feel worthless or redundant (`video:predictive-history-re2uribefio@transcript:v1#seg-0143`). Those passages can deepen AI As Control Surface or Attention, but they do not turn AI Eats Itself into an independent page by themselves.

## Public Mutation Rule

Do not publish **AI Eats Itself**, **AI Data Exhaustion**, **Synthetic Data Collapse**, **Embodied AI Data Grid**, **AI Brain Chip Safety Bargain**, or **AI Redundancy Machine** as standalone pages now.

Create or split only if one of these conditions appears:

1. AI As Control Surface crosses the ordinary size warning line and a replacement pass cannot keep the degradation/body/sacred-interface sequence coherent.
2. Mass Society grows past useful density and the embodied data grid needs a child distinct from digital permission, domestic war control, and surveillance bargain.
3. Episode marks cluster around the exact data-exhaustion-to-body-grid sequence and need a narrower durable hover than `ai-body-interface-sells-control-as-safety` or `mass-society-embodied-data-grid`.
4. Prediction work turns the December 9 two-year AI-quality claim into a dated ledger family, requiring a stable explanatory note without making the ledger itself a broad AI page.
5. At least four dated sources repeat the same degradation-to-embodiment mechanism, not merely general AI control, AI God, loneliness, or surveillance.

Until then, route source marks to existing anchors and use this review as the boundary note.
