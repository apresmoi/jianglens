## Routing Rule

Use the active mechanism, not the metaphysical vocabulary:

- **Poetry** owns archetypes, universal memory, poet-prophet-teacher, divine connection, Homer, Dante, Shakespeare, beauty, truth, language, rereading, and civilization-making when unusual access becomes shareable language or a durable poetic world.
- **Fictional Heroes** owns Achilles, Odysseus, Dante, Beatrice, Dido, Jesus-as-character, or any literary person when the active mechanism is a figure becoming real inside the reader and clarifying self-perception.
- **Human Heart** owns the pride, vulnerability, forgiveness, love, guilt, divine spark, created memory, and moral self-recognition that the poet-prophet or fictional person exposes.
- **Dead World And The Cave** owns the ontology of material prison versus living consciousness, divine spark, escape practice, and Great Books as rival universes.
- **Attention** owns consciousness as capturable or directable energy. It should not absorb poet-prophet material unless the source asks how attention is focused, harvested, retuned, or commanded.
- **Eschatology** owns prophetic scripts when they assign historical roles, sacred sequence, final victory, apocalypse, or salvation politics. Do not treat every "prophet" usage as Prediction or Eschatology.
- **Prediction** owns dated forecasts only after the object, horizon, measurement surface, and comparison trail are explicit. Poet-prophet truth is not automatically forecast scoring.

## Boundary Notes

Do not create an **Archetype** page yet. Jiang's most exact public examples are Homeric character aliveness and poetic civilizational language. Pulling "archetype" out as a free-standing topic would weaken the existing Poetry and Fictional Heroes pages and encourage decorative links from any source using mythic or personality-pattern language.

Do not create a **Universal Consciousness** page yet. The phrase recurs, but the current public pressure splits across different mechanisms: Homeric access to characters, Great Books/cave ontology, Talese-style created memory, divine spark, and attention/consciousness capture. A page would need to prove one reusable diagnostic rather than collect metaphysical nouns.

Do not create a generic **Prophet** or **Poet-Prophet** page yet. Jiang uses prophet in several registers: Homer and Dante as civilizational language-makers; Jesus as divine-spark speech inside a slave empire; Marx as secular prophetic misfire; eschatological actors as role-script accelerators. A single page would blur these registers unless later synthesis proves a clean parent-child map.

Do not add ordinary public prose to Poetry now. `how-poetry-creates-civilization.md` is already about 4,948 words with 60 evidence marks and 9 stable anchors. It is below the split watch line, but the next Poetry edit should be replacement/compression or a sharply sourced chronology repair, not another additive section.

## Public Readiness Test

A public child page becomes justified only if later work can prove all five conditions:

1. At least four dated Jiang sources with completed corpus-impact records carry the same mechanism across Homer, Dante/Shakespeare/Milton/Jesus/Marx/Talese or another comparable set.
2. The mechanism cannot be cleanly carried by Poetry plus Fictional Heroes, Human Heart, Dead World/Cave, Attention, or Eschatology without losing reader usefulness.
3. The page can state a reusable diagnostic more precise than "great figures access universal memory."
4. It preserves Jiang's distinction between poetic truth, spiritual ontology, eschatological role scripts, and dated prediction.
5. It can avoid turning provocative claims about archetypes, faces, divine spark, or universal consciousness into unsupported empirical canon.

The likely public mechanism, if it matures, would be narrow:

> A poet-prophet makes the invisible field of memory, truth, or divine consciousness shareable when a person receives patterns deeper than private invention and gives them language, characters, or witness strong enough to live in others.

That sentence is not yet public-ready. It still needs corpus-impact completion for adjacent sources, boundary testing against Poetry and Fictional Heroes, and a decision about whether the center is language, fictional-personhood, divine spark, created memory, or spiritual practice.
