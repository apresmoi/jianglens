# Story Virus And Remade Consciousness Child Review

Status: proposal for public lens boundary decision

## Decision

Do not extract **Story Virus And Remade Consciousness** into a public child page yet. Keep `stories-virus-remakes-consciousness` on [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) for now, but treat it as the next Stories-family child candidate to test after Screen-World Governance And War Script.

The mechanism is real and reusable: a story lodges in memory, repeats inside consciousness, removes guilt or ordinary inhibition, and spreads through people or institutions as a new way to see and act. The current public source base is strong enough for a parent-page anchor and reader diagnostic, but not yet strong enough for a mature standalone child page without pulling neighboring concepts out of shape.

## Why This Advances The Lens

This is synthesis work after the Stories split-review and Screen-World extraction, not another historical backfill. The live corpus-impact audit still shows many historical gaps, but the room has no fresh ready source from Virgil and no newly merged high-pressure source lacking first impact. The recent Plato portfolio already moved the most public-ready Stories child into its own page, then strengthened its source fan-in. The next useful step is to govern the remaining child candidates so the parent does not keep absorbing true but structurally different examples.

This proposal keeps the lens map from making a premature page. The story-virus cluster touches Secret Society, Taboo, Attention, Dead World, Poetry, Guide Trap, and Eschatology. If it is split too early, it will either duplicate those pages or become a broad "stories affect people" alias. The right boundary is narrower: infection, repetition, disinhibition, and transmission through consciousness.

## Current Source Pressure

The public parent page already has a durable anchor:

- `stories-virus-remakes-consciousness`: a story spreads like a virus when it lodges in memory, seeps into consciousness, removes guilt or inhibition, and travels through people and institutions as a new way to see and act.

The source base has four visible pressures:

- **2025-12-28**, `interview-f7n-6pid0tk`: Jiang moves "magic" into storytelling, compares a song to a virus that seeps into consciousness, and says Frankist stories spread through elites and institutions by changing what power feels permitted to do.
- **2026-01-07**, `predictive-history-tsd-8fga84a`: the cave and attention model explains why consciousness and imagination are recruitable powers rather than passive media effects.
- **2026-04-01**, `interview-oerknj-uypa`: operative myth can shape action and prediction while making later truth correction unstable.
- **2026-04-08**, `predictive-history-6m1z-v3wgok`: Dante gives the literary form: poetry enters memory, creates dissonance, and keeps remaking perception over time.

The cluster has enough recurrence to stay visible, but it is still asymmetric. `interview-f7n-6pid0tk` is the central source for the infection/disinhibition/institutional-spread chain. The other sources strengthen adjacent mechanisms: attention as energy, operative myth outrunning correction, and poetry as long memory. A public child page should probably wait until one more source directly repeats the infection/disinhibition/transmission sequence or until the parent page needs another compression pass.

## Recommended Boundary

Keep **How Stories Control Reality** as the parent mechanism: story as world-setting through attention, imagination, memory, desire, coordination, inversion, guide pressure, and material test.

Treat a future **Story Virus And Remade Consciousness** child as narrower:

1. a story or song becomes memorable enough to repeat inside the person;
2. repetition changes what the person treats as possible, natural, or guiltless;
3. the changed person carries the story into institutions, relationships, or public action;
4. correction becomes difficult because the story is no longer outside the person as a claim; it is inside as a way of seeing.

That child should not become a general "memes" page, a Secret Society page, or an Attention page. It should own the infection path from narrative to consciousness to permission to transmission.

## What Would Make It Public-Ready

Extract the child only when at least one of these conditions is met:

- another dated source directly repeats the infection/disinhibition/transmission chain beyond `interview-f7n-6pid0tk`;
- a concept-strengthening pass can combine `interview-f7n-6pid0tk`, `predictive-history-6m1z-v3wgok`, `interview-oerknj-uypa`, and one more source into a compact child with four distinct source slugs;
- parent-page compression needs the entire story-virus section moved to keep Stories readable after another child split;
- episode-to-lens provenance needs a narrower hover than the current parent-page anchor can provide.

If extracted, preserve `stories-virus-remakes-consciousness` unless the public child reframes the anchor enough to require a new ID. The existing ID is already specific and linked from `interview-f7n-6pid0tk`, so moving it intact would be preferable.
