## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns this review because it protects source-date, horizon, object, falsifier, later comparison, and status. The review is not a public theory of Iran or a ledger promotion.

Chokepoint owns the material gate: Hormuz, Kharg/Kark, Qeshm, GCC infrastructure, desalination, oil, LNG, fertilizer, maritime access, and the cheap-energy platform. No-Exit War owns bounded move becoming next requirement. Mass Society owns draft, riots, National Guard, rationing, emergency powers, and domestic permission machinery if a branch moves into population administration.

Eschatology owns Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, Greater Israel, Pax Judaica, sacred sequence, and convergence scripts when those are active mechanisms. It should not absorb the whole Iran-war family, because several branches are material, monetary, or scoring problems.

Dollar Hegemony owns petrodollar recycling, Treasury demand, reserve confidence, Gulf surplus, sanctions, and forced dollar demand. Imperial Retrenchment owns Western Hemisphere fallback, ally-confidence failure, resource-fortress logic, and successor burden.

Game Theory owns player-map reversals: Trump optics, Israel threat removal, GCC safe-haven panic, Iranian attrition incentives, U.S.-China codependence, and allied protection decisions. Strategy owns the material audit of any war story and the incompatible-victory problem.

Secret Society does not own this synthesis. Some sources use speculative or sacred-script language, but this file is not evidence of hidden coordination. Keep hidden-coordination claims in Secret Society only when directly evidenced and caveated.
