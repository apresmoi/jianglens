## Current Parent Boundary

Keep Legitimacy Fiction as the parent when the active mechanism is political inheritance:

- a crisis cannot be settled by force, administration, or private advantage alone;
- a story, name, office, title, ritual, debt, rule, mercy practice, public scene, or text becomes the carrier;
- several actors need the fiction enough to repeat it, compete inside it, teach it, finance it, or transfer love through it;
- the fiction survives founder death, scandal, battle, election, fiscal emergency, or institutional collapse;
- later people inherit the made world as name, scripture, constitution, office, debt, rule, role, wound, or game board.

That parent boundary remains coherent. The issue is density, not conceptual failure.

## Compression Target

The next public Legitimacy mutation should compress rather than expand these areas:

1. **Caesar / false messiah / oligarchic monarchy.** Keep the two stable anchors `legitimacy-fiction-monarchy-relieves-oligarchic-despair` and `legitimacy-fiction-false-messiah-savior`, but tighten the surrounding prose so the parent teaches the rescue-story mechanism without absorbing forecast scoring, domestic control machinery, debt-fire payoff, or civil-war game theory.

This should be replacement/compression-first: reduce the parent while preserving all stable anchor IDs unless a later public child extraction preserves their meanings more clearly.
