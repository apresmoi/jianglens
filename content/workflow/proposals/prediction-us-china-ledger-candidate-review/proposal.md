## Ledger Status

Recommended status: **active watch / partial adjacent signal, not confirmed**.

Reasons:

- **Positive signal**: the relation did visibly move from tariff-war escalation toward negotiated arrangements by late 2025 and a Beijing summit package by May 2026.
- **Clock ambiguity**: the September source gives a six-month clock, while the strongest public event surface appears later than six months and closer to the broader half-year-to-one-year restatement.
- **Hidden mechanism problem**: Jiang's core clause depends on a behind-the-scenes compromise and private Chinese concessions, which public fact sheets cannot directly verify.
- **Mechanism uncertainty**: public deliverables around boards, rare earths, Boeing aircraft, agriculture, beef, poultry, Iran/Hormuz, and North Korea do not prove the specific dollar-stabilization and U.S.-debt-relief mechanism.
- **Later-source revision risk**: January and May sources recast the bargain through Western Hemisphere resource leverage, China dollar buying, Taiwan pressure transfer, stablecoins, Treasuries, AI chips, and energy access. That may be continuation, revision, or a separate family.
