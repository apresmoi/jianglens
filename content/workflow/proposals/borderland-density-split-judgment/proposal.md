## Boundary Rules

Use **The Borderland Engine** when the active mechanism is pressure becoming energy, openness, cohesion, adaptation, learning, sacrifice, contact, margin-to-center reversal, or victory turning the edge into a new center.

Use **Borderland Knowledge Becomes Empire** only as a future child test when the active mechanism is narrower: the margin captures the center's intellectual or military machinery, standardizes it, co-opts elites, and rules through the conquered culture. Do not promote this child without drafting parent and child together.

Use **How Poetry Creates Civilization** when language, myth, literature, archetype, or speech forms imagination and a common world. Borderland may cite Homer or Aristotle only when the active mechanism is marginal pressure, learning, or captured knowledge becoming imperial grammar.

Use **Education As A Soul Game** when the active mechanism is person formation through school, teacher, curriculum, tests, trust, creativity, or authority.

Use **Bureaucracy As Institutional Death** when knowledge machinery hardens into offices, procedures, credentialed preservation, institutional capture, or authorized interpretation for its own sake.

Use **Strategy** when battlefield adaptation, logistics, military form, and material audit are primary. Borderland owns the deeper energy/openness/cohesion scorecard only when that diagnostic is doing the work.

Use **Prediction** for dated claims, horizon discipline, forecast comparison, active-watch status, miss/revision language, and ledger readiness.

Use **Power As Alchemy** when money, contracts, financial shelter, capital platforms, or abstraction becoming lived command is the active mechanism. Borderland owns only the dynamism question: whether easy money drains motion, morality, and cohesion.

## Reopen Conditions

Reopen a public child split only if one of these happens:

1. Borderland crosses about 6,000 authored words or 20 distinct authored local source slugs after compression opportunities are exhausted.
2. New evidence clusters episode links around `borderland-knowledge-becomes-empire` so strongly that the parent hover becomes too broad for readers.
3. A public draft proves that moving the knowledge-machine branch makes both parent and child clearer while preserving source support and stable anchor meaning.
4. A future source changes the chronology or mechanism enough that the parent can no longer hold contact-learning, knowledge standardization, and victory reversal as one diagnostic.

Do not create standalone Borderland Cases, Greece, Arabia, Vikings, Mongols, Rome, Qin, Pax Judaica, Ukraine Adaptation, Philip, Alexander, Aristotle, Alexandria, Bank of England, Poverty Is Not Magic, Easy Money Drains Motion, or Stable Is Stagnant pages from this pressure alone.
