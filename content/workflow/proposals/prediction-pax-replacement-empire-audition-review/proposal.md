## Decision

Do not promote "Pax Judaica," "Israel replaces America," or "Israel defeats America" as one prediction.

The ledger-shaped branch is narrower:

> In the March 26, 2026 Game Theory #16 lecture family, Jiang says Israel is auditioning to replace America as the Middle East muscle of the world-system if America is forced out, retreats, or implodes after the Iran war; the audition tests whether Israel can supply coercive will, system maintenance, infrastructure control, corridor access, human capital, AI surveillance, and capital protection.

Recommended status: **unpromoted; split-before-promotion; active comparison family**.

A ledger item would need observable thresholds: American Middle East retreat or loss of protection credibility, transfer or inheritance of military/infrastructure functions, capital and talent movement, Israeli control over routes or reconstruction, and later Jiang comparison that preserves the source's speculative caveat.

## Boundary Notes

Prediction owns this artifact because it defines source date, condition, candidate object, caveat, and future scoring thresholds.

Imperial Retrenchment owns the public mechanism. The existing public page already carries `lens-point:strategy-replacement-empire-audition`, and that remains the correct reader-facing surface: successor empire is tested by whether it can carry coercive burden after the incumbent loses capacity or will.

Power owns capital migration and safe enforcement. A claim that capital moves to Israel is not the same as a claim that Israel inherits American military function.

Chokepoint owns routes, ports, Hormuz, corridors, logistics, Gulf fragility, and resource gates. Route control can support the audition, but it is not sufficient to confirm the ledger branch.

AI Control and Mass Society own the surveillance stack. Data centers, digital ID/currency, Gaza-style control, and population administration should not be swallowed by Prediction just because they appear under the Pax Judaica phrase.

Eschatology owns Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, diaspora return, final-war interpretation, sacred sequence, and messianic role language. This review deliberately does not score the sacred sequence branch.
