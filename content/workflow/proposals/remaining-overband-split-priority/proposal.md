## Priority Order

### 1. Power As Alchemy: compress and route first

Power should be the next public test because it combines three pressures:

- it is a root lens page, so blur here propagates into money, nation, schooling, individualization, attention, legitimacy, and religion;
- it is close to the 20-source warning line and has the highest anchor count among the remaining candidates;
- an existing 2026-06-02 source-density review already identified the overloaded branch: monetary alchemy is crowding the parent while **Dollar Hegemony** and **Debt As Sovereignty** already own narrower public mechanisms.

The public mutation should preserve the parent definition:

> Power becomes alchemy when it trains imagination until invented abstractions act like unavoidable reality and organize labor, desire, fear, and obedience.

The edit should compress monetary examples that mostly teach reserve currency, Treasury hostage logic, forced dollar demand, reserve-asset tribute, or debt command. Those should route to Dollar Hegemony or Debt As Sovereignty unless the paragraph is actively teaching abstraction-to-reality conversion.

Do not move stable Power anchors in the first pass unless a public draft proves that the target page can preserve the same meaning. A compression/replacement pass can improve reader usefulness without destabilizing episode links.

### 2. Eschatology As Script: compress around the definition spine

Eschatology is also over the soft word line, but it has two recent governance records: the 2026-06-04 split review and the 2026-06-06 definition-spine review. Those records already answer the child-page question: no standalone Holy Empire, AI God, Pax Judaica, Al-Aqsa, Antichrist, Gog and Magog, or Third Rome page yet.

The next public Eschatology move should therefore be a replacement/compression around the August 1 definition spine, preserving all stable anchors and routing forecast status to Prediction, material war to Chokepoint or Iran pressure, AI interface to AI As Control Surface, population machinery to Mass Society, value conversion to Power, and agency/love to Free Will or Human Heart.

Eschatology is second because its public path is already clear and recent. Power is more likely to blur multiple neighboring pages if left untouched.

### 3. Strategy Material Test: watch, then compress

Strategy is the largest remaining authored page by word count, but the source fan-in is lower at 14 distinct local video slugs, and the page has already produced public children: Chokepoint, No-Exit, and Imperial Retrenchment. Its current over-band signal is more likely a parent-gateway compression issue than a new split issue.

The next Strategy-family public mutation should not be a third child by reflex. It should measure whether the parent can be shortened around economics, organization, logistics, war-form, deathbed hubris, and spectacle-versus-board diagnostics while keeping child routing explicit.

## Boundary Rules For The Next Public PR

Use **Power As Alchemy** when the active mechanism is abstraction becoming lived compulsion: money, individual, nation-state, school history, monotheism, expert reality, scarcity, or counter-alchemy becoming the world people obey.

Use **Dollar Hegemony** when the active mechanism is the dollar operating system: Bretton Woods, reserve currency, petrodollar demand, SWIFT/IMF/World Bank pipes, Treasury tribute, reserve privilege, or forced dollar demand.

Use **Debt As Sovereignty** when the active mechanism is obligation becoming rule: Treasury hostage logic, household debt absorption, default pressure, debt as policy command, or foreign assets becoming leverage.

Use **Eschatology** when the active mechanism is sacred role assignment, final order, promise becoming plan, ritual sequence, prophecy as geopolitical plan, holy empire, or catastrophe made meaningful as obedience to the end.

Use **Strategy Material Test** when the active mechanism is whether a war story survives economics, organization, logistics, war form, supply, morale, enemy adaptation, and the material board.

Use **Chokepoint** when the active mechanism is a material gate or access-control surface: strait, canal, route, base network, blockade, port, island chain, food or oil passage, naval attrition, client-state exposure, or protection credibility.
