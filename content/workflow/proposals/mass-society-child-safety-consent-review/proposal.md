## Decision

Do not split **Child Safety Converts Control Consent** into a public child page yet, and do not add another ordinary Mass Society subsection now.

The seed is real, but the current public Mass Society page already carries the mechanism in the **Digital Permission Grids** and **Embodied Data Grids** sections. The January 24 source gives the gate: child-safety age verification becomes digital ID, then digital currency, then financial repression. The December 9 source gives the bodily-data form: AI needs real-world data, microchips and digital identity/money solve the data problem, and parental fear makes child tracking attractive. The February 15 source intensifies the same consent pathway through Epstein panic, missing-child fear, microchipping, and surveillance-state normalization.

That is enough recurrence to treat the seed as a named Mass Society submechanism, but not enough to make a standalone public concept. It remains a child of the digital-permission grid: fear for children can make identity, payment, tracking, implant, and AI-security infrastructure feel protective instead of dominating.

This proposal is synthesis, not impact intake. The January 24 and February 15 source impacts already exist, and the December 9 material is already public on Mass Society. The purpose is to keep future edits from turning a precise consent mechanism into a new catchall page about children, parenting, Epstein, social media, biometrics, or AI.
