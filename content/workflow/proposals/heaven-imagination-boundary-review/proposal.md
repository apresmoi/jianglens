## Decision

Do **not** create standalone public pages for `heaven-as-remembered-bliss` or `imagination-beyond-love` now.

The June 20 Dante livestream gives real concept pressure, but the pressure is still an intersection rather than a clean public boundary. `Heaven As Remembered Bliss` belongs first to Eschatology's personal afterlife layer, Poetry's memory-triggering method, and Human Heart / Love's concrete relation. `Imagination Beyond Love` belongs first to Guide Trap's threshold mediation, Love's person-to-person recognition, Poetry's expressive medium, and Free Will's active participation in redemption.

The correct near-term action is restraint: keep both seeds held, route future source moments by active mechanism, and use them only in replacement/compression passes where they reduce confusion rather than add another Dante append.

## Boundary Decision

Keep **Eschatology** as the home for heaven, death, fear of death, afterlife, salvation, and final-order scripts. R526 should matter there only when a future compression adds the personal-experiential afterlife layer: Heaven as felt bliss and memory rather than institutional terror or abstract destination.

Keep **Poetry** as the home for the method that makes Heaven felt: memorized operating software, music, rhythm, imagination, and private memory made shareable (`video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0005`, `video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0062`, `video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0073`). If public work follows, it should be a Dante-access replacement/compression, not a Heaven child page.

Keep **Human Heart** and **Love Recognizes The Other** as the homes for concrete relation. R526's children, distance, Beatrice, and love-across-dimensions material strengthens love as personal recognition (`video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0022`, `video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0027`, `video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0047`). It should not become a generic Bliss page.

Keep **Guide Trap** as the home for threshold mediation. Beatrice's disappearance matters because the necessary guide or beloved can lift the person to a threshold and still have to yield. That is adjacent to Virgil but not identical to Virgil's trap: Beatrice is not a false-world guide in R526; she is a necessary beloved whose role ends before the final act.

Keep **Free Will** as the home for active participation in redemption. When Jiang reads faith, Mary, imagination, and salvation as active rather than passive (`video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0211`, `video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0219`, `video:predictive-history-r526-wug3me@transcript:v1#seg-0224`), the public boundary is agency under love, not a new Marian or imagination page.

Keep **Fictional Heroes** secondary. R526's final Virgil material strengthens the interior-guide problem, but `imagination-beyond-love` is not primarily about fictional characters becoming real. Fictional Heroes owns Virgil living inside the reader; Guide Trap and Poetry own the route by which the reader must pass beyond inherited guides.

## Seed Status

Hold `heaven-as-remembered-bliss`.

The seed is real because R526 gives Heaven a diagnostic mechanism: fear of death is loosened when afterlife becomes imaginatively inhabitable through remembered bliss, embodied relation, music, and poetry. It is not public-ready as a standalone page because the active components already route cleanly. Eschatology owns afterlife and death fear; Poetry owns the memory-triggering form; Human Heart and Love own children, relation, and recognition.

Hold `imagination-beyond-love`.

This seed is also real. R526 sharpens a difficult claim: love can be highest expression and still not be the final faculty, because the last approach requires imagination and active selfhood rather than total dissolution into the beloved. But a public page now would blur Guide Trap, Love, Poetry, Free Will, and Eschatology. Use it later only if at least one future source repeats the threshold pattern outside Beatrice, or if a replacement/compression pass needs a compact label for the boundary between beloved mediation and self-active imagination.

Do not create standalone Heaven, Bliss, Happiest Memory, Children, Fear Of Death, Beatrice, Bernard, Mary, White Rose, Imagination Beyond Love, Love Versus Imagination, or Dante Final Vision pages from the current evidence.
