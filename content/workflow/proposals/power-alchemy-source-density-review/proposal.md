## Decision

Do not create a new public **Power As Alchemy** child page today.

The page is over the ordinary size warning line at about 6,500 words, with 18 distinct authored evidence sources and 17 durable lens points. That is real source-density pressure. The pressure, however, is uneven: the **Money: A Number That Commands Work** section carries about 4,200 words, 14 distinct sources, and 12 of the 17 lens points. The individual and nation-state sections remain compact. The page is not globally incoherent; the monetary branch has become the overloaded branch.

The first response should be parent compression and stricter routing to already-existing child-neighbor pages, not a new child. **Dollar Hegemony** already owns the dollar world game, reserve-currency institutions, demand creation after gold, petrodollar dependence, SWIFT/IMF/World Bank pipes, Treasury tribute, protection credibility, and dollar-addiction demand. **Debt As Sovereignty** already owns debt as policy command, reserve-asset hostage, household debt absorber, default pressure, and debt becoming rule. A new "Money Alchemy" child would mostly duplicate those two pages unless it kept a much narrower boundary.

Power As Alchemy should remain the parent for the upstream conversion: invented abstractions become lived reality. It should carry the portable mechanism across money, the individual, national memory, monotheism, school history, expert authority, and counter-alchemy. The next public mutation should compress the parent monetary branch and route ordinary dollar/debt examples to the two existing child-neighbors.

This is synthesis, not impact intake. The audit still shows many historical missing impact files, but no fresh ready-source handoff outranks the live source-density cue. Recent Plato work has already produced several public child extractions; this review prevents the next one from being mechanical.

## Why This Advances The Lens

Power As Alchemy is one of the lens's root mechanisms. If it keeps absorbing every monetary example, it will stop teaching the conversion and become a loose archive of debt, reserve, stablecoin, petrodollar, gold, and financial-war scenes. That would blur three useful layers:

- **Power As Alchemy:** how a symbol, number, individual, nation-state, or official history becomes reality people work and obey inside.
- **Dollar Hegemony:** how the dollar specifically becomes the world game and demand system.
- **Debt As Sovereignty:** how obligations become policy command, hostage assets, household absorption, or default-pressure rule.

The public map improves if those layers remain distinct. Readers should be able to land on Power and understand alchemy before being routed to dollar or debt. Readers who already know the monetary branch should be sent to the child-neighbor page whose active object is narrower.

2. Compress the Money section by replacing repeated dollar/debt explanation with short routing paragraphs to Dollar Hegemony and Debt As Sovereignty.

4. Keep only the monetary evidence needed to show alchemical conversion: scarcity discipline, money as grace/reality, symbol trust, debt entering daily life, and reserve privilege addiction as examples.

If compression cannot do that cleanly, reopen the child-boundary question with a redistribution plan that accounts for Dollar Hegemony and Debt As Sovereignty first.

Reopen this review if one of these happens:

- Power As Alchemy rises above about 7,000 words or 20 authored evidence sources.
- Episode-to-lens links cluster around one monetary Power anchor enough that a more specific hover page is needed.
- Dollar Hegemony or Debt As Sovereignty starts duplicating Power prose rather than narrowing it.

Until then, the decision is compression, routing, and child-neighbor hygiene, not a new Power child.
