## Why Game Theory First

Game Theory is a root method page. When it blurs, many neighboring pages absorb the cost: Strategy, Prediction, Proximity Games, Secret Society, Power, Nation, Eschatology, Education, and Stories. Recent impact intake has increased exactly that kind of pressure.

The strongest new pressure is not "make a Law of Asymmetry page" or "make a Communist Specter page." It is whether Game Theory can still hold several distinct method moves without becoming a catch-all:

- real-game identification: actors may be rational once the true payoff is named;
- non-material payoff correction: the prize may be martyrdom, humiliation, sacred destiny, or consciousness control rather than money or territory;
- visible-alternative manipulation: a false or extremized option can sabotage the board by clearing rival authorities or making the counter-position serve the same system;
- player and board correction: declared threats, proxy actors, host-written rules, and target-order sequencing can hide the actual player or payoff.

Those moves still share one parent mechanism, so the first action should be a **Game Theory parent compression / child-boundary review**, not a public split. The review should ask whether newer asymmetry and false-dialectic pressure can be absorbed by strengthening `game-theory-real-game` and diagnostics, or whether a narrow child boundary is needed later around something like **Extremized Alternatives And False Games**.

That possible child remains only a test. It should not be created unless at least four dated sources prove a reusable mechanism that is narrower than Game Theory and not already owned by Power, Nation, Legitimacy Fiction, Religion As Administrative Filter, Strategy, or Stories.
