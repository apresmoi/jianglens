## Decision

Keep **force substitutes for dollar trust** as a held bridge, not a standalone public page, not a new ledger item, and not an ordinary append to either Dollar Hegemony or Chokepoint.

The candidate mechanism is:

> When dollar legitimacy weakens through excess finance, frozen assets, sanctions, rival bargaining, and lost reserve confidence, American empire tries to make reserve-currency obedience material again through military control of routes, energy, and trade chokepoints.

That sentence is useful because it names the bridge between pages. It is not yet a concept boundary. The current public pages already have the needed homes:

- [Dollar Hegemony](/lens/dollar-hegemony/) owns reserve legitimacy, post-gold demand creation, frozen-asset distrust, forced dollar demand, Treasury buying, sanctions, petrodollar recycling, and reserve addiction.
- [Chokepoint Empire](/lens/chokepoint-empire-and-no-exit-war/) owns the route-control board: Hormuz, Malacca, energy gates, food routes, resources, ports, bases, and trade access.
- [No-Exit War And Escalation Ladder](/lens/no-exit-war-and-escalation-ladder/) owns the moment a military move cannot stop because retreat would expose the dollar-and-protection story as false.
- [Debt As Sovereignty](/lens/debt-as-sovereignty/) owns the debt hostage surface: Treasuries, foreign buyers, domestic default impossibility, and future revenue streams becoming command.
- [Power As Alchemy](/lens/power-as-alchemy/) owns the deeper conversion where money and financial signs become lived reality before the operating system or military board tests them.
- [Imperial Retrenchment](/lens/imperial-retrenchment-and-proxy-attrition/) owns the fallback posture when financial empire weakens into resource fortress, ally-burden transfer, and extraction from protected dependents.
