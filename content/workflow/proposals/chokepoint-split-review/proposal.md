The page is about 5,300 words and currently cites 18 distinct Jiang video sources. That keeps it below the ordinary word and source-count split thresholds, but it is close enough that the next edit should be governed by replacement, compression, or a narrower child decision. Treat this review as the guardrail for the next public mutation: compress the page or replace weaker prose before adding more evidence; route forecast status to Prediction; route sacred role pressure to Eschatology; route dollar belief to Power As Alchemy; route fortress-resource retreat to Imperial Retrenchment; and route population-control infrastructure to Mass Society.

## Boundary Decision

Keep Chokepoint as one child concept for now:

> A weakening empire turns circulation into access control; the narrow gates it controls then become places where energy, food, dollar demand, alliance belief, military credibility, and exit cost are audited together.

That boundary is still coherent. The current sections are not separate public pages yet because they form a single mechanism chain:

1. **Chokepoint empire:** reserve-currency power weakens, so control shifts toward passages, bases, canals, straits, blockades, and naval attrition.
2. **System-pivot straits:** Hormuz and similar gates matter because multiple dependencies pass through the same narrow route.
3. **No-exit war:** a war launched to defend money, allies, or credibility cannot leave without breaking the protection story.
4. **Ceasefire theater:** public peace language may change while the war architecture keeps organizing blockade, energy disruption, defense planning, and financing systems.
5. **Escalation ladder:** each actor's next move becomes locally necessary, so stopping feels more dangerous than another bad wager.

These are stages of the same diagnostic, not yet clean sibling pages. Splitting them now would create pages that immediately need each other for context.

## Routing Rules For Future Work

Use **Chokepoint** when the active object is a material gate: strait, canal, route, base network, blockade, island chain, port, food or oil passage, naval attrition, ground-war trap, or the exit cost created by protection credibility.

Use **Prediction** when the active object is source date, claim, condition, horizon, public-event check, miss, revision, or ledger readiness. The March 24 draft/emergency-powers chain and the May 7 World War III synthesis belong there unless a public Chokepoint edit is explaining the war trigger.
