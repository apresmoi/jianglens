## Decision

Cassandra's fresh split-set warning is useful because Free Will now has high generated ref density and is a major routing page for Dante, Secret History, Zoroastrianism, cave/slavery, institutional Christianity, love, and anti-love material. Direct authored checks, though, show the live page remains below the ordinary split diagnostics: about 5,187 authored Markdown words, 73 authored evidence attributes including lens-point evidence, 62 compiled doc evidence marks, 16 distinct authored local video slugs, and 11 page lens points.

That means Free Will should stay on compression-watch, but the current signal does not justify extracting a child page before replacement/compression is attempted. Do not add ordinary Asha, cave, Dante, Paradiso, Purgatory, Grand Inquisitor, Paul, Augustine, school, parent, or anti-love examples to the parent. Future Free Will work should either replace weaker parent prose, route pressure outward by active mechanism, or reopen a narrow child only under the threshold below.

## Why This Is The Right Boundary

The page remains a coherent parent because its active object is **agency as the burden that makes love, truth, imagination, damnation, and redemption real**. The recurring question is not simply whether Jiang mentions choice. It is whether the soul can hand responsibility to God, Satan, school, church, parent, guide, institution, desire, fear, or social reality without losing the thing that makes love and truth possible.

The current page already keeps neighboring mechanisms routed outward:

- **The Dead World And The Cave** owns the false world, captured attention, and screen-mediated reality when the wall itself is the object.
- **The Guide Who Becomes A Trap** owns Virgil, false guides, mediation, and trusted authority when the guide relation is the object.
- **Dante As Poetic Access And Reception** and **How Poetry Creates Civilization** own Dante's literary method and poetic access when technique, reception, or imagination-as-reading is the object.
- **Human Heart As Civilizational Measure**, **Love Recognizes The Other**, and **Love As Civilizational Force** own forgiveness, recognition, and love as social or relational measure.
- **Religion As Administrative Filter** owns Zoroastrian truth-duty when it disciplines offices, state formation, or priestly administration rather than the soul's own becoming.
- **Education As A Soul Game** and **Meritocracy Converts Wounds Into Credentials** own school formation, parental face, credential injury, and institutional trust when education is the active mechanism.
- **Eschatology** owns apocalypse, judgment, final scripts, sacred role, and coordinated end-times pressure when sequence and world-ending choreography are the object.

Because those routes already exist, Free Will can remain the parent for the sharper agency bundle: truth must be lived, slavery can be chosen, love cannot coerce redemption, darkness tests human light, hell can become desired, purgatory requires self-forgiveness, Paradiso exposes self-authored station, and institutions can rename obedience as freedom.

Route outward when the active object changes:

- **The Guide Who Becomes A Trap** for Virgil, necessary guides, mediation, false authority, and guide capture.
