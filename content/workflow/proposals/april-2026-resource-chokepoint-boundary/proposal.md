- **Prediction** owns dated scoring for China energy bargaining, Russia shadow-fleet attrition, post-2028 policy continuity, long Russia-America struggle, and mid/long-term coalescence around Russia and China.

The next public edit should therefore be replacement or routing, not expansion. If the April 13 material is used visibly, it should replace weaker prose in Imperial Retrenchment or Dollar Hegemony, or tighten Chokepoint's routing language. It should not create standalone **Policeman Becomes Pirate**, **Technate Resource Fortress**, **Financial Ponzi Must Fight Eurasia**, **Heartland War After The Petrodollar**, **Greater North America**, **Petrodollar War**, **Hormuz**, or **Malacca** pages.

## Boundary Rules

Use **Chokepoint Empire** when the question is where the gate is, what passes through it, how access is denied or priced, and how the access-control board creates exit cost.

Use **Imperial Retrenchment** when the question is what a weakening center does after finance legitimacy weakens: continental resource consolidation, naval tolls, weapons sales, paid protection, allied burden transfer, proxy clients, and resource-fortress business models.

Use **Dollar Hegemony** when the question is why dollar demand must be preserved: petrodollar demand, Treasury markets, reserve assets, SWIFT/sanctions, frozen assets, BRICS/gold alternatives, and coercive foreign buying.

Use **Prediction** only for source-date accountability, forecast-family comparison, later status, and ledger readiness. Do not solve a public-size problem by moving the same dramatic April 13 claims into Prediction prose.

Use **Power As Alchemy** only when the emphasis is symbol-to-reality conversion rather than the dollar operating system or the material enforcement surface.
