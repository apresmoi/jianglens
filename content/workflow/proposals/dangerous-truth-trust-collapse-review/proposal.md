## Decision

Do not publish **Dangerous Truth And Trust Collapse** as a public concept yet.

`interview-oerknj-uypa` makes the seed visible: Jay asks whether a dangerous truth can cause more harm than silence, Jiang answers that truth has value in itself but can trigger destructive institutional reaction, and the conversation ends by saying skepticism now has to distrust both official myth and counter-myth (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0109`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0110`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0112`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0124`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0144`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0145`). The source is strong, but the current public map already has homes for most of the pressure:

- [How Stories Control Reality](/lens/how-stories-control-reality/) owns operative myth, trust-floor collapse, and the danger that correction itself becomes another script.
- [Prediction As Falsifiable Prophecy](/lens/prediction-as-falsifiable-prophecy/) owns Hasan's facts-versus-truth audit and the rule that speculative pattern language must stay exposed to checkable facts, misses, later evidence, and hostile questioning.
- [Bureaucracy As Institutional Death](/lens/bureaucracy-as-institutional-death/) owns destructive official reaction when a true report becomes an administrative instruction rather than living judgment.
- [Human Heart As Civilizational Measure](/lens/human-heart-as-civilizational-measure/) owns Jiang's answer when the practical technology is good-faith conversation across offensive disagreement.
- [Free Will As Cosmic Burden](/lens/free-will-as-cosmic-burden/) may own the individual duty to seek truth when a later source makes responsibility, not story or method, the active mechanism.

The seed should remain a reviewed workflow proposal until a second dated source repeats the full public-harm mechanism or the neighboring pages need a narrower hover target.

## Why This Advances The Lens

This run follows three compact Story Virus-adjacent impact intakes and one threshold proposal. The live audit still shows historical missing impact files, including `interview-kh8dvnddooq`, but no fresh ready-source handoff is waiting and the recent portfolio is already impact-heavy. A workflow proposal is the right bounded step because it turns `interview-oerknj-uypa` into a public-boundary decision without appending another large section to Stories or Prediction.

The proposal also prevents a common atlas error. "Dangerous truth" can sound like a moral topic, a media-ethics topic, or a free-speech topic. Jiang's reusable mechanism is narrower: when the shared trust floor breaks, factual correction, speculative pattern, official myth, and counter-myth can all become weapons. The question is not whether truth matters. It is what social and institutional machinery can carry truth without destroying the people it is supposed to help.

## Source Comparison

### Jay Shapiro: Truth, Harm, And The Broken Trust Floor

**2026-04-01**, `interview-oerknj-uypa`, Myth Outruns Truth.

Jay's opening problem is that mythology can outrun truth until truth itself becomes suspect (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0005`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0006`). The middle of the interview turns that into a speech-ethics question. If a truth about 9/11, Masada, Zionism, Islam, Judaism, or Palestine were available, Jay asks whether the public consequences might outweigh saying it badly (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0107`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0108`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0109`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0121`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0123`).

Jiang's answer has two parts. First, truth is not dispensable: he says truth has value in itself and ties that to his life in journalism and teaching (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0110`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0111`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0115`). Second, publication can become grotesque when an institution turns a true fact into a dead instruction. His Afghanistan case is not abstract: a couple of avian-flu cases could cause chicken killing in a malnourished country, so the factual report and the humane action could separate (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0112`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0117`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0118`).

The closing answer is not censorship. Jiang reaches for a remembered liberal room: say offensive things, hear offensive things, do not call the police, do not deplatform, and sit together to find the differences (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0124`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0125`). Jay then names the social condition that makes the problem acute: the myth-makers have made the stories too ridiculous, the nightly-news safety bargain no longer works, and people now have to remain skeptical of official narratives and of the people who doubt official narratives (`video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0128`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0144`, `video:interview-oerknj-uypa@transcript:v1#seg-0145`).

## Boundary Rule

Use **Dangerous Truth And Trust Collapse** only when a source answers this question:

> What happens when a truthful claim, correction, or speculative pattern enters a society whose trust floor has broken, so that official myth, counter-myth, fact, and warning can all become weapons unless conversation, evidence, and human regard remain alive?

Do not use the seed merely because a source says "truth," "facts," "propaganda," "free speech," "censorship," "prediction," or "myth." Route by active mechanism:

## Public-Readiness Test

1. A second dated Jiang source directly repeats the full mechanism: truth or correction enters broken public trust, creates foreseeable harm or weaponization risk, and requires a social technology beyond silence.
2. `interview-kh8dvnddooq` impact plus a later Prediction or Eschatology comparison shows that facts-versus-truth has become too large for Prediction alone.
3. A Bureaucracy pass finds multiple dated sources where true information becomes harmful because official systems process it without judgment.

5. A public Stories or Prediction compression pass needs to move trust-collapse material out without losing the mechanism.

If the page is later created, it should not become a general free-speech essay. Its first source trail should probably run from Hasan's facts-versus-truth audit to Jay's dangerous-truth tightrope, then test whether Jiang's Afghanistan case and good-faith-conversation answer recur elsewhere.
