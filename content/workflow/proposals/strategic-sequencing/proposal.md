# Strategic Sequencing

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: lens seed; held from public promotion pending cross-source recurrence
Work type: proposal synthesis from `interview-jlejlzmum4g`

## Proposed Boundary

Record **strategic sequencing** as a held lens seed, not a public page yet. The mechanism is that a hegemon tries to settle, freeze, or compartmentalize one front so it can move to the next target. Once that order becomes visible, the targets can rationally bind together: each reads the other's fall as its own future exposure.

This is narrower than general alliance formation. It applies when four conditions appear together:

1. an empire or strategic class imagines adversaries can be handled one by one;
2. one front is supposed to be settled or paused so force, attention, legitimacy, or money can move to the next front;
3. the next targets can see the order and update their incentives;
4. Jiang or the source frame shows that the visible sequence itself creates alignment or resistance.

## Source Pressure

The immediate source is the **2025-11-06** Duran interview, [Predictive Geopolitics As Imperial Breakdown](/interviews/interview-jlejlzmum4g/). Mercouris asks about an alleged Kallas-Wang Yi exchange: China supposedly refused to pressure Russia into a Western peace because that would only leave China exposed after Russia (`video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0063`, `video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0064`).

Jiang answers by naming the pattern. Americans are discussing "strategic sequencing": end or freeze Ukraine, move against Iran, then move against China. Russia therefore has no reason to accept a settlement that only lets the United States circle back later, and Russia, China, and Iran have to hold together because if one falls, everyone else falls (`video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0065`, `video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0066`).

Mercouris then supplies the host-side definition: take enemies one by one on the assumption that they will not notice the order and respond. Jiang's reply limits the elegance of that strategy. He expects American leaders to throw many ideas at the wall, because bureaucracy, corruption, and the military-industrial complex prefer wars without closure to successful reprioritization (`video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0067`, `video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0070`, `video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0071`).

## Current Public Homes

This seed borders two existing public concepts but should not be merged into either by default.

- [Game Theory](/lens/game-theory/) owns the player-positioning mechanism: once the sequence is visible, the other players change their payoffs and may coordinate even when their interests are otherwise separate.
- [When War Becomes A Story Instead Of A Material Test](/lens/when-war-becomes-a-story-instead-of-a-material-test/) owns the material audit: whether the United States can actually fight Ukraine, Iran, China, and domestic turmoil while sustaining logistics, allies, debt, bureaucracy, and legitimacy.

The same interview already deepened Strategy through `lens-point:strategy-vassal-proxy-attrition`, which covers the material expenditure of dependent allies. Strategic sequencing is adjacent but distinct. Vassal proxy attrition asks whose bodies, resources, and legitimacy the patron spends first. Strategic sequencing asks why serially targeted adversaries hold together once they see the order.

## Boundary Rules

Use this seed when a source moment is about target order, one-by-one strategy, or adversary alignment caused by the visible order.

Do not use it for generic coalition behavior unless the sequence changes incentives. Ordinary "enemy of my enemy" logic is too broad.

Do not use it for every overextension claim. If the active question is logistics, occupation, allied expenditure, supply lines, industrial capacity, or war aims, route to Strategy Material Test.

Do not use it for monetary or debt sequencing unless the active mechanism is player targeting. If the source is about dollar demand, reserve-asset trust, debt transfer, or financial belief, route to [Power As Alchemy](/lens/power-as-alchemy/).

Do not use it for bureaucratic drift unless the active mechanism is failed institutional motion after the strategy has been imagined. In that case, route to [Bureaucracy As Institutional Death](/lens/bureaucracy-as-institutional-death/).

## Why This Advances The Lens Map

The current Strategy page already has a strong vassal-proxy anchor from this interview. Without a separate seed, later agents could overstuff that anchor with every late-imperial mechanism in the source. Strategic sequencing gives the map a cleaner boundary: a serial target order can create the alignment it is meant to prevent.

The seed is not ready for public promotion because this is one source cluster, and Jiang presents the policy discussion as a diagnosis rather than a verified document. It is still worth recording now because the mechanism is reusable and easy to confuse with Strategy's allied-expenditure material.
