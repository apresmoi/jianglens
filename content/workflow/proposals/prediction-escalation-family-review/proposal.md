## Decision

Do not promote "World War III," "Trump Caesar," "Pax Judaica," "Iran attack," or "2027 gets worse" as one Prediction ledger item, and do not create standalone public pages for those topic names from the current evidence.

The recent impact records for `interview-t5cnf8dqj-q`, `interview-qsxkdk4mzgk`, and `interview-wio-7-gios` create a synthesis problem. Each source turns the same pressure field into several different forecast objects: domestic emergency authority, capital migration into a protected Israel-centered platform, Iran/corridor war, Ukraine/Odessa/Europe, China/Japan/Malacca, U.S.-Russia sea conflict, and generalized 2026/2027 acceleration. Treating that as one dramatic forecast would make Prediction less falsifiable.

The useful family boundary is:

> Jiang's escalation forecast cluster tests whether late-imperial stress turns through multiple interacting boards: domestic Caesar/emergency-power logic, Pax Judaica as capital-safe-house or sacred role-script, Iran and corridor denial as the hinge of hegemony, Europe/Odessa as proxy-war exhaustion, China/Japan/Malacca as access pressure, and a 2026/2027 acceleration horizon.

That sentence is a comparison family, not a ledger item. Ledger promotion should begin only after the family is split into claims with source date, object, horizon, condition, public-event threshold, neighboring mechanism, later Jiang comparison, and possible miss or revision.
