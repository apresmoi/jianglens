# Resilience After Efficiency Cluster Review

Date: 2026-06-10
Status: held seed; synthesis proposal before public promotion
Work type: concept-scoped proposal synthesis

## Decision

Hold **Resilience After Efficiency** as a real concept cluster, not yet a standalone public lens page.

The reusable mechanism is strong: Jiang repeatedly treats the efficient unipolar world as a best-case machine that breaks when energy, food, water, trade, logistics, political trust, and meaning are stressed. Efficiency gives cheap movement, cheap food, financial abstraction, megacity convenience, and the illusion that ordinary life can continue after a shock. Resilience asks the harsher question: what can still feed, move, coordinate, and morally sustain people when that optimized system is interrupted?

That mechanism is already partly public, but it is distributed across several correct homes. Publishing a new page now would risk turning a useful diagnostic into a broad collapse topic. The next public move should be a replacement/routing pass, not immediate promotion: tighten the existing Strategy/Chokepoint/Mass Society/Nation/Human Heart surfaces so the resilience cluster can be found without duplicating them.

## Boundary Rules

Use **Resilience After Efficiency** as a cluster label only when the source asks what an optimized system lost by optimizing: redundancy, local capacity, energy slack, food security, water security, social trust, moral discipline, political participation, or relations that still work under stress.

Do not route every collapse forecast here. If the active question is dated scoring, horizons, or whether a prediction has happened, use Prediction.

Do not route every energy or food passage here. If the active mechanism is a gate, route, strait, blockade, port, payment pipe, or protection bargain, use Chokepoint/Strategy.

Do not route every community passage here. If the active mechanism is love, neighbor relation, spirituality, courage, or meaning as the thing that survives money and geography, use Human Heart or Free Will.

Do not route every surveillance passage here. If the active mechanism is allocation, digital permission, AI database command, draft administration, or bonded population management, use Mass Society.

Do not route every small-scale-political passage here. If the active mechanism is successor body, city-state, participation, or national-machine breakdown, use Nation or Civilization.
