## Boundary Notes

Chokepoint owns the mechanism when the active question is how Hormuz, Belt and Road, the North-South Corridor, GCC exposure, food/fertilizer flows, and trade access turn Iran into a route and revenue pivot.

Iran As Pressure Point owns the transmission surface: a strike on Tehran becomes a test of energy, food, money, bases, morale, and alliance trust. The current review can cite that page but should not duplicate it.

Prediction owns source date, speculative status, forecast object, horizon, later comparison, and miss/revision. A future ledger candidate must decide whether it scores Hormuz control, toll collection, Russian/Chinese rebuilding, GCC fragmentation, Israel-Iran division, or Iran's long-run power separately.

Game Theory owns the strong-respect-strong / weak-ally-with-strong rule when the claim is about why Israel and Iran could cooperate despite ideology. Chokepoint owns the material board that makes cooperation useful.

Imperial Retrenchment owns American retreat, successor burden, and GCC protection failure. The Iran corridor branch may follow from American weakness, but it is not the same as Israel replacing America as imperial muscle.

Dollar Hegemony owns petrodollar demand, Treasury buying, GCC oil-dollar recycling, and reserve credibility. Chokepoint owns the gate that tests those monetary relations.

Power owns capital's search for protected enforcement and cheap postwar assets. Rebuilding Iran or buying distressed resources only belongs to Power when the investor calculation is primary.

Eschatology owns sacred role-script, final-war, Al-Aqsa, Third Temple, and messianic acceleration. Do not let the Iran corridor branch become proof of sacred sequence.

Mass Society owns rationing, energy lockdowns, digital permission, war administration, and scarcity control if the corridor shock becomes population management.

## Ledger Readiness

Status: **not ledger-ready as confirmed**.

Possible future ledger forms:

1. **Active-watch Hormuz corridor record**: Iran retains meaningful Hormuz control and turns it into a toll or revenue surface after major war damage.
2. **Russia-China rebuild record**: Russia and China supply visible financing, industry, logistics, or corridor infrastructure that makes Iran stronger after the war.
3. **Two-pole Middle East record**: Israel and Iran become the two primary regional poles while GCC actors fragment toward stronger patrons.
4. **Miss/revision record**: Iran loses route control, Russia/China do not rebuild, GCC protection remains intact, or Jiang later revises the corridor model.

Minimum requirements before promotion:

- define "control over Hormuz" as military denial, legal tolling, escort regime, revenue extraction, blockade threat, or shared settlement;
- distinguish destruction in Iran from strategic victory for Iran;
- distinguish Russia-China interest from actual rebuilding activity;
- distinguish China mediation/no-ground-troops from Chinese backing of Iran;
- preserve source dates: 2025-09-01, 2026-03-24, 2026-03-26, and 2026-04-07;
- keep the March 26 classroom caveat visible: the two-pole model is theory and intellectual speculation, not prophecy;
- compare later Jiang sources and public events before scoring confirmation, partial signal, miss, or revision.
