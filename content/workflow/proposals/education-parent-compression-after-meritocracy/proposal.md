## Decision

The problem is that the parent still reads like the pre-extraction page and gives full-section weight to several clusters that should now be gateway treatments.

Do not open another workflow-only Education split review unless new evidence changes the boundary. The next useful Education work is a public compression/replacement PR that keeps Education as the soul-game gateway, preserves all remaining parent anchors, routes meritocracy to the child page, and tests whether the trust/test cluster needs a future child only after the parent has been compressed.

## Recommended Parent Boundary

Education should stay the parent for formation through rewarded educational games:

1. an institution names learning, creativity, citizenship, wellbeing, or human development as its mission;
2. the actual reward game trains trust, obedience, status ambition, compliance, attention habits, measurable legibility, or rival self-formation;
3. the student adapts until that game helps decide what the student can trust, fear, desire, notice, and become;
4. rival education tries to recover attention, world contact, historical imagination, bodily competence, craft, love of learning, and the belief that action matters.

The compressed parent should be a gateway chapter rather than a complete archive of every school-related source. It should keep short treatments of trust, separation, status, tests, creativity, rival formation, research, cave schooling, and compliance. It should route readers outward when a narrower mechanism now has a public home.
