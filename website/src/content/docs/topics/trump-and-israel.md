# Trump And Israel

Jiang does not give one simple Trump-Israel story. Sometimes Trump appears as the vehicle for Saudi and Israeli pressure. Sometimes he appears as a Caesar figure trying to use war and spectacle for his own legitimacy. Sometimes Jiang emphasizes Zionist personnel around Trump. Sometimes he complicates the picture by suggesting Trump and Netanyahu may hate or embarrass each other in public.

The safest reading is structural: Trump is the figure through whom Israel, Zionist networks, donor politics, Christian Zionism, Saudi alignment, U.S. empire, and American attention politics become visible at once.

## Fast Answer

The earliest clear Trump-Israel-Iran map is the **2024-05-29** Iran Trap lecture. Jiang says Trump is the champion through whom three forces manifest, and he identifies Jared Kushner as the private channel because Kushner is close to [Benjamin Netanyahu and Mohammed bin Salman]{evidence="video:predictive-history-7y-hbz6loeo@transcript:v1#seg-0003"}. In that lecture, Trump is not an isolated decision-maker. He is embedded in donor, family, Saudi, Israeli, and AIPAC-linked networks.

Jiang's later shorthand is "personnel is policy." In the **2025-12-13** interview, he says the Trump White House includes many Zionist or pro-Israel figures and that these personnel control enough levers of power to make the war trajectory difficult to avoid. He names [Jared Kushner, Steve Witkoff, Marco Rubio, and Mike Huckabee]{evidence="video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0088"} as examples inside that dated claim.

## The Donor And First-Term Channel

Jiang's Trump-Israel argument begins before the second term. In June 2024, he says Trump had publicly argued Biden was not doing enough to protect Israel and that pro-Israel billionaires were coalescing around him. He names Miriam Adelson as [the most pro-Israel billionaire in America]{evidence="video:predictive-history-go1bmqknjbq@transcript:v1#seg-0048"}. In the Saudi lecture, he also ties [Kushner's Middle East role, MBS, Israel, and the Abraham Accords]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lctucvqhhns@transcript:v1#seg-0021"} together, then points to [the Soleimani assassination as evidence that Trump's first term already escalated toward Iran]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lctucvqhhns@transcript:v1#seg-0025"}.

This is why Trump and Israel cannot be separated from Iran in Jiang's corpus. The Israel alignment is not only embassy symbolism or campaign rhetoric. It is one channel into the Iran Trap, where donor pressure, regional strategy, and sacred story all point toward escalation.

## Trump As Actor Or Believer

Jiang does not always claim Trump controls the script. In the Tucker interview, he offers several possibilities: [Trump may be an actor following a script, a man with his own messianic calling, a president forced by Netanyahu's escalation, or a compromised figure under pressure]{evidence="video:interview-2k2nqsttjqe@transcript:v1#seg-0075"}. That refusal to choose is part of the source-grounding discipline. The evidence available from outside may show function without proving interior motive.

In other interviews, he says Trump was under pressure from allies and donors to start Middle East conflict, but he also reads Trump as a political performer who can use crisis, attention, and war to consolidate power. The point is not puppet versus mastermind. The Jiang Lens question is what role Trump is made to play inside overlapping games.

## Tension With Netanyahu

The Trump-Israel relationship is not always smooth in Jiang's telling. In the Epstein-file interview, Jiang discusses Trump's Knesset appearance after a Gaza ceasefire and says Trump embarrassed both Netanyahu and Miriam Adelson. He reads Trump asking whether Adelson loved America or Israel more as a public insult, and says the moment makes Trump and Netanyahu look like [rivals rather than friends]{evidence="video:interview-vvj4mxqu2qk@transcript:v1#seg-0059 video:interview-vvj4mxqu2qk@transcript:v1#seg-0060"}.

That complication matters. A page about Trump and Israel should not reduce the relationship to "Israel controls Trump" or "Trump controls Israel." Jiang's better model is factional: declining empires produce allies who compete, donors who demand returns, leaders who need spectacle, and ideological projects that temporarily align before they diverge.

## Why Iran Keeps Returning

Iran is the test case because both Trump's pro-Israel network and Israel's Greater Israel horizon converge there. Jiang says powerful forces in the United States push toward war, that Trump escalated with Iran in his first term, and that Iran can provoke or exploit the trap. In April 2026, he also says Trump may have [an off-ramp strategy through antiwar figures such as J.D. Vance and Tulsi Gabbard]{evidence="video:interview-kh8dvnddooq@transcript:v1#seg-0025"}, which again prevents a flat reading of Trump as only one thing.
