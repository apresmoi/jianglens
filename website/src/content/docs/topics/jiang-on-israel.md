# Jiang On Israel

Jiang's Israel analysis is one of the most charged areas of the corpus and needs careful boundaries. He does not use "Israel" in one simple way. Sometimes he means the Israeli state. Sometimes he means religious Zionists. Sometimes he means the Greater Israel project. Sometimes he means Pax Judaica, which he distinguishes from Israel as a state and ties to transnational capital, technology, surveillance, and trade.

That distinction is the guardrail. Do not turn Jiang's Israel material into a generic claim about Jews. The actual Jiang Lens question is how state power, sacred geography, military intelligence, diaspora networks, capital, and empire interact around the Levant.

## Fast Answer

Jiang reads Israel as a small state with outsized strategic position because the Levant sits between older centers of wealth and present trade routes. In the **2025-11-06** interview, he says the Levant has historically been a linchpin because control there grants access to [Egypt, Mesopotamia, Anatolia, and the Mediterranean]{evidence="video:interview-7qz4kmtbvru@transcript:v1#seg-0057"}. In later material, that geography becomes the base for his Greater Israel and Pax Judaica arguments.

The most compact version appears in the **2026-04-15** interview. Jiang says to distinguish the [Greater Israel project from Pax Judaica]{evidence="video:interview-ril8pssntzg@transcript:v1#seg-0015 video:interview-ril8pssntzg@transcript:v1#seg-0016"}. Greater Israel is the religious-territorial project from Nile to Euphrates. Pax Judaica is the capital-and-technology project that would finance, secure, surveil, and globalize the order that emerges around it.

## Greater Israel

Greater Israel is Jiang's term for a territorial and sacred claim. In a 2026 war-board lecture, he says Israel's grand strategy is the Greater Israel project, where the promised land stretches [from the Nile to the Euphrates]{evidence="video:predictive-history-6anh6sbpqvq@transcript:v1#seg-0042"}. He then names [Israel's three attack vectors as the Bible, Mossad, and diaspora capacity]{evidence="video:predictive-history-6anh6sbpqvq@transcript:v1#seg-0043"}: biblical legitimacy recruits outside believers, Mossad supplies intelligence and infiltration, and diaspora networks provide money, talent, and reach.

The controversial part is that Jiang reads the Iran war through this project. In March 2026, he says much of the Middle East war is driven by [Israel's desire to achieve Greater Israel]{evidence="video:interview-pvhuffwtu3i@transcript:v1#seg-0018"}. That does not mean every Israeli actor wants the same thing or that every Jewish person belongs to the same project. It means Jiang is analyzing the organized factions that, in his model, turn sacred geography into strategy.

## Pax Judaica

Pax Judaica is broader than Greater Israel. Jiang describes it as a future trading, financial, technological, and surveillance order with Jerusalem as the hub. In the **2026-03-09** interview, he says Israel could establish [a trading, financial, and technological empire based in Jerusalem]{evidence="video:interview-zy2t6wr3ttm@transcript:v1#seg-0003"}. In another lecture, he gives the material version: Israel's location, data centers, IT talent, intelligence capacity, and trade corridors could make it the [AI surveillance backbone of Pax Judaica]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0050 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0051"}.

This is where Israel connects to [Power As Alchemy](/lens/power-as-alchemy/) and [Chokepoint Empire And No-Exit War](/lens/chokepoint-empire-and-no-exit-war/). The claim is not only ideological. It is about where capital believes enforcement, data, labor, energy, and trade can be made reliable after Pax Americana weakens.

## Israel And Iran

Iran matters because it blocks both the military and sacred versions of the project. In Jiang's model, Israel benefits when America is drawn into war, the GCC is weakened, and Iran is forced into a costly confrontation. But Iran also creates the danger that the whole strategy backfires. If Iran survives with Hormuz leverage, trade links to Russia and China, and renewed Persian civilizational identity, it becomes [a counterweight to Pax Judaica rather than only an obstacle]{evidence="video:interview-ril8pssntzg@transcript:v1#seg-0022"}.
