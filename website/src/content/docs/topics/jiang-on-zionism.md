# Jiang On Zionism

Jiang's Zionism material is not a generic definition page. It is a map of actors who treat Israel as the sacred and strategic center of history. His corpus repeatedly separates several layers: Christian Zionism, Jewish Zionism, Greater Israel, Pax Judaica, American national-security personnel, and wider eschatological systems.

The guardrail is simple: Jiang's claims about Zionism should not be flattened into claims about all Jews or all Christians. He repeatedly focuses on organized, fanatical, politically effective minorities and on the institutions that amplify them.

## Fast Answer

Jiang's sharpest formulation comes from the **2024-05-08** lecture on Christian Zionism. He distinguishes a promise from a plan. Historic premillennial believers wait for Jesus to return; dispensational premillennial believers treat prophecy as [a plan believers must achieve]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lkkrzq4ydqy@transcript:v1#seg-0014"}. That makes Israel, the temple, the Antichrist, Iran, and Middle East war operational requirements rather than background beliefs.

He also gives the caveat in the same lecture. Most Christians do not hold this view, and many would see it as trying to [force God to return]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lkkrzq4ydqy@transcript:v1#seg-0017"}. The reason it matters anyway is organization. Jiang says the groups that believe this most intensely are also [organized and fanatical enough to achieve objectives]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lkkrzq4ydqy@transcript:v1#seg-0018"}. That is the political mechanism.

## Zionism As Prophecy Fuel

In Jiang's reading, Christian Zionism turns war into prophecy fuel. Supporting America, Israel, and conflict with Iran can become, for believers inside that frame, a way to help history reach its promised ending. He says conflict in the Middle East is desirable to that worldview because it [drives the prophecy]{evidence="video:predictive-history-lkkrzq4ydqy@transcript:v1#seg-0024 video:predictive-history-lkkrzq4ydqy@transcript:v1#seg-0030"}.

That is why the topic belongs with [Eschatology As Script](/lens/eschatology/). Jiang is not saying religion mechanically causes every war. He is saying an end-times story can assign roles, identify enemies, organize money and personnel, and make catastrophe appear meaningful.

## Zionism, Greater Israel, And Pax Judaica

In the **2025-07-11** lecture, Jiang places Christian Zionists and Zionists inside a larger anti-Iran coalition. Christian Zionists want biblical prophecy; Zionists want the [Greater Israel project]{evidence="video:predictive-history-5i2vpypjj68@transcript:v1#seg-0002"}. Financial elites and the American empire may want different things, but their roads can point toward the same target. Jiang explicitly warns against a crude master-plan reading: the groups may not even talk to each other, because [separate incentives can align without a smoke-filled-room command center]{evidence="video:predictive-history-5i2vpypjj68@transcript:v1#seg-0003"}.

Later interviews add Pax Judaica. Greater Israel is the sacred-territorial project; Pax Judaica is the capital, surveillance, labor, trade, and finance project that could grow around it. That distinction matters because Zionism in Jiang's material is not one actor. It is a convergence point where religious longing, imperial geography, donor politics, intelligence, and mobile capital can reinforce one another.

That convergence is also why personnel matters. In the **2025-12-13** interview, Jiang says the U.S. national-security apparatus is full of Zionist-aligned figures and explicitly distinguishes [Zionists, Christian Zionists, Mormons, secret societies, and other religious groups from Jews as a whole]{evidence="video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0087 video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0088"}. That caveat is essential to using the term without turning it into an ethnic or religious smear.

## The Jerusalem Script

Jerusalem is the center because many eschatologies converge there. In March 2026, Jiang says people are drawn to Jerusalem because it is imagined as where history begins and ends, and where believers feel [compelled to act out their belief]{evidence="video:interview-zsgyhwkinzk@transcript:v1#seg-0033"}. The story of Gog and Magog, Armageddon, Israel against the world, and messianic return becomes powerful because it allows people to [participate in achieving heaven on earth]{evidence="video:interview-zsgyhwkinzk@transcript:v1#seg-0034"}.

The Al-Aqsa and Third Temple thread is the most dangerous version. Jiang ties the red heifer, Third Temple preparation, Al-Aqsa, Hamas, Iran, Hezbollah, and wider Islamic reaction into a sequence where sacred action can force political escalation. In the **2025-12-13** interview, he says Al-Aqsa's destruction would compel Iran and allied actors to respond, while Israeli actors seeking Pax Judaica imagine the order arriving through [a baptism of fire]{evidence="video:interview-pnt-6v5m1wa@transcript:v1#seg-0083"}.
