# Imperial Retrenchment And Proxy Attrition

This child lens names one mechanism inside Jiang's broader strategy map: a weakening empire tries to preserve the old order by changing who pays the material cost. It may retreat toward nearby resources, turn naval protection into toll extraction, audition a successor muscle, spend allies as proxy bodies, or make war continue because admitting loss would collapse a financing story.

The parent Strategy lens, [When War Becomes A Story Instead Of A Material Test](/lens/when-war-becomes-a-story-instead-of-a-material-test/), asks whether a war story can survive economics, organization, logistics, supply, troops, bases, and geography. This page owns a narrower imperial-transition question: when the center can no longer carry open global circulation cheaply, which resources, allies, clients, sanctioned rivals, debt structures, and replacement actors are made to carry the burden?

Keep the boundary sharp. [Power As Alchemy](/lens/power-as-alchemy/) owns money becoming social reality. [Game Theory](/lens/game-theory/) owns player-payoff correction. This page owns the material expenditure after those maps are named: what the old empire must enforce with ships, resources, bases, weapons, bodies, and clients when belief no longer does enough work.

## Resource Empire After Petrodollar Strain

The January 5, 2026 interview gives the transition its earlier geopolitical shape. Jiang says the United States no longer knows what it can extract from Europe: the ally is aging, unwilling to fight, low on useful resources, and, in his reading, protected for decades by American security infrastructure. The result is not sentimental alliance repair. It is abandonment. He says [the United States is going to throw Europe overboard]{evidence="video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0054"} and then use the Western Hemisphere as the base from which it can tell China: [buy resources from the Western Hemisphere, and therefore buy from us]{evidence="video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0055"}. That matters because the resource base is already tied back to the monetary order. Jiang names the strategy as keeping China [subordinate to the U.S. dollar and dependent on the U.S. dollar]{evidence="video:interview-orycs0r2tpg@transcript:v1#seg-0055"}.

The April 2026 interview cluster pushes the same chokepoint argument into a sharper imperial transition. Jiang is no longer only saying that a reserve-currency empire may harden into access control. He is describing a possible move from finance empire to resource empire.

The April 2 classroom lecture now gives that cluster its earlier classroom origin. Jiang starts with a material-stack shock: Hormuz disruption is not only oil and LNG, but [fertilizer inputs, food production, semiconductor inputs, AI supply, and the modern economy's physical base]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0013"}. The ugly inversion is that a failed or unwinnable Middle East war can still benefit the United States if it makes the world [more dependent on North America for oil and resources]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0014 video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0020"}. By the time he reaches U.S. debt, the resource argument has become an imperial finance argument: Asia and Europe need energy and fertilizer, so the American debt problem is softened because [the entire world is dependent on North America for resources]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0022 video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0023"}.

That is why the Technate passage belongs here only as resource-fortress evidence, not as a new doctrine page. Jiang introduces a self-sufficient Greater North America map after naming Greenland, Canada, Mexico, Colombia, Venezuela, Cuba, Honduras, and Nicaragua as the near-abroad perimeter, then says the collapse of the GCC and Middle East can make Asia and Europe dependent on [North American energy and fertilizer exports]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0032 video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0033"}. He then defines the replacement order by contrast with the post-1991 New World Order: instead of finance, consumer peace, and Pax Americana, Trump World Order moves America [from finance to resources and manufacturing]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0035"}, assumes [permanent war supplied from a protected American fortress]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0036"}, and rebuilds American manufacturing while the outside world burns. In the later question period he states the mechanism directly: the Middle East war helps extend decline long enough to transform America [from finance toward resource exportation and manufacturing]{evidence="video:predictive-history-xrmerlhuqbk@transcript:v1#seg-0042"}.

In the April 13 petrodollar interview, the starting point is legitimacy failure. Jiang treats the dollar order as a system whose belief has been strained by U.S. debt, frozen Russian assets, weaponized dollar access, treasury exits, BRICS alternatives, and China's gold corridor. That is why he says [the petrodollar is the very basis of American empire]{evidence="video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0004"}. If consent weakens, the material substitute is not persuasion. It is carriers, chokepoints, sanctions, blockades, and war.

The image is deliberately ugly: the global policeman becomes a pirate. Jiang says the military that once guaranteed peace and trade is being turned [from a police force into a pirate force]{evidence="video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0027"}; when tariff authority is blocked, the Navy can become the collector, because if courts and Congress will not enforce tariffs, [tolls can be enforced at sea]{evidence="video:interview-p-dhmudovdo@transcript:v1#seg-0027"}. The story of rules-based order is therefore tested by the institution that enforces the rule. If naval protection becomes naval extraction, the same infrastructure that once made trade feel safe starts making trade conditional on obedience.

The November 30 CapitalCosm interview shows the same transition before the April petrodollar cluster. Asked about Greenland, Canada, and Mexico, Jiang says the old empire can retreat from distant theaters while becoming more aggressive nearby: [resources, labor, supply chains, Canada, Mexico, and Greenland become the near-abroad substitute]{evidence="video:interview-sutm8lojrqw@transcript:v1#seg-0055 video:interview-sutm8lojrqw@transcript:v1#seg-0056 video:interview-sutm8lojrqw@transcript:v1#seg-0058"}. He then generalizes the form. America has no peer competitor in its own region, so retreat abroad does not mean abdication; it means backing Israel, Germany, and Japan as regional clients while asking where bases, technology, and military infrastructure go when the center pulls back. In his compressed phrase, America will try to preserve hegemony by [supporting regional proxies and pretending to be the peacemaker inside conflicts it keeps alive]{evidence="video:interview-sutm8lojrqw@transcript:v1#seg-0064 video:interview-sutm8lojrqw@transcript:v1#seg-0065"}.

The April 9 strategy interview adds the later replacement map. If America loses the Middle East and GCC petrodollar recycling, Jiang says it must [move from finance empire to resource empire]{evidence="video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0068 video:interview-rsld81qme04@transcript:v1#seg-0069"}: retreat into the Western Hemisphere, then sell oil, rare earths, agriculture, and weapons to East Asia. The April 13 Dugin interview gives the same mechanism with a larger geography. Greater North America would shift America [from financial empire to resource empire]{evidence="video:interview-n44of1y7zgo@transcript:v1#seg-0064"} by turning Greenland, Canada, Mexico, Venezuela, Colombia, Cuba, Nicaragua, Honduras, oil, minerals, food, shipping routes, and two-ocean safety into a continental fortress.

The April 16 Capital CosM interview makes the boundary with Chokepoint sharper. Jiang says the Trump strategy treats [the Western Hemisphere as American territory, including South American lithium and Venezuelan oil]{evidence="video:interview-t8jfdx3gfcw@transcript:v1#seg-0013"}, while the Navy's old free-trade function becomes a paid gate: [control over the seas can be monetized instead of protected for free]{evidence="video:interview-t8jfdx3gfcw@transcript:v1#seg-0014"}. This is not isolationism. It is anti-globalist empire: [America is recentered from finance toward resources and manufacturing]{evidence="video:interview-t8jfdx3gfcw@transcript:v1#seg-0020"}, then war scarcity makes Venezuelan oil and North American production profitable because the world needs energy while the world burns. Jiang's summary is the resource-fortress form: [America becomes the resource and manufacturing hub while wars it started keep the outside world dependent]{evidence="video:interview-t8jfdx3gfcw@transcript:v1#seg-0021"}.

That makes the Iran war a test of imperial form rather than only a regional war. In the April 7 PBD interview, Jiang defines loss as being forced out of the Middle East and losing the GCC petrodollar machine; the result is not only prestige failure but [a dollar-order and domestic-order crisis]{evidence="video:interview-wio-7-gios@transcript:v1#seg-0026 video:interview-wio-7-gios@transcript:v1#seg-0027"}. His proposed exit is not liberal international repair. It is a retreat into [a self-sufficient Western Hemisphere]{evidence="video:interview-wio-7-gios@transcript:v1#seg-0039"} where the United States pays less for sea lanes, European defense, and the reserve-currency burden.

Resource empire appears when a weakening finance empire can no longer rely on dollar legitimacy and consent, so it turns crisis into dependence: material-stack shocks push buyers toward North American energy, fertilizer, resources, manufacturing, near-abroad labor, proxy clients, chokepoints, paid sea control, and weapons supply as the substitute for open global circulation.

## Replacement Empire Audition

The March 2026 Pax Judaica lecture adds a succession test. Jiang's claim is dated and speculative: [he says the lecture is intellectual speculation, not prophecy]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0058"}. But the mechanism is useful beyond that forecast. When an incumbent empire can no longer supply reliable muscle for the world-system, the question becomes whether another actor can audition as the replacement.

Jiang states the world-system stack in blunt terms: [empire is the muscle, finance sets the game, the dollar economy runs the game, and multilateral organizations and culture hide coercion]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0023 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0024"}. If the muscle fails, the beneficiaries of the system do not necessarily want revolution. They want continuity. Jiang therefore says that if the American empire loses, [the edifice needs another empire to replace the American muscle]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0025 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0026"}.

The material test is not moral worth. It is audition criteria. Jiang lists the incumbent's limits in the Iran war: [weak political will, insufficient manufacturing capacity, and unwillingness to sustain casualties]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0033 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0034"}. A would-be replacement has to prove the reverse: unity, capacity, determination, low-cost effectiveness, and willingness to do what the incumbent no longer can. His venture-capital analogy makes the ugly logic visible: the global elite backs the desperate actor that can prove [unity, capacity, and determination]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0035 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0037"}.

The late-processed **2025-12-19** Hinkle interview shows the same audition as a trap rather than only an abstract succession model. Jiang says Israel cannot defeat Iran directly, so the desired sequence is [a war between the United States and Iran]{evidence="video:interview-hzix1hy8hr0@transcript:v1#seg-0029"} that a Hormuz crisis can make look like defense of global trade. The military succession comes after failure: if America is defeated, retreats, and loses morale, [CENTCOM and other American military assets transfer to Israel]{evidence="video:interview-hzix1hy8hr0@transcript:v1#seg-0027"}, immediately creating the Pax Judaica scenario in his dated forecast. Iran's side of the trap supplies the material audit. Jiang says Iran has prepared for this confrontation for about twenty years and that [the only way to defeat America is to drag America into Iran]{evidence="video:interview-hzix1hy8hr0@transcript:v1#seg-0035 video:interview-hzix1hy8hr0@transcript:v1#seg-0036"}, where asymmetric warfare, supply-line disruption, morale, manufacturing capacity, and personnel depth answer the shock-and-awe story.

Replacement empire audition occurs when a failing imperial muscle is judged against a possible successor by political will, manufacturing capacity, casualty tolerance, unity, determination, cost effectiveness, infrastructure control, and whether another actor can inherit the old empire's bases, logistics, and command shell after defeat.

That is why Gaza, the Lebanon pager attack, and the later infrastructure map appear in the same source. Jiang is not only describing force. He is describing proof of concept. In his reading, Gaza shows willingness and unity; the pager attack shows cheap psychological reach; Pax Judaica is then drawn as [oil, gas, trade routes, data centers, human capital, AI surveillance, and corridor control]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0042 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0043 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0050 video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0051"}. The replacement is not merely a flag over territory. It has to operate the gates, resources, data centers, coercive tools, and psychological instruments of the next order.

The forecast caveat belongs inside the mechanism. Jiang says the two-pole Israel-Iran order is [theory, not prophecy]{evidence="video:predictive-history-mk4vchtawso@transcript:v1#seg-0058"}, and that warning keeps replacement-empire analysis from becoming an oracle. Use it as a material diagnostic: which actor is being evaluated as the next muscle, what proof is being offered, what infrastructure would make the replacement real, and what would falsify the audition?

## Vassals As Proxy Attrition

The undated Russia Today interview gives the same page a smaller but reusable imperial tactic. Asked whether Trump's less liberal security language might mean less intervention, Jiang says no. America is still intent on maintaining empire, but the method changes when the old moral cover stops working. The empire moves from liberal hypocrisy to [standard divide and rule]{evidence="video:interview-qsxkdk4mzgk@transcript:v1#seg-0023"}.

The mechanism is material before it is rhetorical. Jiang's examples are not only diplomatic labels. Rather than confront China directly in Southeast Asia, America can let Japan engage China; rather than confront Russia directly, it can tell Europe to fight Russia; in Africa, it can challenge Chinese economic dominance through allied resources. In his blunt formulation, [Japan's resources and wealth are treated as belonging to America]{evidence="video:interview-qsxkdk4mzgk@transcript:v1#seg-0023"} because Japan is read as a vassal state.

The **2025-11-06** Duran interview gives this section an earlier Peloponnesian version. Jiang reads Athens as fighting its allies as much as Sparta because the center keeps drawing allied resources into pointless wars. The analogy lets him name the Atlantic reversal before the later Canadian Prepper formulation: Washington can make Europe buy expensive gas, accept tariffs, and enter an unwinnable Ukraine war until Europe asks whether [Russia is the enemy or America is the enemy]{evidence="video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0029 video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0030"}. The phrase [fight Russia to last European]{evidence="video:interview-jlejlzmum4g@transcript:v1#seg-0019"} belongs here because the active mechanism is not only propaganda or bad diplomacy. It is the material use of an allied body as the expendable layer before the patron has to pay the same cost.

The **2025-12-09** Canadian Prepper interview gives the vassal mechanism a European decline form. Jiang starts from Athens: an empire in decline finances war by behaving like a mafia state toward allies, increasing taxes and tribute until the allies revolt. He then maps the pattern onto Europe. The American empire is heavily indebted and wants Europeans to keep financing it through higher LNG prices, higher defense spending, the social cost of refugees from American wars, and finally European bodies in Ukraine. In his compressed line, [America is cannibalizing the youth, wealth, and future of Europe]{evidence="video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0038 video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0039 video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0040"}.

The war-board piece is why the passage stays inside strategy. Europe does not merely pay tribute; it is moved toward the battlefield. Jiang says unpopular, co-opted European leadership will [sleepwalk into war in Ukraine through mission creep, volunteers, special forces, and eventually a draft]{evidence="video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0042 video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0044"}, while Russia can let the trap work because European volunteers enter terrain already shaped by drones, artillery, infantry motivation, and reconnaissance. In that setting, [the dependent ally becomes the exposed body before the patron has to invade or be invaded]{evidence="video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0045 video:interview-lhssc-w0bk4@transcript:v1#seg-0047"}.

The late-processed **2025-10-30** Cyrus Janssen interview now belongs before that Canadian Prepper formulation as the same European attrition mechanism in a rawer live-interview register. Jiang says the empire is in danger because America has [$37 trillion in debt and can no longer finance these wars]{evidence="video:interview-xrk5vsezj4y@transcript:v1#seg-0019"}, so it wants [NATO to fight these wars on its behalf]{evidence="video:interview-xrk5vsezj4y@transcript:v1#seg-0019"}. The phrase "mafia empire" is not only moral color. It names the extraction sequence: allies are pushed to pay more, buy expensive American weapons, and eventually [draft troops and send them off to Ukraine to die]{evidence="video:interview-xrk5vsezj4y@transcript:v1#seg-0020"}. The material test is whether the proxy layer can absorb that escalation without breaking into European dissent.

The **2025-12-19** Glenn Diesen interview makes the same mechanism sharper because the target is named directly: [the long-term goal is to strangle China]{evidence="video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0039"}. The method is not a single battle plan. It is resource denial and alliance extraction at once: South American copper, lithium, food, and oil are put under naval pressure; African relations become a competitive resource board; and Washington says it will use [the full resources of its allies]{evidence="video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0040"} to counter Beijing. The proxy layer then becomes bodily. Glenn distinguishes deterrence from hot war by saying a stabilizing patron would put its own army on the line, while a patron seeking conflict wants a proxy; Jiang answers with the brutal formula that [Japan and South Korea become cannon fodder]{evidence="video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0042 video:interview-p5gg9xxbqze@transcript:v1#seg-0044"} because troops, captured elites, and alliance dependence make them spendable.

Vassal proxy attrition appears when an empire avoids direct confrontation by making dependent allies spend bodies, resources, territory, wealth, or regional legitimacy against the rival, including the harsher anti-China form where allied resources are pooled to strangle a rival's trade and the European decline form where debt, tribute, weapons purchases, and draft pressure push allies toward being the first exposed bodies in a war trap.

The **2025-11-15** Danny Haiphong interview names a neighboring Ukraine mechanism that should not be flattened into ordinary propaganda failure. Jiang says Ukraine is already lost in military terms, but NATO and Europe cannot admit defeat because defeat threatens a political earthquake: they froze Russian assets, promised victory and reparations, and spent years telling their publics that Ukraine would march to Moscow. The war then becomes financial machinery. In Jiang's analogy, the Bank of England kept funding wars against Napoleon because admitting defeat would wipe out the debt structure; Europe has likewise invested in Ukraine as [a Ponzi scheme that collapses if defeat is admitted]{evidence="video:interview-vvj4mxqu2qk@transcript:v1#seg-0104 video:interview-vvj4mxqu2qk@transcript:v1#seg-0106"}.

The **2025-10-30** Janssen interview gives this debt-war anchor an earlier casino form. Jiang says Ukraine should cut its losses because the war is lost, but Europe has [stuck billions of dollars in Ukraine]{evidence="video:interview-xrk5vsezj4y@transcript:v1#seg-0032"}, been promised rare-earth wealth, and would face [a complete loss of face if NATO surrendered]{evidence="video:interview-xrk5vsezj4y@transcript:v1#seg-0032"}. The result is not rational battlefield revision. It is war continuation because sunk cost, prestige, and fantasy collateral make admission of loss harder than another round of attrition.

<span id="strategy-debt-war-cannot-admit-defeat" class="lens-point-anchor"></span>
Debt-financed war cannot admit defeat when assets, reparations promises, sunk costs, fantasy collateral, elite survival, and public victory stories are leveraged into the battlefield so heavily that loss would collapse the financing structure or face-saving story and push the war's backers toward escalation.

The Peloponnesian analogy makes the cost visible. Jiang says Athens used vassals as [cannon fodder and proxies against Sparta]{evidence="video:interview-qsxkdk4mzgk@transcript:v1#seg-0024"}, producing a massive allied rebellion. That is why this belongs here rather than only on [Game Theory](/lens/game-theory/). Game Theory owns the player-positioning question: different players, under different circumstances, choose different strategies. This page owns the material audit after the positioning is named: whose bodies, ports, factories, debt capacity, resource base, and domestic legitimacy are being consumed, and whether the proxy layer can absorb the cost without turning against the patron.

## Overextension As Enemy Strategy

The Putin strategic-imagination lecture gives the same mechanism from the enemy's side. An empire does not have to be defeated head-on if it can be made to perform its own weakness. Jiang defines imperial death as the convergence of [overextension, debt, and civil discord]{evidence="video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0001 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0004 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0006"}. Overextension comes from hubris: blindness to one's own limits, the opponent's strategy, and the larger geopolitical board.

Here strategy is not only moving armies. It is making the empire waste attention. Ukraine becomes [a black hole for NATO because money, weapons, manpower, alliance trust, and domestic legitimacy keep draining out]{evidence="video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0010 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0013 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0014"}. A second war in the Middle East, North Korean pressure in East Asia, BRICS expansion, and China remaining neutral all matter because they [force America to divide focus while protecting the dollar system and military posture at once]{evidence="video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0018 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0019 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0020"}.

<!-- lens-point id="overextension-turns-empire-against-itself" concept="strategy-material-test" evidence="video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0001 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0013 video:predictive-history-b-al2wgk49y@transcript:v1#seg-0014" -->
<span id="overextension-turns-empire-against-itself" class="lens-point-anchor"></span>
Overextension turns empire against itself when an opponent does not need direct victory; it only needs to multiply wars, costs, alliance frictions, debt pressure, and domestic dissent until imperial power becomes self-draining.

This gives the page its diagnostic edge. Do not ask only whether an actor is stronger. Ask whether the actor is being made to spend strength in the wrong shape.

The **2026-05-21** Putin-in-Beijing lecture returns to this overextension mechanism from the sanctioned-state side. Jiang says Putin's strategy after forced-dollar-withdrawal is to find states sanctioned by America, form relationships with them, and [create volatility that destabilizes the global order and the U.S. dollar]{evidence="video:predictive-history-x83hclwvhi8@transcript:v1#seg-0031"}. The coalition is not described as a harmonious replacement civilization. It is a pressure system: Russia, Iran, North Korea, Belarus, and other dissatisfied states do not need to govern the world together; they need to make the American policing role more expensive than the monetary privilege can justify.

North Korea gives the mechanism its cleanest theater. Jiang says the June 2024 Russia-North Korea mutual defense pact matters because Pyongyang can add pressure without matching South Korea's wealth. In his rough war grammar, poor societies can still be dangerous when their soldiers fight to the end and the society retains [energy, openness, and cohesion]{evidence="video:predictive-history-x83hclwvhi8@transcript:v1#seg-0034 video:predictive-history-x83hclwvhi8@transcript:v1#seg-0035"}. The strategic result is not victory in a neat Korean war story. It is friction: artillery near Seoul, Japan pulled into danger, American attention split, and another edge of the dollar-policing system made unstable.

<span id="strategy-sanctioned-states-create-volatility" class="lens-point-anchor"></span>
Sanctioned states become a volatility machine when an anti-imperial strategist links them into low-cost theaters of pressure, making the policing empire divide attention, finance allies, defend debt demand, and spend military credibility across boards it cannot fully absorb.

This belongs inside strategy rather than [Power As Alchemy](/lens/power-as-alchemy/) because the active question is no longer how the dollar becomes lived reality. It is how an enemy tests the enforcement surface of that reality: Korea, Iran, Europe, Japan, Treasury buyers, trade routes, and alliance credibility. It also borders [Game Theory](/lens/game-theory/) because Jiang is naming the players and incentives, but this page owns the material audit of the coalition: which sanctioned actor can generate pressure, what the empire must spend to answer, and where friction becomes too expensive to police.

## Diagnostics

Ask these questions when applying this lens.

**What cost is the center trying to stop paying directly?** Look for sea-lane protection, allied defense, resource access, debt credibility, ground troops, industrial replenishment, or public consent moving away from the imperial center and onto a proxy layer.

**Which material substitute replaces dollar belief?** When monetary legitimacy weakens, ask whether the proposed substitute is continental resources, naval tolls, weapons sales, energy leverage, bases, sanctions, blockades, or near-abroad control.

**Is retreat being sold as withdrawal while becoming extraction?** Jiang's resource-fortress mechanism is not simple isolationism. It appears when the center drops parts of global liberal management but keeps monetizing seas, nearby resources, allied conflict, weapons supply, oil scarcity, and manufacturing dependency.

**Who is being auditioned as replacement muscle?** Identify the actor being judged for political will, manufacturing capacity, casualty tolerance, unity, cost effectiveness, infrastructure control, and ability to inherit command shells or coercive infrastructure.

**Which ally is being spent?** If the empire says it is merely coordinating, ask whether a vassal is being made to supply bodies, wealth, territory, ports, legitimacy, weapons purchases, or regional risk.

**What would admitting defeat collapse?** Debt-financed wars continue when assets, reparations, sunk costs, collateral fantasies, elite survival, and public victory stories make loss more dangerous to the backers than another round of attrition.

**Can the enemy create volatility cheaply?** A sanctioned state does not need to govern the whole system. It may only need to open another pressure point that makes the policing empire divide attention, finance allies, defend dollar demand, and spend credibility across too many boards.
